# Genesis 1 (gen001), Jamaican Creole English: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 1 (31 verses, Hebrew and BSB numbering agree) in Jamaican Creole
English, translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen001.txt`, with the clause packages) with the BSB as the only
assisting translation. The spelling, word forms and constructions come from Di Jamiekan Nyuu Testiment (looked up
with frequency counts), the verse pack, the wiki and the store. Status: draft, not reviewed. Source of the text:
`gen001.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** This is the project's first chapter: no earlier draft exists, so the wiki's rules (bare verb for the
narrative past, *did* for states and backgrounds, *Mek* + subject + verb for jussives, *se … se* speech frames,
phonemic spelling) and the corpus's creation passages (JHN 1:1–3, ACT 4:24, REV 14:7, JAS 3:7, 2PE 3:5) are the
model. The chapter fixes the refrains every later creation and genealogy chapter will reuse (see the store entries
by `agent:gen001`).

## Exegetical observations

- **1:1–2.** 1:1 is read as a main clause (BSB), with *Wen taim did staat* from JHN 1:1, the NT's own echo of the
  verse. 1:2 is background (S-V order, qatal and nominal clauses): *did* marks it (*did kova*, *did a flai*), while the
  wayyiqtol main line from 1:3 on has bare verbs (*se*, *si*, *plit*, *kaal*, *mek*).
- **The expanse.** רָקִיעַ is something beaten out and spread; *wan big ruuf* keeps the picture of a vault without the
  English loan *firmament*. 1:8 names it *Skai*, so 1:14–20 say *di skai ruuf* (the expanse of the sky).
- **The jussives** (יְהִי, תַּדְשֵׁא, יִשְׁרְצוּ, תּוֹצֵא) are all *Mek* + subject + verb; the weqatal continuations (1:14b, 1:15)
  are *Dem ago bi* / *Mek dem shain*.
- **Day 6.** The plural *נַעֲשֶׂה* "let us" is kept (*Mek wi mek*) without comment, as the BSB keeps it. אָדָם is collective
  and 1:26b already says "let *them* rule", so *piipl* and *dem* run through 1:26–28; 1:27's singular אֹתוֹ is folded
  into *dem*. *man an uman* is the corpus's pair for male and female.
- **1:29–30.** The gift of food is a speech; *Luk ya* for הִנֵּה (attested 22×), *mi gi* (bare verb for the perfect of
  a declared act). 1:30 has no verb in the Hebrew; *mi gi* is repeated from 1:29.

## Open questions

1. **Expanse:** *wan big ruuf* (proposal) or simply *skai*? The decision affects 1:6–8, 14–20 and later Psalms.
2. **אָדָם:** *piipl* in Genesis 1, or *man* / *uman biin* (not in the corpus)? From Genesis 2 the word becomes the
   name *Adam*.
3. **Be fruitful and multiply:** *Unu av nof nof pikni* fits people; for fish and birds (1:22) the team may prefer a
   word for breeding.
4. **"according to its kind"**: *difrant difrant kaina* (from 1CO 15:38) or a fixed phrase like *kaina bai kaina* (not
   attested)?
5. **Divine names** remain open (questions page): Genesis 1 has only אֱלֹהִים = *Gad*.
