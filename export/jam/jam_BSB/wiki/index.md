# Jamaican Creole English: wiki

What Di Jamiekan Nyuu Testiment (the style corpus) teaches about Jamaican Creole English as written,
for drafting the Old Testament from the Hebrew. Drafting agents read it before every chapter; the
language team reads it, corrects it and answers the [questions](questions.md). The facts the agents
work with (checked against the corpus, not yet by the team) are in the store (`../profile/observations.json`, `../profile/decisions.json`); each page shows its store
entries in a generated block at the end. The pages are in English; examples are short quotations from
the corpus with their references.

Set up on 2026-10-16 from the NT alone (no OT drafts yet), with the derived report's statistics
confirmed or rejected against the corpus.

## The five things a drafter must not get wrong

1. **No English tense.** The narrative past is the bare verb; *did* is for states and backgrounds;
   future is *ago*; progressive *a* ([verbs](grammar/verbs.md)).
2. **No English copula.** *a* between nouns, nothing before adjectives, *de* for place
   ([clauses](grammar/clauses.md)).
3. **The spelling is phonemic**: iina, uol, kindom, faada, ous; never English spelling
   ([orthography](orthography.md)).
4. **Speech is "X se tu Y se, “…”"**, and negation doubles (*neva av no pikni*)
   ([connectors](grammar/connectors.md), [clauses](grammar/clauses.md)).
5. **Plural *dem* after the noun, *unu* for plural you, *shi/ar* for women**
   ([nouns and pronouns](grammar/nouns-pronouns.md)).
