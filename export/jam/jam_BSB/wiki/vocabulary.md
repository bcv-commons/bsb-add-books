# Vocabulary

The term base: the corpus word for a concept, with its share in the alignment lexicon
(`_learn/lexicon_eng.tsv`) where a choice is at stake. Every word here is spelt as the corpus spells
it; the English-looking form is wrong (*kingdom* → kindom, *whole* → uol).

## Words that differ from English

## Creation words (Genesis 1–2)

evn (heaven) and ort (earth): *di evn an di ort* (2PE 3:5); skai (sky); lait, daak / daaknis; die,
nait, iivnin, maanin; sii, waata; grong (ground), dos (dust); graas, siid, fruut, chrii (tree); bod,
fish, animal, siniek; man an uman (MAT 19:4); **mek** for create and make (all 10 *created* in the
lexicon); ruul (rule over), bles, kos (curse), gud, gyaadn, niekid, shiem, res (rest).

## God and worship

Gad, di Laad, Gad Spirit, uoli, ienjel, priis, prafit, alta, sakrifais, worship, priez, bles, sin,
siev, frii (redeem), joj, kindom, king. The divine names and the words with no settled rendering
(covenant, חֶסֶד, glory) are on [the project's decisions](decisions.md).

## Family and society

faada, mada, breda, sista, waif, ozban, daata / gyal pikni, fos pikni (firstborn), niem, fambili
(household), chrienja (foreigner), konchri (land, nation), siti / toun, giet, ous / yaad, chraib.

## Animals and goods

shiip, biebi shiip (lamb), guot, kou (cattle), kyamil, dangki, silva, guol, wel (well), flod, rienbuo,
jriim (dream).
