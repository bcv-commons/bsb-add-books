# Questions for the language team

What the NT corpus could not settle. Answers go into the store as new entries (with `supersedes`
where they change one); a quick answer can be written on this page as
`> **Correction (name, date):** …`.

1. **How close to the Hebrew?** The Jamaican NT is meaning-based: it explains customs, converts
   measures and turns abstract nouns into clauses ([style](style.md)). Should the OT follow the same
   style, or keep closer to the Hebrew wording?
2. **The LORD (YHWH):** the proposal is *di Laad*, as in the NT's quotations (MAT 4:7). Does the team
   want a different form for the divine name (e.g. a name, or *LAAD* in capitals)? And *di Laad Gad*
   for YHWH Elohim (GEN 2–3)?
3. **El Shaddai, LORD of Hosts:** the NT uses relative clauses ("di Laad we av aal a di powa", ROM
   9:29; "di Laad we ruul uova evriting", JAS 5:4). Which one should be fixed for the OT?
4. **Covenant (בְּרִית):** *pramis* (35%), *plan* (30%) or *diil* (11%)? *pramis* is also "promise"
   and "oath"; Genesis 9, 15 and 17 make the covenant central.
5. **Gad, God and the tribe:** the corpus writes God and the son of Jacob the same (REV 7:5). Should
   Gad the son (GEN 30:11, 49:19) be spelt differently?
6. **Names the NT lacks:** built by rule from the English pronunciation (Iebram, Sierai, Ishmiel,
   Liiya, Lieban, Iidn, Am, Jiefet, Biebl): are these the forms the team wants? Should they follow
   English pronunciation as the NT does, or the Hebrew?
7. **Measures:** REV 21:17 converts cubits to feet. Convert the ark's measures (GEN 6:15), or keep
   cubits with an explanation?
8. **חֶסֶד (loving devotion):** *kain* / "gud an kain tu" in the NT. Is there a fixed phrase for God's
   faithful love (Psalms, Genesis 24:27, 32:10)?
9. **Glory:** no single word (*brait lait*, *priez*, *rispek*). What should the OT use for God's glory?
10. **Genesis 1 refrains:** "evening and morning, the first day", "and it was so", "according to its
    kind": the corpus has no model. Proposals are on [formulas](formulas.md) and [gaps](gaps.md).
11. **Contractions** (fi'im, fu'unu, na'av, dwi'it): they appear in some NT books only. Should drafts
    use them or the full forms?
12. **The book title** of Genesis and the chapter word: the corpus has no OT book; project.yaml has
    `chapter_word: Chapter` from the edition's headings. What should Genesis be called?
13. **"unu" vs "yu":** God addressing Adam and Eve together, or a crowd: the drafts will use *unu* for
    every plural "you". Is that right in solemn speech (blessings, laws)?
