# Style: how the corpus translates

Di Jamiekan Nyuu Testiment is a meaning-based translation in everyday speech, not a word-for-word one.
A draft that follows the BSB's sentence shapes falls back on English. What the corpus does:

- **Abstract nouns become clauses.** Faith = *chos iina Gad* or *biliiv* (HEB 11); salvation = *siev*;
  righteousness = *du wa rait*; the image of God = "Jiizas a siem laik im Faada" (COL 1:15).
- **Implicit information is spelled out.** "Wen taim did staat" for "in the beginning" (JHN 1:1);
  "Gad … mek" for "creation"; the birthright explained (HEB 12:16).
- **Customs and measures are explained or converted**: cubits to feet (REV 21:17), the tithe as one
  of every ten (HEB 7:4).
- **Long Greek sentences are cut** into short ones; a Hebrew verse may become two or three sentences.
- **Lively speech**: doubled adjectives (big big), *nof nof*, interjections ("Shhh"), *Matarafak*.
- **Kinship and address** are Jamaican: *Dadi* in the parable (LUK 15:12), *breda an sista* for
  "brothers" addressed to a church.

This page records what the corpus does; how far the OT drafts follow it (meaning-based vs closer to the
Hebrew) is a question for the team ([questions](questions.md), 1).
