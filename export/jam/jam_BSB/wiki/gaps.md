# Gaps: what the NT has no model for

Concepts the Old Testament needs and the Jamaican NT does not have, or has only as a paraphrase.
Each gap names the first verse that needs it (in `source`) and the closest models. No drafts exist
yet, so every solution here is a proposal; the drafts' notes should say what they chose, and the next
agent records it here.

The corpus's own way with missing concepts is to **explain**: the birthright is "everything Esau
would get as his father's first son" (HEB 12:16), the tithe is "wan outa evri ten" (HEB 7:4), cubits
become feet (REV 21:17). Follow that style rather than coining nouns.

## Genesis 1–3

Genesis 1 is drafted (2026-10-16); its choices replace the first proposals here (the old gap entries are
superseded, the new ones are open decisions for the team):

- **expanse** (GEN 1:6): *wan big ruuf*, then *di skai ruuf* (*ruuf* 7× in the corpus); footnote *wan big kova*.
- **formless and void** (GEN 1:2): *neva av no shiep, an it did emti*.
- **according to their kinds**: *difrant difrant kaina* (1CO 15:38).
- **be fruitful and multiply**: *Unu av nof nof pikni* (for the animals in 1:22 too; birds *ton nof nof*).
- **creeping things / livestock / wild animals**: *animal we kraal pan di grong* / *animal we piipl kip* / *wail
  animal*, the classes of JAS 3:7.
- **great sea creatures**: *big big animal iina di sii*.
- **image and likeness**: *fi luk laik wi, fi bi laik wi*.
- **bring forth** (of the earth): no model; *Mek di ort bier …* for plants, *Mek … kom out a di ort* for animals.
- Still open from Genesis 2–3: garden of Eden, the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, cherubim.

## Later in Genesis

ark (*aak*, LUK 17:27, or *big buot*, HEB 11:7), cubits, birthright, tithe, concubine, tent,
sojourning (*liv laik chrienja*, ACT 7:6), flocks and herds, the peoples of Canaan (Hittites,
Hivites, Perizzites).

## The derived report's list

Frequent OT words the NT hardly has: weight, blue, pomegranates, wheels, basin, cart, twisted,
fortified, Hittites, Hivites, Cushite, Perizzites, enlarge; none is needed before Exodus.
