# Nouns, pronouns, possession, numbers

## Articles

**di** (the, 11,136×), **wan** (a, also "one"). Bare nouns for generic and mass reference. Names and
*Gad* take no article; *di Laad* does. (Derived report: Greek ὁ = di 45%, nothing 43%.)

## Plural: dem after the noun

"di sina dem" (LUK 15:1), "im falara dem"; *di N dem* 2,342×. One *dem* covers a coordinated phrase:
"fren an nieba dem" (LUK 15:6). After a numeral it is usually left out: "tuu bwai pikni" (GAL 4:22),
though "twelv bwai pikini dem" (ACT 7:8) has it. Written apart, never *-dem*, never English *-s*.

## This and that

| | form | example |
|---|---|---|
| this | **da N ya**, dis N | "da tuori ya" (LUK 15:3), "da plies ya" (JHN 14:31) |
| that | **da N de** | "da piis a lan de" (ACT 7:16) |
| those | **dem N de** | "dem sitn de" (HEB 10:6) |
| here / there | ya-so / de-so | |

## Pronouns

| | sg | pl |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | mi | wi |
| 2 | yu | **unu** |
| 3 | im (he, him, his, it), **shi / ar** (she / her), it | dem |

One form for subject, object and possessor. Women are *shi/ar*: in verses where the BSB has only
she/her, shi 161, ar 145, im 32 (MRK 5:29 "shi fiil iina ar badi"). *ar* also means "or". **unu**
is strictly plural: decide singular *yu* or plural *unu* from the Hebrew, not from English "you".
Reflexive: *imself*, *unuself*.

## Possession

The possessor comes first, with nothing between: "Lat waif" (LUK 17:32), "Gad kindom" (35× for the
kingdom of God). Emphatic or predicative: **fi** + possessor, written apart: "fi mi Laad" (MAT 22:44),
"a fi dem". A few fixed phrases link with *a*: "Spirit a di Laad" (LUK 4:18), "di King a Iijip" (ACT
7:10). Contracted *fi'im* (67×) and *fu'unu* (54×) occur in some books; the full forms are safer.

## Doubling

A doubled word intensifies: "big big shiit" (ACT 10:11), "nof nof" (1CO 2:6); makes a distributive:
"difrant difrant siid" (1CO 15:38); or an iterative: "kot op kot op" (ACT 19:16). Fixed doubles:
**faada faada** (forefathers, 61×; 2PE 3:4), *pikni pikni* (grandchildren). Hebrew "very" can be a
doubled adjective (*gud gud*) or *riili*.

## Comparison

*beta*, *biga*, *iiziya* + **dan** ("iiziya dan", MAT 10:15); otherwise *muo* + adjective + *dan*.
Superlative *di muos* ("Di muos impuotant", MRK 12:29) or *-is* in a few words (liklis). The
derived report counted dan ×59, muo ×29, biga ×20, beta ×14 for Greek comparatives.

## Numbers

Written as words: wan, tuu, chrii, fuo, faiv, siks, sevn, iet, nain, ten, twelv, onjrid, touzn;
"nainti-nain" (LUK 15:4), "fuo onjrid ier" (ACT 7:6), "twelv touzn" (REV 7:5). Digits are rare (14
tokens). Ordinals: fos, sekan; "die sevn" (HEB 4:4, number after the noun) beside "di sevn die".
Genesis 5 and 11 ages: write the numbers in words.
