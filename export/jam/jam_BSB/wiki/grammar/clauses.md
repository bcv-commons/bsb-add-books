# Clauses: order, copula, focus, negation, questions

## Word order

SVO, with no case on nouns or pronouns (derived report: VO 63%, OV 27%; the OV share comes from
relative clauses, focus fronting and alignment noise). A plain object follows the verb.

## Being: a, de, nothing

| meaning | form | example |
|---|---|---|
| X is a Y (equative) | **a** | "im a wan" (MRK 12:29), "Evn a mi chuon" (ACT 7:49) |
| X is (adjective) | **no copula** | "yu so glad" (LUK 15:5); *im glad* 70×, *im a glad* 0× |
| X is somewhere | **de** | "de pan mi" (LUK 4:18) |
| was (equative) | **a did / did a** | "Aizak a did Iebriyam pikni" (LUK 3:34) |
| became | **ton**, **komiin** | "ton wan griet man" (LUK 1:32) |

Never write English *is/was/are* or *iz*: the corpus has neither.

## There is / there was

A new participant: "Nou yu did av wan man …" (ACT 5:1, ACT 8:9; *yu did av* 35×). There is not:
**no de** ("Notn no de we im neehn mek", JHN 1:3).

## Focus with a

A focused phrase is fronted with **A**: "a im mek evriting" (1CO 8:6), "A outa Iijip mi kaal mi
bwai pikni" (MAT 2:15, the object fronted). Negative focus "A no …" (1CO 1:2). Questions do the
same: "A uu ded pan di kraas fi unu?" (1CO 1:13). Use it for the Hebrew fronted subject or object
and for contrast.

## Negation

Before the verb:
- **no** (general, present): no 3,487× (also as determiner, "no pikni");
- **neva** (past: did not) 505×, **neehn** (past, have not) 235×: "im neehn ded at aal" (HEB 11:5);
- **naa** (progressive and future: naa go) 597×;
- **kyaahn** (cannot), **kudn**, **wudn**; **na'av** (has not, JHN 2:3).

**Negative concord** is the rule: "dem neva av no pikni" (LUK 1:7), "neva yuuz no fansi taak" (1CO
2:4), "Notn no de" (JHN 1:3): about 975 verses. The priors' *duon*, *kyaan*, *noh*, *nonaa* do not
occur.

## Relative clauses: we

After the noun, **we** for subject and object alike, no resumptive pronoun: "wan sina we ton tu Gad"
(LUK 15:7), "Di Gad we mek di worl" (ACT 17:24). *dem we* (those who) 1,353×. The OT's "the LORD
your God" is a relative clause: "di Laad we a yu Gad" (MAT 4:7). Whose = *uufa*. *uu* is the
question word, not a relative.

## That: se

*se* introduces what is said, known, thought or seen: *nuo se* 313×, *tingk se* 152×, "biliiv se"
(HEB 11:20). A causal כִּי is **kaaz**, not *se*.

## Questions

wa (what), uu (who), uufa (whose), ou (how), **wich paat** (where, 141×), wen (when), **wa mek**
(why, 49×; *wai* only 6×), wa kaina (what kind). Yes/no questions keep statement order. Tags:
*iihn?* (36×), *duohn?* (2PE 3:4).
