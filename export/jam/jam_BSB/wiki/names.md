# Names

## How the corpus spells names

Names are spelt as a Jamaican speaker pronounces the **English** form, in the spelling of the
[orthography](orthography.md) page: not from the Greek or Hebrew. The derived report's Greek → target
letter pairs (e → ii, a → ie, o → uo) confirm it: they are English vowel sounds.

| English | rule | examples |
|---|---|---|
| stressed long a | ie | Iebl (Abel), Kien (Cain), Siera (Sarah), Iebriyam |
| long e | ii | Iiv (Eve), Iinok (Enoch), Iisaa (Esau) |
| long o | uo | Muoziz (Moses), Juozif (Joseph) |
| long i | ai | Aizak (Isaac), Sainai, Liivai (Levi) |
| au, aw | aa | Paal (Paul) |
| initial h | dropped | Iegar (Hagar), Iemuor (Hamor), Ieran (Haran) |
| h between vowels | y / w | Iebriyam (Abraham), Nowa (Noah), Nieyar (Nahor) |
| final -ah | -a | Juuda, Siera, Tera, Metyuuzila |
| ph, ch (k), th | f, k, t | Fiera (Pharaoh), Melkizedek, Set (Seth) |
| -s / -ses (voiced) | z | Muoziz, Jiemz |

Genesis names the NT has are in the store as attested (Adam, Iiv, Kien, Iebl, Set, Iinos, Iinok,
Lamek, Metyuuzila, Nowa, Shem, Tera, Iebriyam, Siera, Lat, Aizak, Ribeka, Jiekob, Iisaa, Riechel,
Juozif and the twelve sons, Iijip, Fiera, Kienan, Sadam, Gomaro, Melkizedek, Sielem, Shekem, Ieran).

## Built by rule (open)

Names the NT lacks were built by the table and are `by-rule`, status open, for the team: Iebram
(Abram), Sierai (Sarai), Ishmiel, Liiya (Leah), Lieban (Laban), Iidn (Eden), Am (Ham), Jiefet
(Japheth), Biebl (Babel), Nimrad, Iifriyim (Ephraim), Daina (Dinah), Or (Ur), Betel (Bethel).

## Homographs to watch

- **Gad** is God and also the tribe and son of Jacob (REV 7:5 "fram Gad chraib"). Genesis 30:11 and
  49:19 need a decision (see [questions](questions.md)).
- **Kienan** is Canaan (ACT 7:11) and Cainan (LUK 3:36–37).
- **Ieran** is Haran (ACT 7:4) and Aaron (lexicon).
- **Am** (Ham, by rule) is also a common word in English-based speech; check with the team.
