# Orthography

The corpus writes Jamaican in the phonemic Cassidy–JLU spelling: one letter (or pair) per sound,
never English spelling. A word spelt as in English is almost always wrong ("kindom", not *kingdom*;
"uol", not *whole*). When unsure, search the form: `python3 scripts/concord.py jam/jam_BSB forms "^kya"`.

## Vowels

| sound (English example) | written | corpus examples |
|---|---|---|
| long a (father, law) | **aa** | faada, laad, aal, taak, staat |
| long e (see, sea) | **ii** | piipl, iina, siid, priis |
| long u (food, true) | **uu** | fuud, juu, nyuu, chuu |
| ay (face, name) | **ie** | fies, niem, siem, die, prie |
| o (go, show) | **uo** | nuo, shuo, uol, uova, bifuo |
| i (time) | **ai** | taim, laik, Krais |
| ou (house) | **ou** | ous, out, toun, roun |
| short o (God, Lot) | **a** | Gad, Lat, rang (wrong), lang (long) |
| short u (love, come) | **o** | lov, blod, kom, son (sun and son alike) |
| ur/er/ir (church, serve) | **o / or** | choch, sorv, worl, dizorv |
| final -er (father) | **a** | faada, mada, breda, afta, neva |
| -ear/-air (hear, care) | **ier** | ier, kier, shier, nier |

*ee* and *oo* do not occur, except *ee* before the nasal *hn* (neehn, gweehn).

## Consonants

- **No h** from English: ous, im, ier (hear/year/here), api, Ai (high). *h* occurs only in sh, ch,
  zh and in the nasal *-hn*.
- **No c, q, x, th**: k, kw, ks; th → t or d (ting, di, dat, Set for Seth).
- **Nasal vowels** are written **hn**: waahn (want), kyaahn (cannot), neehn, gweehn. *kyan* = can,
  *kyaahn* = cannot: the *hn* carries the negation.
- **Palatals**: *ky*, *gy* before *a* (kyan, kyari, gyal, gyaadn, Gyalalii), *ny* (nyam, nyuu). Not in
  every k/g + a: Gad, kaal, gaan have none.
- **tr/thr → chr, dr → jr**: chrii (three, tree), chrang, jriim, jringk; *chuu* = through and true.
- **Clusters** lose their last consonant: lan, fiil, fren, ges; voiced -s is *z* (kaaz, Muoziz, tingz).
- **Syllabic l, n** without a vowel: likl, onggl (only), gyaadn, mountn (also moutn ×7), iivn (even),
  evn (heaven); -ing is -in/-n (iivnin, maanin).
- Apostrophes only in a few contractions (fi'im, na'av, dwi'it); hyphens in compounds and doubles
  (nainti-nain, dege-dege, rait-an-said).

## The probe's slips

The write probe (`scripts/probe_language.py`) showed where the model spells by English or by another
language: the store's `slip:` entries list the corpus forms (iina, uol, kindom, choch, chrang, diiman,
pred, grombl, Iebriyam, Timoti). Some "never" forms there come from the model mixing in another
language (*glajnis*, *schrik*); they only mean the model was unsure of the word.
