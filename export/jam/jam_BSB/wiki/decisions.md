# The project's decisions

The project has no style guide or review overview yet, so nothing here is decided by the team.
The entries are **proposals** (`project-decision`, status open) built on how the NT renders the same
words in its OT quotations; drafting agents use them until the team answers the
[questions](questions.md).

## Divine names

| Hebrew | proposal | model |
|---|---|---|
| אֱלֹהִים (God) | **Gad** | 3,763×; lower case *gad* for false gods (faalz gad) |
| יְהוָה (the LORD) | **di Laad** | MAT 4:7, MRK 12:29, HEB 8:8: κύριος in quotations |
| יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים (the LORD God) | **di Laad Gad** | REV 1:8, ACT 3:20 (14×) |
| the LORD your God | **di Laad we a yu Gad** | MAT 4:7, MRK 12:30 (relative clause) |
| God Most High | **di Muos Ai Gad** | LUK 1:32, HEB 7:1 |
| El Shaddai, LORD of Hosts | **di Laad we av aal a di powa** | ROM 9:29; JAS 5:4 has "di Laad we ruul uova evriting" |
| the Spirit of God | **Gad Spirit** | 39× |

Small capitals cannot be shown; *di Laad* serves both for YHWH and for אֲדֹנָי addressed to God, as in
the NT. Where Hebrew has אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, propose *Laad Gad* (by rule).

## Words with no settled rendering

- **covenant** (בְּרִית): pramis 35%, plan 30%, diil 11%. *pramis* also means promise and oath; the
  covenant of Genesis 9, 15, 17 may need its own word.
- **חֶסֶד** (loving devotion): via ἔλεος the corpus has *kain* (51%), often "gud an kain tu" (MAT
  23:23, GAL 6:16).
- **glory**: brait 17%, priez 17%, big 16%, lait 11%: no single word. Shining glory = *brait lait*
  (ACT 7:2); honour = *priez*, *rispek*.

## Numbers and measures

Numbers are written in words (the corpus has 14 digit tokens). Measures: REV 21:17 converts cubits
into feet; whether the OT does the same is a question for the team.
