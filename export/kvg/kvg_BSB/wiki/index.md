# Kuni language wiki

What the Kuni NT teaches about Kuni, for drafting the OT. Agents and people read and edit these pages. The checked facts are in the store (`../profile/`); each page shows its store entries in a generated block at the end. Start with the page your verse needs; `inbox.md` lists store entries no page claims yet; `log.md` records changes.

The pages were first gathered from the Kuni NT alone while drafting Ruth 1–4 (14.10.2026), with
the human Ruth and Jonah held out as a test. Since then the human Ruth and Jonah (eBible 2015,
the only OT in Kuni) have joined the corpus, and the pages were checked against them: where the
human text differs, the store has a superseding entry and the page says what changed.

Start with:
- [OT narrative and dialogue](ot-narrative.md): how the human translators tell Hebrew
  narrative and dialogue, with the style figures;
- [Sources](sources.md): what each text, lexicon and dataset is and how far to trust it;
- the grammar pages, [names](names.md), [vocabulary](vocabulary.md) (by domain) and
  [gaps](gaps.md) (with the human solutions).

Questions for the Kuni team are marked "**Question for the Kuni team:**" on the pages (divine
name, kin -pakha, genealogy spelling, redeem, explanations in the text, Shaddai, gumbag).

Tone marks: the NT writes acute (á), grave (à), macron (ā), circumflex (â, questions) and the
rising mark (a᷄, prohibitives). `concord.py kvg` ignores them, but the draft should copy them
from the model verse. Headings (`==...==`) carry no tone marks (Luk 1 "==Meli Elizabet-te okha
gumat==", Luk 2 "==Yesu ge꞉qan=="). `꞉` is part of some prefixes (ge꞉, gi꞉, me꞉, gu꞉).
