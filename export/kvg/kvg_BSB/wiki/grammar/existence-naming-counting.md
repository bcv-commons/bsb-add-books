# Existence, naming, counting

- "mø̀ndøgoám" there was (Luk 1:5, 2:25, 15:11); "ndøgoám" was; "gegoam" he was (subordinate).
- Names: "Teqa iz Zekalayá." (Luk 1:5), "Tuqa iz, Melí." (Luk 1:27), "Ta vemiav-qa iz
  Betliém." (Luk 2:4), "Kawa sævam, tuqa iz Kandasi" (Act 8:27), "tiqa iziz" (their names).
  Naming: "iz Yesu mbo-akhaém" (Luk 2:21), "Teqa iz Zion ndǿgoát" (his name shall be John,
  Luk 1:60).
- Numbers: kopo 1, menas 2, misika 3, foa 4, faev 5, siks 6, seven 7, eit 8, nain 9, ten 10,
  tuelv 12 (Luk 1:56, 2:42, 15:8, Mrk 12:20); "viav seven" (seven years, Luk 2:36), "viav
  foti"; "ezoqa menas" (two people), "nakhei angana menás" (two sons, Act 7:29).
- Age: "No lawaqapé" (I am an old man, Luk 1:18), "Ndugu ngæpuká" (she is old, Luk 1:36,
  2:36).

## In Ruth and Jonah (human, 2015)

- **Naming**: "Ngenek ezoqam, teqa iz Elimelék. Neka zas-qa iz Naomí. Neka nakhei angana-qa
  iziz nqánek, Malon neka Kilion." (RUT 1:2); "Teqa iz Boáz." (2:1); "tiqa iziz nqazǿgo, Opa
  neka Lut" (their names were, 1:4). The name keeps a final acute (Elimelék, Naomí, Boáz).
  Naming a child: "iz Obed mbo-akhaém" (4:17). A name's meaning: "Taqa manqa mokho, ‘...’"
  (1:20).
- **Having**: "Naomi ekesu mø̀ndømbo-goám." (Naomi had a relative, 2:1); 'with' = -ús
  ("no anganeam-ús neka nakhei angana-ús", with husband and sons, 1:21); 'without' = mbaín
  ("Zivis mbaín-a, nakheis mbain.", 1:5).
- **Numbers**: misiká 3 (JON 2:1), foti 40 (JON 3:4), seven (RUT 4:15), ten (1:5, 4:2); large
  numbers as English words: "wan andled tuenti taosen ezoqam" (120,000 people, JON 4:11);
  measures with digits: "10 kilogrém" (RUT 2:17), "20 kilogrém" (3:15). 'About N' = "ande āv N
  ndægó" (1:5, 2:17), not ndi꞉gú as the first version had.

## In Genesis 1 (agent draft gen001, 2026-10-15)

- **Let there be X.** The Hebrew jussive יְהִי is the optative of the verb 'be': "<X> begó."
  (Heb 9:23 "matev vini begó", let there be another way). Gen 1:3 "Waev begó." The NT quotes
  the same verse as "Waev bekhā" (let light shine, 2Co 4:6), so either would do; the draft keeps
  begó for all the jussives of existence and "Yaq waev mø̀ndøgoám." (and there was light) for
  the fulfilment (feature existence).
- **Naming.** God names things with the same construction as people are named: "iz Yesu
  mbo-akhá" (he named him Jesus, Mat 1:25) gives "Mbumbukiam waev, iz Khøuwa mbo-akhá" (God
  named the light Day). The thing named stands first, set off by a comma.
- **Ordinals** are verbs of becoming, not adjectives: the Nth angel is "enzol ezoqam, gè-menas-éz"
  (Rev 8:8, the angel who became the second), "it is now the third day" is "khøuwa
  mø̀ndømø-misika-éz" (Luk 24:21), "on the third day" is "khøuwa timømbo-misika-ez ndøgo" (Luk
  9:22). Only 'first' is an adjective before the noun: "bugukhokhof enzol ezoqam" (Rev 8:7). The
  draft closes each creation day with "Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-<N>-éz." and the first with "Ndøgo
  bugukhokhof khøuwá." The prefix chain is not fixed (foa takes mø̀ndømbo-, Jhn 11:39); menas
  and siks are built by the pattern.

## In Genesis 1–30 (the team, 15.10.2026): corrections

The section above records our Genesis 1 draft; the team's Genesis differs in all three points:

- **Let there be X** is *bete-* + *fakhan* (come about): "Løvøzumba betéfakhan" (GEN 1:6),
  plural "beté-fakhanam" (1:14); for light "Waev betékha. Yaq waev vø̄kha." (1:3). Not *begó*
  (formula:let_there_be.g).
- **Naming** is confirmed ("iz “paveat” mbo-akhá", 1:5), but the light is named **paveat**
  (daylight), not Khøuwa; for a child "Yaq ndugu iz X mbo-akhá." with the meaning in brackets
  (formula:she_named_him.g).
- **Ordinals** (ordinal.g): the creation days are "Ndakin khøuwa gè-<N>." (gè-menás, gè-misiká,
  gè-foá, gè-fáev, gè-síks; 1:8–31) and the first "Khøuwa qa-kopo, nqánek" (1:5). "On the third
  day" = "khøuwa misika-te ndøgo" (22:4), "the seventh day" = "khøuwa seven-te ndøgo" (2:2). Only
  dates keep -ez: "løvøyak gimø-menas-ez" (in the second month, 7:11, 8:14), "khøuwa
  gimø-seventin-ez" (on the seventeenth day, 8:4); "eight days old" = "khøuwa gimømbo-eit-ez"
  (21:4); first = bugukhokhof (8:13).
- **Numbers** (numerals.g): English loans throughout, as said: wan andled (100), andled teti
  (…30), faev andled (500), wan taosén (1000), teti-faev (35), tuenti-sevén (27), nainti-nain
  (99); kopo, menas, misika stay Kuni. Ages (age_and_years): "X viav N qamømbo-ndap" (when X was
  N years old, 22 times) or "viav N mbogoám" (12:4); a life: "viav ate qambøegoam, N geyagam"
  (5:5). Weighed silver: "fo andled silva moni mokho" (400 shekels, 23:16).

## From the study runs on the NT (2026-10-15, checked by agent:curation-study)

- **Plural**: nouns have no general plural ending. Number shows in a numeral, a plural pronoun
  or possessive (-gi, ndigu), the verb, a plural stem (nakhasam / nakheis), reduplication
  (kawakawa, bawabawan) or -za on some person nouns (kawakawa-za, tat-za, and the kin
  -pakha-za of Ruth). Do not add -za to things (sip sheep, manqat word stay as they are).
