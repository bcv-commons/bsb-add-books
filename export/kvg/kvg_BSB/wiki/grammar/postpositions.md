# Postpositions and noun suffixes

## Postpositions and suffixes on nouns

- -te: in, at, to, and also from: "Betliem vemiav-te" (Luk 2:6), "Mbumbukiam-qá khoev-té
  ginøzáv" (to the temple, Luk 2:22), "Izip manqei-te ginduqavaz" (came back from Egypt,
  glossary Balam), "no-te" (to me, Luk 1:25), "ti-te" (to them, Act 3:11).
- namba: with: "Elizabet namba gi꞉søkhoam" (Luk 1:56), "tu namba" (with her, Luk 1:58).
- -qa yaq-te: about, concerning (Luk 1:4 heading, 2:17); zapaya: because of (Luk 15:... "qo
  zapaya", Mrk 14:31); nonqo: for, purpose (Luk 2:24).
- mokho-te: inside: "emu mokho-te" (in the womb, Luk 1:15, 1:36); "Zuda manqei mokho-te"
  (Mat 2:6).
- zita-te: behind, after: "Yesu-qá zita-té gunduwáv" (Luk 7:38); "taqa zita-te" (after that).
- avønin-te: near (Luk 7:38); megemege-te: before, in front of (Luk 1:8).
- -qa after a verb stem: purpose, "in order to": "okha gumat" / "tøke-qa gemboviam"
  (Luk 15:15), "Manqat yoge-qa mbozáv" (came to hear, Luk 15:1), "nqawa vesezam-qa ndøgoám"
  (wanted to go back, Act 7:39), "veseze-qa" (1Co 16:11).
- -ak: person/people of: "Izlael-ak", "vemiav-ak ezoqa ewaqape" (the whole town, Mrk 1:33),
  "Itiopia manqei-ák" (Act 8:27), "Asa khagua bawan-ák" (of the tribe of Asher, Luk 2:36),
  "Masedonia plovins-ak menas" (two Macedonians, Act 19:29).
- -us: having, with: "emu-ús" (pregnant, Luk 2:5), "iz-ús" (Luk 1:9), "bazaføgakh-us".
- até X-a: also X, even X: "Até Zoséf-a" (Luk 2:4), "Até Elizabét-a" (Luk 1:36),
  "Yaq até ndegó-a, vø̄nanim" (he too died, Luk 20:30).
- mba: only: "Mbumbukiam mba me꞉gó" (Luk 18:19), "khoev mokho-te mbá gunu꞉goám" (Luk 1:24);
  yakhapús: alone (1Ti 1:17, 1Co 5:13).

## In Ruth and Jonah (human, 2015)

- -te for to, in, from is confirmed throughout ("Moab manqei-té genøyág" went to Moab, RUT 1:1;
  "Moab manqei-te gunduqavan" came back from Moab, 2:6); lexeme-aligner's WALS value
  (postpositions) agrees; its own alignment statistic ("mixed") does not (see [sources](../sources.md)).
- namba 'with' after the companion, often after the verb: "zas neka nakhei angana menas namba"
  (1:1), "Naomí namba me꞉geavún" (2:6); avønin-te 'beside' (2:14 'sasae ezoqam namba', 3:7
  'møvøza avønin-té'); mokho-te 'inside' ("zongøyam-qá emu mokho-té", JON 2:1; "palai khagua
  mokho-te", 1:5); "zenda tokhotapak-te" (at his feet, RUT 3:4, 3:7, 3:14); "bugug-té" (before
  the face of, 3:13); "-qa yaq-te" about ("gumbag-qa yaq-te", JON 4:9-10; "lou-qa yaq-te" with
  food, RUT 1:6); zapaya because of ("tiqa manqa-zapazapa zapaya", JON 3:5).
- -ak origin confirmed: "Moab manqei-ak mbasønakám" (RUT 2:6), "Niniva taon-ak" (JON 3:5),
  "manqei vini-ák" (foreigner, RUT 2:10), "gekha manqei-âk?" (JON 1:8).
- -us having: "anganeam-ús" (with a husband, RUT 1:21), "bazaføgakh-us" (with strength, JON
  1:13, 3:8), "kuku matev-us Mbumbukiám" (a God full of love, JON 4:2).
- ava is a **bag** (RUT 2:17, JON 1:7), not a lap (our term:H2436 was wrong).
