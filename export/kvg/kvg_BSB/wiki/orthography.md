# Writing: tone marks and letters

See the index page for the tone marks the NT writes.

## In Ruth and Jonah (2015)

- The 2015 books write the same marks as the NT; 23-24 tone marks per 100 words (NT 22, our
  drafts 19): copy every mark from the model form.
- A star marks a glossary word: "*Yawe" (RUT 1:6). Headings have no tone marks ("==Elimelek
  Moab manqei-te geyag==").
- Spelling differs a little from the revised NT: "lovalova" (JON 4:7) vs NT "lovølova";
  "gemanaz" (Ruth) vs "gendomanaz" (Mat 1). Prefer the NT's spelling where it has the word, and
  note the case (see [sources](sources.md)).
- Letter correspondences for names are on the [names](names.md) page.
