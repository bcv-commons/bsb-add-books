# Questions for the Kuni team

Numbered questions where two findings disagree. The store keeps the entry based on the human
Ruth and Jonah; the other reading is written here with its evidence. Answers go into the store as
a new entry (with `supersedes` where the answer changes a fact). Further questions are marked on
the pages as "Question for the Kuni team".

1. **Idols: khape mbumbuk or khokho mbumbuk?** The store has khokho mbumbuk for Jonah's
   "worthless idols" (term:H1892, JON 2:9). A study run on the NT found khape mbumbuk for
   idols in general: 16 verses, 7 of the 21 BSB verses with idol(s) (1CO 8:4, 8:7, 10:19, 1JN
   5:21), now term:H6456; khokho goes with "false" (11 of 30 BSB "false" verses; "khokho
   mbumbuk" in LUK 6:26, MAT 24:24). Which word should the OT use for plain "idols", carved
   images and the gods of the nations?
2. **חֶסֶד (loving devotion): tanakh?** The store renders it as good deeds, matev mbomømboma, for
   people (RUT 1:8, 3:10) and as kuku matev for God (JON 2:9, 4:2), term:H2617.h. A study run
   proposed tanakh, the NT's word for mercy (75 of 85 BSB mercy/compassion verses, 1CO 7:25,
   1PE 1:3), which Ruth uses for favour (term:H2580.h). Is tanakh right for God's חֶסֶד in the
   Psalms ("his loving devotion endures forever"), or should it stay kuku matev?
3. **New OT names: from the English sound or from the spelling?** The study runs build names
   from the English pronunciation, as the NT does (Zems James, Pailøt Pilate, 1TI 6:13). The
   human Ruth keeps names new in the OT close to the spelling: Naomi (not Neomi), Elimelek,
   Kilion (letter:vowels.2). The store follows Ruth for names the NT lacks and the NT form for
   names it has. Is that the rule the team wants?
