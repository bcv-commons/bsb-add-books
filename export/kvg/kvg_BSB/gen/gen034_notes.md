# Genesis 34 (gen034), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 34 (31 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen034.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen034.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 33 draft and notes (thread, Sekem, Hamo, *khavoz*); the team's Genesis 23
(negotiation at the gate), 17 (circumcision), 14 (the raid: goods carried off, people seized), 12:15–19
and 29:23 (taking a woman, sleeping with her), 20:9 (a thing not done), 24 and 29 (giving a daughter as
wife). Short sentences opened by *Yaq* or *Geté*, verb last; short frames (*gezø-ein*, *gindu-qavøinam*),
the long frame only for Shechem's demand to his father and for Hamor's address at the gate.
Headings follow the BSB's two (v1, v13), without tone marks.

## Not found in the corpus (built, or rendered by meaning)

- **Defile** (v5, 13, 27): no model for defiling a person; "did an immoral act with her" (REV 18:3).
- **By force** (v2): "she did not want; he by strength …" (no word for rape).
- **Dowry** (v12): no word; *fia* (price) as GEN 23:15 (gap).
- **Intermarry** (v9): *bizi-tøkuaném* (let us unite), built.
- **Stench** (v30): by its meaning, "they hate me".
- **Hesitate** (v19): "quickly"; **unsuspecting** (v25): "they did not know" as its own sentence.
- **Acquire property** (v10): "buy land".
- Built verb forms (the patterns are attested, these forms not seen): *gumøfakhan*, *qotøndó-ndáf*,
  *tozombo-etoam*, *tozonøgo*, *tozonø-viømemat*, *gìnø-etoám*, *ndø̀izø-etøomém*, *zíndafém*,
  *vø̀ndafém*, *bizi-tøkuaném*, *tizi-matønømem*, *tizi-etoam*, *ti꞉kopo꞉nem*, *zí-kopo꞉nem*, *tiyagat*,
  *tigoat*, *gégoâk*, *zømbe-khavozám*, *vøzø̄-geanem*, *vø̄-aqonømem*, *qazo-amb*, *ti꞉tøkuanem* (attested

## Open questions for the Kuni team

1. **YHWH**: not in this chapter; God does not occur either.
2. **How plainly to tell the rape** (v2) and the word for "defiled" (v5, 13, 27): is *mambe matev* right,

4. **Intermarry** (v9) and **acquire property** (v10): right words?

6. **Stench** (v30): "they hate me" is the meaning; is there an image Kuni uses?
7. The **built forms** above, especially the 1pl irrealis *tizi-* and the plural imperatives.
