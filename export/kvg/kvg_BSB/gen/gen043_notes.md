# Genesis 43 (gen043), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 43 (34 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen043.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen043.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 42 (the brothers' first journey: *zapikia-za*, *moqos*, *moni*, "the man",
*okhov-qase*, *Ni lou wi-qa qazigeavun*) and 41, and the team's Genesis 1–30: 29:6 (*Ndego kopømbâ
tego?* "Is he well?"), 24:32 (water to wash the feet, food for the animals), 19:12 ("Do you have anyone
else?"), 26:30 (they ate and drank), 28:3 (God Almighty in a wish), 2:18 (*yakhapus*), 30:15 (*Ság.*).
NT models: JHN 21:15 (finished eating), JHN 14:5 (how can we know?), ACT 20:25 (you will not see my face
again), ACT 10:5 (send, 2sg), PHM 1:19 (Paul standing for a debt), JHN 15:22 (counterfactual), HEB 10:37
and 1TI 3:15 (delay), 2CO 11:24 (*Khøuwa faev*, five times), MAT 2:11 (gifts, spices, myrrh), LUK 15:23
(kill the calf), MAT 1:24 (did as he was told), ACT 5:23 (we arrived and opened), GEN 21:26 (I do not know
who did it), MAT 13:52 (treasure), ACT 16:30 (brought him out), ACT 20:10 (he is still alive), MAT 2:11
(bowed and worshipped), MRK 2:5 (*Áti*, son), 2TI 1:18 (mercy wish), MAT 6:6 (inner room), JHN 9:7 (washed
his face), LUK 15:22 (bring, pl), MAT 12:4 (not lawful: *khafeáp*), MRK 9:15 (*nqova zøndáf*). Headings after
the BSB's two headings, without tone marks.

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## Open questions

1. **Surety** (v9): "I will look after him; you may ask me about him" — does Kuni have a way to say "I stand for
   him / I take the blame"? (44:32 repeats it.)
2. **Double the silver** (v12, 15): *moni bawan menás* (two lots of money) — understandable?
3. **Pistachios and almonds** (v11): "two kinds of tree fruit"; are there local nut names to use?
4. **"Peace to you"** (v23) as *Ság.* (fine) — or a greeting with *sambi*?
5. **"They drank freely"** (v34): the Hebrew says they became drunk; the draft says they ate, drank and were glad.
6. **"May God be gracious to you, my son"** (v29): *Áti* as the address from a ruler to a young man.
7. Least sure: v9, v10, v12, v18, v31.
