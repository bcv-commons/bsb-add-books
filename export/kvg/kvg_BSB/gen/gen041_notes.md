# Genesis 41 (gen041), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 41 (57 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen041.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen041.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 37 (the dream formulas), 39 and 40 (Felo, the cupbearer and baker, the
prison, the interpretation formula), the team's 12:10–20 (Pharaoh, famine), 4:25 and 29:32 (name
meanings in brackets), 28:12 (dreaming), and the NT: ACT 7:9–13 (the NT's own telling of Joseph and the
famine), ACT 8:28 (chariot), ACT 13:6 (sorcerer), LUK 8:6–8 (seed springing up and withering), LUK 12:18
(barns), LUK 15:22 (ring), JHN 2:5 (do whatever he tells you), REV 17:9 (X are seven Y). Headings after
the BSB's five headings, without tone marks.

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## New name forms

- *Nael kea* — the Nile (יְאֹר), by the letter rules (/ai/ → ae) with the river class noun *kea*; later
  *ta kea*. Not in the corpus.
- *Zafenat-Panea* — Zaphenath-paneah (v45).
- *Asenat* — Asenath (v45, 50).
- *Potifela* — Potiphera, priest of On (v45, 50); kept apart from Potiphar, *Potifa* (37:36, 39:1).
- *On taon* — the town On (v45, 50); he is "On taon-ge iziz ezoqam".
- Reused: Zosef, Felo, Izip, Izip manqei-ak, Iblu ezoqam, Mbumbukiam; *Manase* and *Eflaim* (NT forms,
  MAT 1:10, JHN 11:54). No name in this chapter differs between the Hebrew and the BSB. No YHWH in this
  chapter.
