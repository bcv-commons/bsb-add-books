# Genesis 42 (gen042), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 42 (38 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen042.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen042.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 37 (the brothers, Reuben's speech, the prohibitive), 41 (Felo, Zosef as
"keoge ezoqam", the famine, *wit wi-qa*) and the team's Genesis 1 (*Yaq matev āv tønefakhán*), 20:7
(*qo qóyagét* you will live), 22:1 (testing), 26:27 and 29:4 (where are you from, why have you come),
9:24 (youngest son), 15:2 (childless), 19:31 (old). The NT's own telling of this chapter is ACT 7:11–13
(*vikia sekeqape ndømát*, *Zekop ge-ewag, av nqægo, … lou zua ndøgō*, *zapikia-za*). Other NT models:
LUK 20:20, HEB 11:31 (spies), MRK 12:14 (honest), TIT 1:13 (strong words), ACT 27:30 (pretend), ACT 7:26
(why do you … each other?), RUT 3:13 (the oath), JHN 18:12 (bound him), LUK 10:34 (on his donkey), MRK 6:8
and LUK 9:3 (bag, for the journey), ACT 7:39 (did not accept his word), JHN 11:40 (I told you), ACT 21:12
(plead), 1CO 12:30, 14:13 (interpret), RUT 1:19 (fear seized them), ACT 16:38 (alarmed). Headings after
the BSB's two headings, without tone marks.

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.
