# Genesis 38 (gen038), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 38 (30 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen038.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen038.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** The team's birth and naming verses (29:32–35, 30:23–24, 25:24–26, 4:1), Rebekah's veil
(24:65), the reports "X was told" (22:20), and our Genesis 34–37 (Judah, *zapikia-za*, *mambe sævam*, the
midwife of gen035). The NT gives the levirate law (MRK 12:19), the pledge and seal (2CO 5:5, REV 7:2),
the woman caught in adultery (JHN 8:3) and MAT 1:3 / 1:25 (Tamar, Perez and Zerah; "did not know her").
Headings follow the BSB's two ("Judah and Tamar", "The Birth of Perez and Zerah"), without tone marks.

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## New name forms

- **E** (Er): attested for Luke's Er (LUK 3:28–29).
- **Onan**, **Sua** (Shua), **Hila** (Hirah), **Adulam-ak** (Adullamite), **Kezib vemiav** (Chezib),
  **Timna taon** (Timnah), **Enaim vemiav** (Enaim): by the letter rules.
- Attested and reused: Zuda, Tama (MAT 1:3, *Tamá*), Sela (10:24; Judah's son has the same form), Pelez, Zela
  (MAT 1:3), Keinan-ak, Evezøza.

## Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

- *spill his seed*: gap (gap:spill_seed.g), "teqa nanga manqei-te sasa genqagim".
- *breach* (Perez): gap (gap:breach.g), "mòqo-keqá", meaning "(Taqa manqa mokho: Gè-keqá.)".
- *pull back the hand*: gap (gap:pull_back_hand.g), "pingim nqawa sègendaf".
- *father-in-law* 2nd person: *qoge zimui* (only JHN 18:13 *zimui*).
- *cord*: *mevoat* (one model, a thread, 14:23).
- *shrine prostitute*: same word as prostitute (*mambe sævam*).
- Built verb forms: *gu-uzupam*, *gukhòfoē*, *mø̀ndø-khonáo*, *guqotám*, *gúgo*, *qonǿ-etoām*, *ndǿkhofotáv*,
  *toqondo-khofotav*, *tèmbo-qatayat*, *vømbō-qatayat*, *zó-itú*, *vø̄-nqagimém*, *vø̄løvøtup*, *sègendaf*,
