# Genesis 35 (gen035), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 35 (29 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen035.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen035.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 34 and 31–33 drafts (names, *gembo-ein* frames, headings without tone
marks); the team's Genesis 28 (Bethel, the pillar), 17 (God Almighty, name change, kings), 23 and 25
(death, burial, "that is Hebron"), 29–30 (births and name meanings in brackets), 10:4 and 25:13 (lists
of sons). Headings follow the BSB's four (v1, v16, v21, v27). Short sentences opened by *Yaq*; the
long frame "āv … nqǽgo" for Jacob's order (v2) and God's solemn speech (v11).

## New name forms

Debola (Deborah), Alon-Bakut (Allon-bacuth, meaning given), El-Betel (El-bethel), Ben-Oni (Ben-oni),
Eda (Eder: "Eda-qa khoev eqawat-qape", the Tower of Eder). From the corpus: Kiliat-Aba (GEN 23:2),
Eflat, Betliem, Mamle, Hiblon, Bila, Zilpa, Den, Gad, Asa, Isaka, Zebulon, Benzamin.

## Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

- *midwife* (gap): "sævam, ndugu Laisol gumbotøke" (the woman who helped Rachel).
- *below* a town (gap): rendered "Betel taon avønin-te" (near Bethel).
- *drink offering*: wine (*waen*) after the NT; the Hebrew names no liquid.
- *earrings*: ring (*agiwa-qasi*) + "on their ears" (*gea-te*, built phrase).
- *purify yourselves*, *change your garments*: built on 1PE 1:22 and COL 3:12.
- Built verb forms: *vø̀khakheinám*, *ndæ̀khakheinám*, *vø̀-eqeieqei-éz*, *qoqo-khokhon*, *qo-te gefakhan*,
  *gemø-otáz*, *zøpavateáv*, *gunanimtát*, *qazø-etoam* (for "I gave them").

## Open questions

1. **YHWH**: no YHWH in this chapter (only Elohim / El Shaddai = Mbumbukiam, Bazaføgakh-Qape).
2. Drink offering as *waen*: acceptable, or a neutral "ibøkha" / no liquid?
3. A Kuni word for a midwife, and for "below / downhill from" a town?
4. "Be fruitful and multiply" (v11) is singular in Hebrew; the team's form *vø̀mønøzumít* is plural
   (to Noah and his sons). Keep, or a singular form?
5. Least sure: v2 (purify, change clothes), v12 (*qazø-etoam*), v17 (midwife and "another son"), v18.
