# Genesis 44 (gen044), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 44 (34 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen044.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen044.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 43 and 42 (the steward, *moqos*, *moni*, *okhov-qase*, *Khanánqa*, the
surety, "you will not see my face", "go back and buy food"), and the team's Genesis 1–30: 18:25, 18:30-32
(Abraham pleading), 21:2, 21:16 (Hagar), 24:18, 30:5-6. NT models: ACT 8:9 (sorcery), ROM 12:17 (repay evil),
REV 3:2, 1CO 1:8, 1CO 4:4, MAT 16:22, JHN 8:9, 1CO 10:16, 1PE 1:8, LUK 22:42. Headings after the BSB's two,
without tone marks.

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## Open questions

1. **"My lord" and "your servant"** in Judah's speech: the draft speaks to Joseph as *qo* and drops many "your
   servant"s; is the built *qeitaqa-qavøinam* (we answered you) right?
2. **Divination** as *manqa-sisi matev* (sorcery): too negative for Joseph's cup, or right for the steward's charge?
3. **Gray hair to Sheol** (v29, 31): rendered by the old man's mourning until death, as in 42:38.
4. **"Equal to Pharaoh"** (v18): *qo andé Felo me qogó* (you are like Pharaoh).
5. **"In place of the boy"** (v33): *nakhasam zapaya* (for the boy's sake).
6. Least sure: v15, v18, v20, v29–31, v33–34.
