# Genesis 40 (gen040), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 40 (23 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen040.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen040.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 37 and 39 (Felo, gaman ezoqam, vos keoge ezoqam-ge kawa, ndimbula; the dream
formulas of 37:5–10), the team's 4:5–6 (the fallen face), 1:4 (saw that it was good), 21:8 (the feast),
29:32 (taqa manqa mokho), and the NT: LUK 23:42 (remember me), MRK 6:21 (Herod's birthday banquet), MRK 6:27
(beheading), ACT 10:39 (hanged on a tree), ACT 25:10 (I have done nothing wrong), JHN 6:13 (baskets), LUK
22:18 / REV 14:18 (vine, grapes). Heading after the BSB's "The Cupbearer and the Baker", without tone marks.

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## New name forms

the BSB in this chapter. No YHWH in this chapter.

## Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

- *cupbearer* (waen etoam ezoqam) and *baker* (flawa løvusam ezoqam): agent nouns, built (term:cupbearer.g,
  term:baker.g). "Chief" as *kandambaqape* (ACT 5:17), given once (v2).
- *interpret / interpretation*: gap:interpret_dream.g applied ("taqa manqa mokho", *nømand*); still open.
- *squeeze* (*vø̄pesegim*): built from the winepress noun (term:squeeze.g).
- *budded, blossoms, ripened*: put together from HEB 9:4, JAS 1:10, MAT 9:37.
- *white bread* (*flawa papaqa*), *the top basket* (ma-clause), *kidnapped* (*gi-aqonømem*), "put me in the
  pit" (*ginø-nqeitan*).
- Built verb forms: *gezømbe-bevap*, *ndozote-keoge*, *qati-tamat*, *zonømbé-einīm*, *ndø̀qa-vé*, *soqo-vé*,
  *toqoyage*, *ndø̀nø-itú*, *ndø̀qanø-tøkéw*, *genø-tøkéw*, *gigonem* with *manqa-zapa*.

## Style check

`style.py --brief` first flagged *kandambaqape* (10 for about 1), *kawa* (12 for about 3), *noqa*, *mokho*,
*taqa*, *ezoqam*, *khoev -te*, *zenda -te* (more) and *manqat*, *matev* (fewer). Revised: the chief title
only in v2 (later just "the cupbearer", "the baker"); the repeated "the king of Egypt" in v1 folded into
"their master" and v5's into *Felo-ge*; "in prison" as *ndimbula-te* (ACT 4:3) in v3, v14 and v5 without
*khoev*; "my dream" without *noqa* in v9, v16; v17 without the extra *mokho-te*; *qo kawa* / *qo monqo* as
3:15 in v13, v19; v22 with the team's "Matev āv tønefakhán" (adds *matev*). Second run: left as flags *zenda
-te*, *kawa*, *mokho*, *taqa*, *tae*, *noqa*, *manqa*, *ezoqam*: the chapter's key words (hand ×5,
head ×6, interpretation ×6, the vine, the agent nouns). Forms found nowhere: *tam* and *ndøtam* (Genesis
only, which the check leaves out) and the built forms above.

## Open questions

   descriptive "wine-giving man" and "bread-baking man" right?
