# Genesis 39 (gen039), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 39 (23 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen039.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen039.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 37 and 38 (the thread of the story: Potifa, Felo, Ismael bawan, *kawa
ezoqam*), the team's 21:20 and 26:28 (God with someone), 24:2, 24:21, 24:36, 24:56 and 25:5 (the
servant's success; everything given into a son's hand), 29:17 (handsome), 21:9 (mocking), 22:12
(withhold), 20:6 (sin against God), 19:16 (grab), and the NT's prison scenes (MAT 18:30, ACT 12:5,
ACT 16:23–36). Headings follow the BSB's two ("Joseph and Potiphar's Wife", "Joseph Falsely
Imprisoned"), without tone marks; the second is shortened to "Joseph in prison".

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## New name forms

None. Reused: Zosef, Izip (manqei), Izip manqei-ak, Felo, Potifa (gen037), Ismael bawan, Iblu ezoqam (14:13),
Evezøza, Mbumbukiam. The BSB's "Potiphar" in v6, v10, v16 (Hebrew "he", "she", "his master") is not added
except in v5–6, where the subject needs a name.

## Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

- *confined* (v20): gap (gap:confined.g); "gi꞉goam" (were there); ACT 12:5 *ge-utavam* is the model for a
  closer word.
- *personal attendant* (v4): "his servant, near him" (term:attendant.g).
- *make sport of* (v14, 17): purpose *vewe-qa* built on 21:9.
- Built verb forms: *gembo-tøke*, *gu-eqa*, *gu-geagim*, *guzø-akhá*, *te-sømbi*, *sømbi-qa*, *tæ-matanam*,
  *tægo*, *qoqotøndo-itu*, *gè-ivāv* and *ndøbøīn* (macron content tones), *gezø-kewagám* (of things),
  *tentév* (attested, but not in this phrase); the rhetorical "Gê, no kopømbâ, …? Āv taoká."

## Style check

`style.py --brief` first flagged *khoev -te* (12 for about 2), *zenda -te*, *ekeza* (12 for about 3),
*ndabua*, *sege*, and fewer tone marks. Revised: *teqa* for *ekeza* where the owner is not the subject's own
(v4, v5, v6, v12), the garment without a possessive in v13, 15, 18; the master's wife in v8 just *zas*;
"in prison" as *ndimbula-te* (MAT 18:30) in v20, v22; *gè-iváv* (MAT 13:1) for three of the four *sège-iváv*;
"his master" in v20 as *ndego* (the subject of v19); *gimatønumam* (attested) for my *gimatanam*; five tone
marks copied from the models (*avønin-té*, *ndǿgo*). Second run: marks 19.6 (the human floor); left as
flags *zenda -te*, *khoev -te*, *ndabua*, *teqa*: the Hebrew key words of the chapter (בְּיַד "into his hand"
five times, the house, the garment six times). Watch items *kawa*, *gigiap*, *namba* the same (master,
"all he owned", "with him"). Forms found nowhere: *potifa* (name), *qanesømbi* and *keaeqateav* (Genesis
only, which the check leaves out), the built forms above.

## Open questions

1. **YHWH**: Evezøza in v2, v3 (twice), v5 (twice), v21 (twice), v23 (twice).
2. v4: is there a Kuni word for a personal attendant (Joseph serving Potiphar himself, also 40:4)?
3. v6, v8, v23: "he did not concern himself with anything" as "he knew nothing (at all)": is the meaning
   clear, and is it right for the warden's "did not look into anything" (v23)?
4. v7, v12: how plainly may the woman's words be told? *qanesømbí* is Rachel's word (30:3).
5. v9: the rhetorical "how could I do this?" is built; the BSB's "no one is greater than I" followed.
6. v20: a word for prisoners being "confined" or "kept" (ACT 12:5 *ge-utavam*?).
7. Least sure: v9, v13, v14, v17 (built purpose and macron forms), v22.
