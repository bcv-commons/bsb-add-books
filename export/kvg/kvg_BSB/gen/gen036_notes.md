# Genesis 36 (gen036), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 36 (43 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen036.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen036.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** The team's own genealogies: Genesis 10 (lists of sons, "X-gi nakhei angana ngínik: A
neka B …", "Nginik ezoqam ate ngi꞉gu, X-gí zí"), 25:1–4 and 25:12–16 (Ishmael's line, "Ndøgo āv
tøzømbe-peawáp", the princes *megetapak*), 22:20–24 (Nahor's sons, the concubine), 26:34 (Esau's wives one
by one), 14:1–8 (lists of kings), and our Genesis 35 (the list of Jacob's sons). Headings follow the BSB's
three, in the form of the team's Genesis 10 headings ("Zeifet-gi zi").

## Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

- *hot springs* (gap): "ibøkha tatas … ndøgo manqei mokho-té qando-fakhatám" (hot water that came up from the
  ground), built on GEN 2:6 and REV 3:15.
- *chief* (ʾallûp): no exact word; *megetapak* (leaders, 25:16 princes) used, said once per list.
- *reign* as a verb: none; "was king" (HEB 7:1) and "took his place, became king" (ACT 24:27 + *vø̄go*).
- *before any king reigned*: rephrased as "at that time the Israelites had no king" (*mbain qagoam*).
- Word forms not seen with this subject: *gimbo-fakhaz* with a mother as subject (v5, 14; attested with a
  father, 10:2, and for "she bore them" the team also uses *mø̀ndømbo-fakház*, 22:20); *gezø-kewagám*
  with donkeys (30:36 has flocks).

## Style check

`style.py --brief` flags *nginik* (25 vs ~2 expected), *neka* (78 vs ~22) and *-gi* (28 vs ~7), and few tone
marks. The comparison verses are NT sentences; the chapter is a genealogy, and the team's Genesis 10 and
25:12–16 have the same density, so the lists were kept as the team writes them (feature genealogy_list.g).
Its "word forms found nowhere" list contains attested Genesis names (Eida, Basømat, Hadad, Zobab, Sia, Hol,
Uz, Bela, zozokha) because the check leaves Genesis out; the rest are new names.

## Open questions

1. **YHWH**: not in this chapter (no divine name at all).
2. *Olibama* or *Oolibama* for Oholibamah (the strict letter rule keeps both vowels)?
3. Shaul (36:37–38) as *Sol*, the same as King Saul, or a separate form (*Saul*)?
4. 36:39 Hebrew *Hadar*: the draft follows the BSB's *Hadad* (1 Chr 1:50); 36:26 Hebrew *Dishan*, the draft
   *Dison* with the BSB.
5. *megetapak* for the clan chiefs of Edom and the Horites, or a title before each name as the Hebrew has it
   ("megeat ezoqam Teman, …")?
6. "hot springs" (36:24): is there a Kuni word for a hot spring?
7. Least sure: v6 (long list, *gekhatob*), v12 (*Elifaz-te*), v24, v31, v40–43.

## Coordinator's fixes (consistency pass, 2026-10-15)

- v26 *Dison* → *Disan* and v39 *Hadad* → *Hada*: the Hebrew has דִישָׁן (Dishan, as in v21, 28, 30) and הֲדַר (Hadar); the BSB follows another reading (Dishon; Hadad as in 1 Chr 1:50). The draft translates the Hebrew; Hada by the letter rule for a final r (Tama). The Hadad of v35–36 is another king, Hadad in the Hebrew too.
