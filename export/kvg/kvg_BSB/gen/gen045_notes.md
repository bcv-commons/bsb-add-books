# Genesis 45 (gen045), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 45 (28 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen045.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen045.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 44 and 43 (Joseph's brothers, *nogé tat*, *zøveqase*, *Khanánqa*, the
speech frames, "is he still alive"), the team's Genesis 1 and Genesis 12, 13, 26, 27, 29 (Esau weeping,
Laban embracing Jacob, Isaac sending Abimelech away, "before I die"). NT models: ACT 7:13 (Joseph made
himself known), ACT 10:21, JHN 9:37, JHN 14:1, 1TI 2:6, 1TI 5:4, LUK 18:22, ACT 8:28, TIT 3:2, LUK 1:12,
ACT 12:3, MRK 16:13. Headings after the BSB's four, without tone marks.

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## New name forms

- **Gosen** (גֹּשֶׁן, Goshen), as *Gosen manqei*: not in the corpus or the glossary; sh → s by the letter rules
  (name:goshen.g45). The BSB and the Hebrew agree on the name.
- Reused: *Zosef* (*Zoséf* as a predicate), *Benzamin*, *Felo*, *Izip*, *Izip manqei-ak*, *Keinan*, *Zekop*,
  *Izlael*; *Mbumbukiam*. No YHWH in this chapter.

## Fixed renderings for the following chapters

- wagons *manqei khagua wil-us* (46:5, 46:29 chariot); Goshen *Gosen manqei* (Gen 46–47, 50);
  "before I die" *Okhokho-té qabatø-naním.*; "embraced" *sège-møvøiú* (46:29 fell on his neck);
  "I will provide for you" *lou søtaqá-etoám* (47:12, 50:21); "still alive" *zua ngekhandî꞉vát* (46:30).

## Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

- wagon (v19, 21, 27), Goshen (v10), plowing (v6), remnant (v7), destitution (v11), "father to Pharaoh"
  (v8), "spirit revived" (v27; one model, 1CO 16:18).
- Built forms: *qazonø-wiím*, *qazonø-khofotáv* (2pl past with the 1sg object), *bezø̄-yagé*, *tozogo*,
  *nøzǿ-etoám*, *vø̀-veém*, *itu-qa*; *andé Felo-ge eve me nogó*.

## Style check

`style.py --brief` first flagged *manqei -te* (12 for about 2), *megemege -te* (4 for 1), *te* (5 for 1), and
fewer *mokho -te*; watch items *neka*, *ate*, *ekeza*, *gigiap*. Revised: "here" as *nqanek* without
*manqei-te* (v5, 8, 13), the famine without *manqei-te* (v6), "sent me ahead" as *bugukhokhof* (v7),
afraid "of Joseph" instead of "before him" (v3), *mokho-te* for "in Egypt" (v13, 18, 19), fewer *te* and
*ekeza* (v1, 3, 4, 14, 15, 23, 24), fewer sentence-initial *Yaq* and *Neka*. Left: *manqei* (18 for about
6), because Genesis writes "Izip manqei", "Keinan manqei" and the wagon term has *manqei*; the human
verses it compares with are from the NT, which names places without *manqei*. Forms found nowhere: *gosen*
(new name), *qoyaget* (the team's *qóyagét*, GEN 12:12; Genesis is left out of the style corpus), *itu*
(the built *itu-qa*).

## Open questions

1. **"A father to Pharaoh"** (v8): kept as a likeness (*andé Felo-ge eve me nogó*, I am like Pharaoh's
   father). Should it be explained ("his adviser")?
2. **Wagons** (v19, 21, 27): the chariot word *manqei khagua wil-us*; does Kuni have a word for a cart?
3. **The built 2pl past with "me"** (*qazonø-wiím*, *qazonø-khofotáv*, v4, 5, 8): is *qazonø-* right?
4. **"His spirit revived"** (v27): *kha vøndæv ndøndáp* (he was refreshed) or *khanakhanakh ndøndáp* (he
   became glad)?
5. **Remnant and deliverance** (v7) and **destitution** (v11): rendered by meaning, low certainty.
6. Least sure: v7, v8, v11, v19, v27.
