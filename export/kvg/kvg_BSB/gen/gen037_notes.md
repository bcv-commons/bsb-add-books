# Genesis 37 (gen037), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 37 (36 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen037.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen037.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** The team's Genesis 1 and 27 (father and sons, dialogue frames, "Yaq nqáe!"), 28:12 (a
dream), 29:6, 29:14, 29:30 (love, "own flesh"), our Genesis 34–36, and the NT's own account of Joseph
(ACT 7:9). Headings follow the BSB's two ("Joseph's Dreams", "Joseph Sold by His Brothers") in the form of
our earlier headings, without tone marks.

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## New name forms

- **Dotan** (Dothan, *Dotan taon*): by the letter rules (th → t).
- **Potifa** (Potiphar): ph → f, final r drops (as Tama, Asa).
- **Ismael bawan** (the Ishmaelites; *Ismael* is attested, 16:15) and **Medan bawan** (the Medanites of the
  Hebrew 37:36; *Medan* attested 25:2; the BSB has "Midianites" there).
- Attested and reused: Zekop, Izlael, Zosef, Bila, Zilpa, Luben, Zuda, Keinan, Sekem, Hiblon, Gilead (store,
  by rule), Midian bawan, Izip, Felo.

2. The coloured robe: a Kuni word or phrase for a garment of many colours (or an ornate, long-sleeved robe)?
3. "Torn to pieces" (v33, again 44:28): how does Kuni say an animal tore someone apart?
4. "Our brother" (v26–27): is there a 1pl form of *eveqase*, or is *nigi nøfu* right for blood brothers?
