# Ruth 3 (Kuni): style revision, control

- v3: "qo mè꞉-otevák" (a statement, "no one knows", 1Co 2:11, 1Jn 3:6) became the prohibitive wish "qo bete-otèva᷄t" ("do not let him know you"). Model: Mat 6:3 "qoqa yogea zenda bete-otèva᷄t, matev ndøgo".
- v7: "Lut khonoam-te ndowáv" became "gunduwáv", the main-clause form for a woman coming up. Models: Luk 8:44 / Mrk 5:27 "Yesu-qá zita-té gunduwáv, ... vømbō-khanaz".
- v14: Boaz's "Ezoqa mè꞉-otevák, av nqægo, ..." became "Ezoqa bete-otèva᷄t, av nqægo, ..." ("let no one know"). Model: Mat 6:3.
- v17: "khakha-mbá nango ndø-òkho᷄" ("do not act", Jas 2:1, 1Ti 5:21) became "ndø̀qa᷄v" ("do not go"). Model: Mrk 8:26 "Geté vemiav mokho-te ndø̀qa᷄v." (khakha-mbá from Luk 20:10).
