# Ruth 3: style revision

- v1: dropped the redundant pronoun "qo" before "qóyagát" (prefix carries the subject); model ACT 10:20 "Manqei-té qonǿvís".
- v3: dropped sentence-initial "Yaq" before "Bali evem nonqo-té qonǿvís"; model ACT 10:20 "Manqei-té qonǿvís."
- v4: "Yaq ndego ... ma ndego tesømbiam" -> "Ndego ... ma te꞉sømbimat" (unattested form replaced, no Yaq); model LUK 9:58 "khoev mbaín, te꞉sømbimat"; MAT 28:11 (clause opening without Yaq).
- v5: dropped "qo" before "toqonø-ein"; model GAL 6:7 "Gekha gigiāp ndøgo, toqo-ogeakh".
- v6: "Yaq ndugu matev ..." -> "Matev ..." (subject already given); model LUK 7:38 (short sentences without Yaq/pronoun repeat).
- v7: dropped opening "Yaq" and two "yaq/Yaq ndego"; model MAT 11:1 "qamømbøe-navøem, yaq ta vemiav ndøgo gè-iváv. Yaq vemivemiav nøme-té genøwáv"; MAT 21:17.
- v8: dropped opening "Yaq" before the time clause and "ndego" in "Yaq gendoqambun"; models LUK 1:5 ("Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo," opening), ACT 14:20 "Yaq taon-té genøveséz".
- v9: "Yaq ndego gumbobevap" -> "Yaq Boaz gumbobevap"; "Zapa ndǿgo, qo nigi ekeza ezoqám, ni ..." -> two short sentences; models ACT 12:19 (name instead of pronoun), LUK 8:48 (short sentences).
- v11: dropped "qo" before "toqonø-ein" (GAL 6:7); "ezoqa ewaqape mø̀ndæzøtéz, qo sævam eqeieqeí. Qoqa matev eqeieqeí." -> "ezoqa ewaqape, ndigu mø̀ndæzøtéz, qo sævam eqeieqeí."; model ACT 4:13 / 2CO 4:2 "ndigu mø̀ndæzøtéz, ...".
- v13: "Yaq qanaqanus," -> "Qanaqanus,"; model MAT 28:11 (subordinate clause opening without Yaq).
- v15: "Yaq ndego gumbo-ein" -> "Yaq gumbo-ein"; "Yaq ndugu sège-matanám" -> "Ndugu sège-matanám"; "Yaq ndego Betliem ..." -> "Yaq Betliem vemiav-té genøwáv"; models MAT 21:17 "Yaq Betani vemiav-té genøwáv", LUK 7:38 "Ndugu gèvín.".
- v16: two sentences joined: "Lut tugu zakhapuka-te gunøqavan, yaq Neomi gumbobevap"; model LUK 22:45 "gevesez, yaq āv genezømét".
- v17: "āv guni-eín nqǽgo" -> "gumbo-ein"; dropped "Zapa ndǿgo," before "Ndego āv genømbe-eín nqǽgo"; model LUK 2:34 "gumbo-ein".

Changed verses: 13 (of 18).

Left as is: the ndabua and zenda flags (clothes v3, uncovering at the feet v4/v7, cloak v9/v15, feet v8/v14 are in the Hebrew; LUK 7:38 repeats "zenda tokhotapak" the same way); built forms gusømbimam (ACT 9:33 gesømbimám), gunøvis (ACT 14:25 ginøvís), bèkhakheinøqavém (LUK 1:42 + be- optative), nqovotaz (ACT 7:34), vawa-qa (purpose -qa), ndowāv/Lūt (macron in indirect content).
