===Ibugukhokhof, chapter 1===

==Mbumbukiam yan neka manqei-qape gekhakheinam==

v1 Ibugukhokhof, Mbumbukiam yan neka manqei-qape gekhakheinam.
v2 Manqei-qape unimé꞉-khakhambá. Gigiap nøme mbaín. Yaq anana-mba-qape bøivun
ndønqeivøém. Yaq Mbumbukiam-ge Nqova, ibøkha ová, me꞉løvuvavún.

v3 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Waev begó.” Yaq waev mø̀ndøgoám. v4 Yaq
Mbumbukiam geqeiv av nqægo, waev ndøgo eqeieqeí. Yaq ndego waev neka
bøivun geqatøzo. v5 Yaq Mbumbukiam waev, iz Khøuwa mbo-akhá, neka
bøivun, iz Lova mbo-akhá. Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Ndøgo
bugukhokhof khøuwá.

v6 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ibøkha livin-te, khaiya begó. Yaq
ndøgo ibøkha beqatøzo, nøme ova, neka nøme mokho-te.” v7 Yaq Mbumbukiam
khaiya gekhakheinam. Yaq ndego ibøkha geqatøzo. Nøme khaiya
mokho-te ndøgo, neka nøme khaiya ová ndøgo. Yaq ate av-té
qafakhan. v8 Yaq Mbumbukiam khaiya, iz Yan mbo-akhá. Yaq qakhagus ndøgo,
qanaqanus qandopave. Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-menas-éz.

v9 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ibøkha, yan mokho-te ndøgo, manqei
kopo-té bepindám. Yaq manqei-qape befakhan.” Yaq ate av-té qafakhan.
v10 Yaq Mbumbukiam manqei-qape, iz Manqei mbo-akhá, neka ibøkha gipindam
ndøgo, iz Ndaola mbo-akhá. Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí.

v11 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Manqei-te gagayag bete-umianét:
avøe gigiap, nanga-us ndigu, neka tae bugug vinivinimbá, nanga ndǿviam.
Ta nanga mokho-te ndøugu, tae nanga.” Yaq ate av-té qafakhan. v12 Yaq
manqei-te, gagayag gi-umianat: avøe gigiap, nanga-us ndigu, bugug vinivinimbá,
neka tae, nanga ndǿviam, ta nanga mokho-te tae nanga ndøugu, bugug
vinivinimbá. Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí. v13 Yaq
qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-misika-éz.

==Mbumbukiam khøuwa neka løvøyak neka nduku gekhakheinam==

v14 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Waev kopoav khaiya-te begó. Ndigu
khøuwa neka lova beqatøzo. Neka ndigu umingiap begó, pindap
khøuwa-qa neka khøuwa-qa neka viav-qa. v15 Yaq ndigu waev begó,
khaiya-te, neka manqei-qape-te waev betekháev.” Yaq ate av-té
qafakhan. v16 Yaq Mbumbukiam waev kandakanda menas gekhakheinam. Waev
kandambaqape, ndøgo khøuwá, khøuwa-qá kawá. Neka waev khasøkhasis,
ndøgo løvøyák, lova-qá kawá. Neka até nduku-a gekhakheinam. v17 Yaq
Mbumbukiam ndigu khaiya-te genøvé. Yaq manqei-qape-te waev betekháev,
v18 neka khøuwa neka lova-qá kawá begó, neka waev neka bøivun
beqatøzo. Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí. v19 Yaq
qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Khøuwa mø̀ndømbo-foa-éz.

v20 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ibøkha-te, khandi gigiap kopoav-qape
begó. Neka pipisi manqei ová bete-løvuvat, khaiya-te.” v21 Yaq
Mbumbukiam søvakha gigiap kandakanda gekhakheinam. Ndigu ibøkha-te ndi-okho. Neka
ndego khandi gigiap ate ndægo gekhakheinam, bugug vinivinimbá. Ndigu
ibøkha-te kopoav-qape ndi-okho. Neka pipisi ate ndægo, ndiløvuvam, bugug
vinivinimbá, gekhakheinam. Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí.
v22 Yaq Mbumbukiam zenda gezømbove, neka āv gezømbe-eín nqǽgo, “Zo kopoav
zógoát. Ndaola-te, ibøkha ate qægoam, zo zóyagát. Neka pipisi,
manqei-qape-te, kopoav begó.” v23 Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave.
Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-faev-éz.

==Mbumbukiam søvakha gigiap neka ezoqam gekhakheinam==

v24 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Manqei-te, khandi gigiap bugug
vinivinimbá befakhan: søvakha gigiap nanák, neka gigiap ndivisigiap, neka
søvakha gigiap lozokhák, bugug vinivinimbá.” Yaq ate av-té qafakhan.
v25 Yaq Mbumbukiam søvakha gigiap lozokhak gekhakheinam, bugug vinivinimbá.
Neka ndego søvakha gigiap nanak, bugug vinivinimbá, gekhakheinam. Neka gigiap
ate ndægo, manqei-qape-te ndivisigiap, bugug vinivinimbá, gekhakheinam.
Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí.

v26 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ezoqam zíkhakheinám, andé niqa
bugug me. Yaq ndigu kawa zømbógoát: zonga, ndaola-te, neka pipisi,
khaiya-te, neka søvakha gigiap nanak, neka manqei-qape-te ate nqægo, neka
gigiap ate ndægo, manqei-qape-te ndivisigiap.”

//1 v27 Yaq Mbumbukiam ezoqam gekhakheinam, andé ekeza bugug me.

//2 Andé Mbumbukiam-qa bugug me, ezoqam gekhakheinam.

//2 Anganeam neká sævam, tèkhakheinøvém.

v28 Yaq Mbumbukiam zenda gezømbove, neka āv gezømbe-eín nqǽgo, “Zo kopoav
zógoát. Manqei-qape-te ate nqægo, zo zóyagát. Ndøgo, zoqá zenda-té
qatǿvé. Zo zonga, ndaola-te, neka pipisi, khaiya-te, neka khandi
gigiap ate ndægo, manqei-qape-te ndivisigiap, kawa zømbógoát.”
v29 Yaq Mbumbukiam gezø-ein, “Nqáe! No zo avøe gigiap ate ndægo, nanga-us
ndigu, manqei-qape-te ate nqægo, neka tae ate ndægo, ndigu nanga
ndǿviam, nanga mokho-te tae nanga-us, mø̀ezø-etoám. Ndigu zogí lou
ndǿgoát. v30 Neka søvakha gigiap lozokhak ate ndægo, neka pipisi ate ndægo,
khaiya-te, neka gigiap ate ndægo, manqei-qape-te ndivisigiap,
khandi-us ndigu, no gagayag ate ndægo, lou mø̀ezø-etoám.” Yaq
ate av-té qafakhan. v31 Yaq gigiap ate ndægo, Mbumbukiam gekhakheinam, ndego
geqeiv. Áe, ndøgo eqeieqei tantáv. Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus
qandopave. Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-siks-éz.
