# Genesis 1 (gen001), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 1 in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew
(`orig/heb/gen001.txt`, BHSA clause lines) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni NT, Ruth and Jonah
(eBible kvg, 2015, and the newer NT working copy), the store and the wiki. Status: experiment, not
reviewed. Tagged source: `gen001.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

Style model: the human Ruth 1, Ruth 2 and Jonah 1 (no earlier Genesis chapter exists). Short
sentences opened by *Yaq*, verb last, the long frame *āv gene-eín nqǽgo* for God's solemn speech,
*Áe* for narrative 'behold'. Headings carry no tone marks.

## Words and forms not found in the corpus

- **zíkhakheinám** (let us make, v26): hortative zí- + khakhein, by the pattern of JON 1:7.
- **bepindám** (let them gather, v9): pind- is only used of people; the waters are treated as
  gathering like a crowd.
- **bete-løvuvat** (let them fly, v20): optative bete- (MAT 13:30, MAT 5:16) + løvuv- (fly).
- **mø̀ndømø-menas-éz**, **mø̀ndømø-siks-éz** (second, sixth day): by the pattern of
  mø̀ndømø-misika-éz (LUK 24:21) and mø̀ndømø-faev-éz (LUK 19:18).
- **nanga-us**, **khandi-us** (with seeds, with life): -us 'with, having' as in anganeam-ús (RUT 1:21).
- **Constructions not found**: a verb for 'swarm' (v20), 'multiply' (v22, v28), 'subdue' (v28),
  'rule' said of the sun and moon (v16, v18). Each is recast; see the store gaps
  `gap:fruitful_multiply.h`, `gap:rule_over.h`, `gap:waters_gather.h`, `gap:birds_fly.h`,
  `gap:let_us_make.h`.

## Additions for the team (feature explicitation)

- v16: the sun and the moon are named ("ndøgo khøuwá", "ndøgo løvøyák"). The Hebrew deliberately
  says only 'the greater light' and 'the lesser light'.
- v28: 'subdue it' is rendered as a grant ("I put it into your hands").
- v22, v28: 'fill the earth / the waters' as 'live all over the earth / in all the water'.

## New name forms

No personal names in this chapter. The book title is new (below). God = Mbumbukiam throughout; the
chapter has no YHWH.

## Open questions

1. **Book title**: proposed *Ibugukhokhof* (In the beginning, after JHN 1:1, the book's first
   word), in the style of the NT's descriptive titles. Store `name:genesis_title` [open].
2. **Expanse / sky**: khaiya for the expanse, which God names *Yan*; is the distinction between
   khaiya (visible sky) and yan (heaven) right for the Kuni reader?
3. **Hovering** (v2): me꞉løvuvavún is the Spirit coming down like a dove (MRK 1:10). Does it
   allow 'hovering over the water'?
4. **Sun and moon** named in v16, and 'rule' as *kawa* (chief): acceptable?
5. **Be fruitful and multiply**: 'Zo kopoav zógoát' (become many) is built; is there a Kuni idiom?
6. **Seasons** (מוֹעֲדִים, v14) as *pindap khøuwa* (feast days), or *khøuwa neka viav* as in ACT 1:7?
7. **Plural of majesty** in v26 (*niqa*, our): the team may prefer a reading.

## Style check (scripts/style.py, final run)

Flags left as they are, with the reason: *Yaq Mbumbukiam* and *-ein* above the human figures
(the Hebrew refrains: eight "God said", seven "God saw"; already reduced by using *Yaq ndego*
and the short 'it was so'); *ibøkha*, *gigiap*, *manqei*, *khøuwa* above (the subject matter);
*matev*, *ezoqa*, *manqat*, *Geté* below (no persons, words or contrasts in the chapter). Marks per
100 words are a little below the human range (about 19 vs 19.6); the forms were copied with their
marks from the model verses, and the plain forms (*gekhakheinam*, *geqeiv*, *qandopave*) are
unmarked in the corpus too.
