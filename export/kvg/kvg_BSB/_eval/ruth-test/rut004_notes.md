# Ruth 4: translator's notes

## What this draft is

A machine draft of Ruth 4 in Kuni (kvg), made 14.10.2026 from the Hebrew
(`orig/heb/rut004.txt`, 22 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) with the BSB as the only
assisting translation. The drafter knows no Kuni: every word, suffix and construction was taken
from the Kuni NT, first through the verse pack (`_learn/packs/rut004.md`) and the learning notes,
then through `_learn/concord.py` (33 lookups) for what the pack lacked, and from chapters 1-3,
whose renderings are reused verbatim wherever the Hebrew repeats (Moab manqei-ak sævam, Moab
manqei-te gunduqavan, ekeza ezoqam, nqovotaz-qa, poeveav tøgoat, nqægo khaiya, tegu zas,
Qotøndéqāv, qanøqóm, mbasønakam, ziwap, the comparison "A, B mø̀ndøløvú"). The genealogy
(vv. 18-22) is copied from Mat 1:3-6. Confidence per verse: high = every piece is an attested NT
form or a direct substitution in an attested frame; medium = one or two forms built by analogy;
low = a construction without a close model. Status: experiment, not reviewed; no footnotes.

## Exegetical observations

- v1 הִנֵּה "behold" is "Nqáe!" as in ch3 v8. פְּלֹנִי אַלְמֹנִי ("so-and-so", the narrator keeps the man
  nameless) is the NT vocative "Nǿfu." (friend), as the BSB has it.
- The redeemer (גֹּאֵל) still has no noun. In narrative he is "ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo" (that
  relative), introduced in v1 as the one "of whom Boaz had spoken" (ch3 v12); the verb is
  nqovotaz throughout, as in chapters 2-3. In v4-6 the object is the land ("ta manqei").
- v3 מָכְרָה (perfect) is read with the BSB as "is selling" ("wi-qa gugoám", was about to sell).
  The NT stem wi both buys (Luk 14:18, Act 1:18) and sells (Act 5:1, 4:37).
- v4 "uncover your ear" = "I am telling you this" (2Ti 2:7); the BSB's "you" in "if you will not
  redeem" (many MSS) is followed against the MT "he".
- v5 קניתי (ketiv "I acquire") is read with the qere and the BSB: "you acquire". The levirate
  purpose "raise up the name of the dead on his inheritance" is a wish that his name stay on his
  land (begó); Luk 20:28's "patu bembō-qatayat" (raise up offspring) is the alternative.
- v7 The narrator's custom note is rendered as a case ("if a man wanted to redeem or buy land,
  he took off his sandal and gave it to his friend"); "confirmation" uses Heb 6:16's oath
  sentence verbatim. "Exchange" (תְּמוּרָה) is folded into wi (trade).
- v8 The Hebrew order (he said "buy it", then drew off his sandal) is kept; the BSB reverses it.
- v11 "build the house of Israel" is "they were the ancestors of the Israelites"; "do valiantly /
  be prosperous" is "may you have many possessions" (the BSB's reading of חַיִל, as in 2:1 and
  3:11 for wealth/worth); "call a name" is "may your fame spread".
- v14 "who has not left you without a redeemer" is turned positive ("who has given you a relative
  to redeem you"), since "has not left (you)" is attested only with 1sg objects.
- v15 "restorer of life" = "he will give you new life" (renewal, 2Co 4:16); "sustain your old
  age" = "when you are old, he will look after you".
- v16 אֹמֶנֶת "nurse" (foster mother) = "she looked after the child" (kewag).
- v18-22 copied from Mat 1:3-6 (same persons, same form); the heading is Mat 1's "...-gi
  atanakha-zapazapa-za" with David.

## New name forms

- Laisol (Rachel): attested, Mat 2:18 and the glossary (Benzamin).
- Lia (Leah): not in the NT; English "Lee-uh", i as in Zesi, final -a kept (Ena). The learning
  notes list "Leia (Leah)" as an example, but no such form is in the NT (Leiab is Rahab).
- Pelez (Perez), Tama (Tamar), Ezlon, Lam, Aminadab, Nason, Salmon, Obed, Zesi, Deivid:
  attested, Mat 1:3-6 / Luk 3:32-33.
- Eflata (Ephrathah, from ch1) here as a place: "Eflata manqei".

## Open questions

1. The kinsman-redeemer: "ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo" in narrative, nqovotaz for the verb; the
   built forms gè-nqovotáz (imperative), nó-nqovotáz (1sg future) and "nqovotaz matev" (right of
   redemption) need a speaker. Is there a Kuni term for the relative who takes over a dead man's
   land and widow?
2. v5/v10 "raise up the name of the dead on his inheritance" as the wish "teqa iz, teqa
   manqei-te nango begó": does it carry the levirate sense, or should Luk 20:28's "patu
   bembō-qatayat" (raise up offspring for him) be used?
3. v7 the custom note: is a conditional case ("tegoat ..., yaq ...") with a plain past natural
   for "used to", and does "wi" cover "exchange"?
4. v11 "May the LORD make the woman like Rachel and Leah" (bè-matanám with "andé"): built;
   "Lia" for Leah is a guess.
5. v11/v14 "be famous / may his name be known" as "may the news of you spread" (bè-panqavøém):
   the positive optative of panqavøem is not attested.
6. v15 "renew your life" ("give you new life") and "sustain your old age" (qá-kewagát): both
   built.
7. v3 "Naomi is selling" as "wi-qa gugoám" (was about to sell): is the tense right for a sale in
   progress?
8. "City gate" as "vemiav mboqog" (the NT has only "taon mboqog"): should Bethlehem here be a
   taon?
