Iqandengaz (Genesis), chapter 39

Zosef neka Potifa-gu zas

1 Zosef Izip manqei-té ginø-ituím. Yaq Potifa, ndego Felo-ge gaman ezoqam, neka Felo-gi vos keoge ezoqam-ge kawa, ndego Izip manqei-ak ezoqam, Zosef gewí, Ismael bawan ezoqam-te, ndigu ndego ndǿgo ginø-ituím.

2 Geté Evezøza mø̀ndøgoám, Zosef namba. Yaq teqa sasae-te, mokho qafakhan. Ndego tege kawa ezoqam-qa khoev-te geyagám. Ngenek kawa ezoqam, ndego Izip manqei-ák.

3 Yaq tege kawa ezoqam āv geneqeív nqǽgo, Evezøza ndøgoám, te namba. Neka matev ate ndægo, ndego gematanam, Evezøza ndego gembo-tøke, yaq mokho qafakhan.

4 Yaq tege kawa ezoqam, Zosef tanakh mbogó. Yaq Zosef, tege sasae ezoqam ndøgoám, te avønin-te. Yaq ndego Zosef, teqa khoev-te keoge ezoqam ndøvé. Neka gigiap ate qambøegoam, Zosef-qá zenda-té genø-vé.

5 Potifa Zosef, teqa khoev neka gigiap ate qambøegoam-te, keoge ezoqam ndøvé. Taqa zita-te, Evezøza ngenek Izip manqei-ak-qa khoev gèkhakheinøvém, Zosef zapaya. Teqa gigiap ate qambøegoam, khoev-te neka khae-te, Evezøza zenda mo꞉vé, ti-te.

6 Yaq Potifa gigiap ate qambøegoam, Zosef-qá zenda-té genø-iváv. Ndego gigiap-qase otevateáv. Teqa lou ndøgo, ndego ndølog, ta mba ge꞉-otev. Zosef, ndego bugug keogé. Qanimáv tentév.

7 Taqa zita-te, khøuwa nøme, tege kawa ezoqam-gu zas, Zosef-te bøi gu-eqa. Yaq gumbo-ein, “No namba qanesømbí.”

8 Geté ndego gèmbøeqasí. Yaq ndego zas gumbo-ein, “Nqáe! No nqanek khoev-te nqǽgo. Yaq noge kawa ezoqam gigiap-qase otevateáv. Gigiap ate qambøegoam, ndego noqa zenda-té genø-vé.

9 Nqanek khoev-te, ezoqa nøme mbaín, ndego no mø̀ndøløvú. Ndego gigiap-qase keaeqateáv, no-te. Geté qo mba. Zapa ndǿgo, qo tegu zás. Yaq gê, no kopømbâ, nqanek soqa matev kandambaqape tæ-matanam, neka manqa-zapa tægo, Mbumbukiam-qa bøi-te? Āv taoká.”

10 Ndugu khøuwa kopoav, Zosef manqat gumbo-ein. Geté ndego tuqa manqat yogeáv. Ndego poeveáv, tu namba te-sømbi, o tu avønin-té tegoat.

11 Khøuwa nøme, Zosef khoev-té genø-ón, ekeza sasae gone-qa. Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, khoev ezoqa nøme mbaín, ndǿgo, khoev mokho-te.

12 Yaq ndugu Zosef-qa ndabua gu-geagim, gumbo-ein, “No namba qanesømbí!” Geté Zosef teqa ndabua, tuqa zenda-te sège-iváv. Yaq gèbǿin, bavokhó genøfakhán.

Zosef ndimbula khoev-te

13 Yaq ndugu gu-omet, av nqægo, Zosef ndabua, tuqa zenda-te gè-ivāv, neka bavokhó ndøbøīn.

14 Yaq ndugu ekeza khoev ezoqa guzø-akhá, guzø-ein, “Nqáe! Ngenek Iblu ezoqam, ni-te ndø-itu, ni otøotam vewe-qa. Ndego no-te ndowáv, no namba sømbi-qa. Geté no manqat bazaføgakh-ús, qæ-akhá.

15 Ndego noqa akhayam ge-ewag ndøgo, ndabua, no avønin-té gè-iváv. Yaq gèbǿin, bavokhó genøfakhán.”

16 Yaq ndugu Zosef-qa ndabua, ekeza avønin-te ndøvé, atema tege kawa ezoqam, teqa khoev-te vø̄qavan.

17 Yaq ndugu, nqanek manqat até kopó, gumbo-ein, “Ngenek Iblu sasae ezoqam, ndego qo ni-te qoqotøndo-itu, ndego no-te ndowáv, no otøotam vewe-qa.

18 Geté no manqat bazaføgakh-ús, qæ-akhá ndøgo, ndego ndabua, no avønin-té gè-iváv. Yaq gèbǿin, bavokhó genøfakhán.”

19 Yaq Zosef-ge kawa ezoqam, ekeza zas-qa manqat ge-ewag. Ndugu gumbo-ein, “Qoge sasae ezoqam, matev nqanek no-te gego.” Yaq ndego qaqa kandambá gego.

20 Yaq ndego, até gendego, Zosef ndøndáf, ndimbula khoev-te vø̄-utav. Ta manqei ndǿgo, ma kawa ezoqam-gi ndimbula ezoqa gi꞉goam. Yaq Zosef, ta ndimbula-te ndǿgo, geyagám.

21 Geté Evezøza mø̀ndøgoám, Zosef namba. Teqa kuku matev, Zosef-te kandambá. Yaq Evezøza Zosef gembo-tøke, yaq ndimbula keoge ezoqam, Zosef tanakh mbogó.

22 Yaq ndimbula keoge ezoqam, ndimbula ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, ta ndimbula-te ndǿgo, Zosef-qá zenda-té genø-vé. Matev ate ndægo, ndigu ndǿgo gimatønumam, Zosef ndégo gezø-kewagám.

23 Ndimbula keoge ezoqam, gigiap-qase otevateáv, Zosef-qa zenda-te ndøgo. Zapa ndǿgo, Evezøza mø̀ndøgoám, Zosef namba. Neka matev ate ndægo, ndego gematanam, Evezøza ndego gembo-tøke, yaq mokho qafakhan.

Genesis 39 (gen039), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 39 (23 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen039.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen039.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. Our Genesis 37 and 38 (the thread of the story: Potifa, Felo, Ismael bawan, kawa ezoqam), the team's 21:20 and 26:28 (God with someone), 24:2, 24:21, 24:36, 24:56 and 25:5 (the servant's success; everything given into a son's hand), 29:17 (handsome), 21:9 (mocking), 22:12 (withhold), 20:6 (sin against God), 19:16 (grab), and the NT's prison scenes (MAT 18:30, ACT 12:5, ACT 16:23–36). Headings follow the BSB's two ("Joseph and Potiphar's Wife", "Joseph Falsely Imprisoned"), without tone marks; the second is shortened to "Joseph in prison".

Certainty: high = the construction is attested as used; medium = attested parts put together; low = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked (built).

New name forms

None. Reused: Zosef, Izip (manqei), Izip manqei-ak, Felo, Potifa (gen037), Ismael bawan, Iblu ezoqam (14:13), Evezøza, Mbumbukiam. The BSB's "Potiphar" in v6, v10, v16 (Hebrew "he", "she", "his master") is not added except in v5–6, where the subject needs a name.

Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

  • confined (v20): gap (gap:confined.g); "gi꞉goam" (were there); ACT 12:5 ge-utavam is the model for a closer word.
  • personal attendant (v4): "his servant, near him" (term:attendant.g).
  • make sport of (v14, 17): purpose vewe-qa built on 21:9.
  • Built verb forms: gembo-tøke, gu-eqa, gu-geagim, guzø-akhá, te-sømbi, sømbi-qa, tæ-matanam, tægo, qoqotøndo-itu, gè-ivāv and ndøbøīn (macron content tones), gezø-kewagám (of things), tentév (attested, but not in this phrase); the rhetorical "Gê, no kopømbâ, …? Āv taoká."

Style check

style.py --brief first flagged khoev -te (12 for about 2), zenda -te, ekeza (12 for about 3), ndabua, sege, and fewer tone marks. Revised: teqa for ekeza where the owner is not the subject's own (v4, v5, v6, v12), the garment without a possessive in v13, 15, 18; the master's wife in v8 just zas; "in prison" as ndimbula-te (MAT 18:30) in v20, v22; gè-iváv (MAT 13:1) for three of the four sège-iváv; "his master" in v20 as ndego (the subject of v19); gimatønumam (attested) for my gimatanam; five tone marks copied from the models (avønin-té, ndǿgo). Second run: marks 19.6 (the human floor); left as flags zenda -te, khoev -te, ndabua, teqa: the Hebrew key words of the chapter (בְּיַד "into his hand" five times, the house, the garment six times). Watch items kawa, gigiap, namba the same (master, "all he owned", "with him"). Forms found nowhere: potifa (name), qanesømbi and keaeqateav (Genesis only, which the check leaves out), the built forms above.

Open questions

  1. YHWH: Evezøza in v2, v3 (twice), v5 (twice), v21 (twice), v23 (twice).
  2. v4: is there a Kuni word for a personal attendant (Joseph serving Potiphar himself, also 40:4)?
  3. v6, v8, v23: "he did not concern himself with anything" as "he knew nothing (at all)": is the meaning clear, and is it right for the warden's "did not look into anything" (v23)?
  4. v7, v12: how plainly may the woman's words be told? qanesømbí is Rachel's word (30:3).
  5. v9: the rhetorical "how could I do this?" is built; the BSB's "no one is greater than I" followed.
  6. v20: a word for prisoners being "confined" or "kept" (ACT 12:5 ge-utavam?).
  7. Least sure: v9, v13, v14, v17 (built purpose and macron forms), v22.

20 Yaq ndego, até gendego, Zosef ndøndáf,
ndimbula khoev-te vø̄-utav.
Ta manqei ndǿgo, ma kawa ezoqam-gi ndimbula ezoqa gi꞉goam.
Yaq Zosef, ta ndimbula-te ndǿgo, geyagám.
21 Geté Evezøza mø̀ndøgoám, Zosef namba.
Teqa kuku matev, Zosef-te kandambá.
Yaq Evezøza Zosef gembo-tøke, yaq ndimbula keoge ezoqam, Zosef tanakh mbogó.
22 Yaq ndimbula keoge ezoqam, ndimbula ezoqa ate gi꞉goam,
ta ndimbula-te ndǿgo,
Zosef-qá zenda-té genø-vé. Matev ate ndægo,
ndigu ndǿgo gimatønumam,
Zosef ndégo gezø-kewagám.
23 Ndimbula keoge ezoqam, gigiap-qase otevateáv, Zosef-qa zenda-te ndøgo.
Zapa ndǿgo, Evezøza mø̀ndøgoám, Zosef namba.
Neka matev ate ndægo, ndego gematanam, Evezøza ndego gembo-tøke,
yaq mokho qafakhan.
