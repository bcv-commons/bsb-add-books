Iqandengaz (Genesis), chapter 34

Sekem, Daina namba mambe matev gego

1 Yaq Daina, ndugu Zekop-gu yu, ndugu Lia-te gumøfakhan, ta manqei-te ndøgo, nakhei mbasi okha-qa gunøwáv. 2 Sekem, Hiv-ak Hamo-ge yo, ndego ta manqei-ge kawa. Yaq ndego Daina gu-omet. Yaq guindaf. Daina poeveáv. Geté ndego bazaføgakh-te, tu namba venanqei nonqo vø̄tøkuanem. 3 Sekem-qa mbøni, Zekop-gu yu Daina-te, sègeqóm. Ndego ngunuk mbasønakam-te, poev kandambá. Yaq manqat eqeieqei gumbo-ein. 4 Yaq Sekem, tege eve Hamo, āv gembøe-eín nqǽgo, “Ngunuk mbasønakam, nogu sævam tugoat, qotøndó-ndáf.”

5 Zekop ge-ewag, av nqægo, Sekem, tege yu Daina namba, mambe matev gegō. Geté tegi nakhei angana, tege søvakha gigiap nanak namba, khae-te gi꞉goam. Yaq Zekop manqat nøme einiáv, atema ndigu vømǿfakhān. 6 Yaq Hamo, Sekem-gé evé, Zekop-te genøwáv, ndego namba manqa manqate-qa. 7 Zekop-gi nakhei angana, khae-te ginduzáv. Ndigu matev ndøgo giyogem, yaq mbøni vaev matev zøfakhán, neka qaqa kandambá gigonem. Zapa ndǿgo, Sekem, Izlael bawan mokho-te, soqa matev kandambaqape gego. 8 Geté Hamo gezø-ein, “Noge yo Sekem, teqa mbøni poev kandambá, zogu yu-te. Kopømbâ, ndugu tozombo-etoam, tegu sævam tugoat? 9 Ni neka zo, bizi-tøkuaném. Zogi nakhei mbasi, ni gìni-etoám. 11 Yaq Sekem, Daina-gé evé neka tugi zapikia-za, gezø-ein, “Zo no tanakh tozonøgo tøgoat, yaq gekha matēv ndøgo, zo tozonø-viømemat, no qazǿ-etoám. 12 Fia kopoav-qapé, neka etoam matev, no-te gèviám. Yaq gekha matēv ndøgo, zo tozonø-viømemat, no qazǿ-etoám. Geté ngunuk mbasønakam sa, nogu sævam tugoat, gìnø-etoám.”

20 Yaq Hamo neka tege yo Sekem, tiqa taon mboqog tambav-té ginduzáv. Yaq tiqa taon ezoqam-te, āv gini-einím nqǽgo, 21 “Nginik ezoqam, ni namba sambi matev-te ndiyage. Neka nigi nakhei mbasi, ti-te zí-etoám. 22 Geté ndigu ni mokho-te tiyagat, neka bawan kopó tigoat, ndigu manqat ni namba ti꞉kopo꞉nem, sa nqánek: Nigi angana ate ndi꞉gu, ngusu bèzøqogøzát, av ndigu ndi꞉gu. 23 Gê, tiqa søvakha gigiap nanak, neka tiqa gigiap, neka tiqa søvakha gigiap ate qægoam, ndøgo niqá gégoâk? Sa nqánek: Ni ti namba, manqat zí-kopo꞉nem. Yaq ndigu ni mokho-te tiyagat.”

24 Yaq taon ezoqam ate gi꞉goam, ndigu taon mboqog-te ginduzav, Hamo neka tege yo Sekem-qa manqat giyogem. Yaq angana ate gi꞉goam, taon mboqog-te ginduzav, ngusu vø̄qogaz.

25 Khøuwa misika-te ndøgo, ndigu zua nqosøgeap giyogem. Yaq Zekop-gi nakhei angana menas, Simeon neka Livai, Daina-gi zapikia-za, ekeza kaiya ndøndáf. Yaq taon-te ginduzáv. Taon ezoqam zøtezateáv. Yaq sasa ndøzav. 27 Yaq Zekop-gi nakhei angana nøme, løvøte ezoqam-te ginduzáv. Yaq taon-te gigiap vø̄-aqonømem. Zapa ndǿgo, ndigu tigu sævømiu namba, mambe matev gigonem. 28 Tigi sip neka kao neka donki, neka gigiap ate qægoam, taon-te neka khae-te, vø̄-upøgimem. 29 Tiqa gigiap ate qægoam, neka tigi nakheis ate, neka tigi sakheis, vøzø̄-geanem. Neka khoev mokho-te, gigiap ate qægoam, vø̄-aqonømem.

30 Yaq Zekop, Simeon neka Livai, gezø-ein, “Zo no soqain-te qazo-amb. Nqanek manqei ezoqam, Keinan-ak neka Pelez-ak, no gèsanqaná. Ni ezoqa khapimbá. More from the notes

Genesis 34 (gen034), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 34 (31 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen034.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen034.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. Our Genesis 33 draft and notes (thread, Sekem, Hamo, khavoz); the team's Genesis 23 (negotiation at the gate), 17 (circumcision), 14 (the raid: goods carried off, people seized), 12:15–19 and 29:23 (taking a woman, sleeping with her), 20:9 (a thing not done), 24 and 29 (giving a daughter as wife). Short sentences opened by Yaq or Geté, verb last; short frames (gezø-ein, gindu-qavøinam), the long frame only for Shechem's demand to his father and for Hamor's address at the gate. Headings follow the BSB's two (v1, v13), without tone marks.

Not found in the corpus (built, or rendered by meaning)

  • Defile (v5, 13, 27): no model for defiling a person; "did an immoral act with her" (REV 18:3).
  • By force (v2): "she did not want; he by strength …" (no word for rape).
  • Dowry (v12): no word; fia (price) as GEN 23:15 (gap).
  • Intermarry (v9): bizi-tøkuaném (let us unite), built.
  • Stench (v30): by its meaning, "they hate me".
  • Hesitate (v19): "quickly"; unsuspecting (v25): "they did not know" as its own sentence.
  • Acquire property (v10): "buy land".
  • Built verb forms (the patterns are attested, these forms not seen): gumøfakhan, qotøndó-ndáf, tozombo-etoam, tozonøgo, tozonø-viømemat, gìnø-etoám, ndø̀izø-etøomém, zíndafém, vø̀ndafém, bizi-tøkuaném, tizi-matønømem, tizi-etoam, ti꞉kopo꞉nem, zí-kopo꞉nem, tiyagat, tigoat, gégoâk, zømbe-khavozám, vøzø̄-geanem, vø̄-aqonømem, qazo-amb, ti꞉tøkuanem (attested as a form, here conditional), ndømatanâm, bèyagé, bègonem, vozomǿ-gonēm, vø̀wím; zogu.

New name forms

  • Hiv-ak (Hivite, v2) and Pelez-ak (Perizzites, v30): not new, the team's forms (GEN 10:17, 13:7).
  • Daina, Sekem, Hamo, Simeon, Livai, Keinan-ak: as the store.
  • No new names.

Fixed renderings kept

Sekem, Hamo (ACT 7:16); "X's father" = X-gé evé; deceive = khavoz; sons = nakhei angana; daughters = nakhei mbasi; brother(s) = zapikia-za; livestock = søvakha gigiap nanak; flock = sip; city gate = taon mboqog tambav (Genesis 23).

Open questions for the Kuni team

  1. YHWH: not in this chapter; God does not occur either.
  2. How plainly to tell the rape (v2) and the word for "defiled" (v5, 13, 27): is mambe matev right, or is there a word for violating a woman?
  3. Bride price (v12): is there a Kuni word?
  4. Intermarry (v9) and acquire property (v10): right words?
  5. "His soul clung to Dinah" (v3): is mbøni … sègeqóm understood, or only "he loved her"?
  6. Stench (v30): "they hate me" is the meaning; is there an image Kuni uses?
  7. The built forms above, especially the 1pl irrealis tizi- and the plural imperatives.

Least sure

v9, v10, v22, v23 (long speeches of negotiation with many built forms), v31 (the question's form).

Sekem, Daina namba mambe matev gego

1 Yaq Daina,
ndugu Zekop-gu yu, ndugu Lia-te gumøfakhan,

2 Sekem, Hiv-ak Hamo-ge yo, ndego ta manqei-ge kawa. Yaq ndego Daina gu-omet.
Yaq guindaf.

zo tozonø-viømemat,
no qazǿ-etoám. Geté ngunuk mbasønakam sa, nogu sævam tugoat, gìnø-etoám.”
