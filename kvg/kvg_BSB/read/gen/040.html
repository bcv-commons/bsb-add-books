Iqandengaz (Genesis), chapter 40

Waen etoam ezoqam neka flawa løvusam ezoqam

1 Taqa zita-te, khøuwa nøme, Izip kawa ezoqam-ge waen etoam ezoqam neka flawa løvusam ezoqam, tige kawa ezoqam-te, manqa-zapa gigonem.

2 Yaq Felo qaqa kandambá gego, tegi gaman ezoqa menas-te, waen etoam ezoqam kandambaqape neka flawa løvusam ezoqam kandambaqape.

3 Yaq ndego ndigu ndimbula-té genø-nqeitáz, vos keoge ezoqam-ge kawa-qa khoev-te. Ta ndimbula khoev ndøgo, ma Zosef ndimbula ezoqam gegoam.

4 Yaq vos keoge ezoqam-ge kawa, Zosef ndøvé, ti namba. Yaq Zosef tige sasae ezoqam ndøgoám, ti avønin-te. Yaq ndigu ndimbula-te, khøuwa andé kandambá gisokhoam.

5 Yaq ndigu menas, Felo-ge waen etoam ezoqam neka flawa løvusam ezoqam, ndigu ndimbula ezoqa gigoam, lova kopo-te, venanqei ndøtám. Tiqa venanqei vinivinimbá, neka taqa manqa mokho até vinivinimbá.

6 Qanaqanus, Zosef ti-té genøwáv. Yaq ndigu ndøzømét. Nqáe! Ndigu mbøni ndøzøvaev.

7 Yaq ndego Felo-gi gaman ezoqam, ndigu te namba ndimbula-te gigoam, tege kawa ezoqam-qa khoev-te, āv gezømbe-bevap nqǽgo, “Gekha zapâ, nqægo zoqa bugug avus-mba ndozote-keoge?”

8 Yaq ndigu gindu-qavøinam, “Ni venanqei qati-tamat. Geté ezoqa nøme mbaín, taqa manqa mokho tenømand.” Yaq Zosef gezø-ein, “Gê, venanqei-qa manqa mokho, Mbumbukiam-qâ mbà? Zoqa venanqei, zonømbé-einīm.”

9 Yaq waen etoam ezoqam, ekeza venanqei, Zosef gembo-ein, “Venanqei tam-te, nqáe! Waen tae nøme, noqa megemege-te qagoam.

10 Neka waen tae-te, tang misika qagoam. Yaq mbusa qafakhan ndøgo, flawas vø̄fakhan, neka waen tae nanga qakhatøkhatoam.

11 Felo-qa kap noqa zenda-te qagoam. Yaq no waen tae nanga nø̀ndáp, Felo-qa kap-te vø̄pesegim, neka ta kap, Felo-qa zenda-te vø̄ve.”

12 Yaq Zosef gembo-qavøiu, “Taqa manqa mokho nqánek: Tang misika ndøgo, khøuwa misiká.

13 Khøuwa misika mokho-te, Felo qo kawa ndø̀qa-eqá. Yaq qo nqawa qoqa sasae-te ndø̀qa-vé. Yaq qo Felo-qa kap, teqa zenda-te soqo-vé, av bugukhokhof ndægo, qo tege waen etoam ezoqam qoqotegoam.

14 Geté qo mbomambá toqoyage ndøgo, no gènøndo-matøvém. Matev mbomømboma, no-te vø̀matanám. Felo noqa yaq-te, manqat vømbō-ein. Yaq ndego no, nqanek ndimbula-te ndøgo, bavokhó ndø̀nø-itú.

15 Zapa ndǿgo, ezoqa nøme, no Iblu ezoqam-qa manqei-te ndøgo, gi-aqonømem. Neka até nqanek manqei-té-a, no manqa-zapa-qase goneáv. Geté ndigu no manqei ozu-té ginø-nqeitan.”

16 Yaq flawa løvusam ezoqam āv geneqeív nqǽgo, Zosef-qa manqa mokho ndøgo, qanimáv. Yaq Zosef gembo-ein, “Até nó-a, venanqei qate-tamat. Venanqei tam-te, nqáe! Yakhav misika, noqa kawa-te qagoam. Flawa papaqa yakhav-té qøugoam.

17 Ta yakhav ndøgo, ma ova-te qagoam, lou vinivinimba qøugoam, Felo-qa lou, flawa løvusam ezoqam gi꞉løvusumatun. Geté pipisi, noqa kawa-te ndøgo, ta yakhav-te ndøgo, ta lou gi꞉logat.”

18 Yaq Zosef gendo-qavøiu, “Taqa manqa mokho nqánek: Yakhav misika ndøgo, khøuwa misiká.

19 Khøuwa misika mokho-te, Felo qo monqo ndø̀qa-kepáz. Yaq qo tae-té ndø̀qanø-tøkéw. Yaq pipisi qoqa kha vø̄log.”

20 Khøuwa misika-te ndøgo, Felo ge꞉qan, ta khøuwa iz gemø-khantaz. Yaq Felo lou kandambaqape ndølóg, tegi gaman sasae ezoqam ate gi꞉goam namba. Yaq tegi gaman sasae ezoqam-qa bøi-te, ndego waen etoam ezoqam-qa kawa ndø-eqá, neka flawa løvusam ezoqam-qa kawa ndø-eqá.

21 Yaq Felo waen etoam ezoqam, nqawa teqa sasae-te ndøvé. Yaq ndego nqawa kap, Felo-qa zenda-te vø̄ve.

22 Geté flawa løvusam ezoqam, Felo tae-té genø-tøkéw. Matev āv tønefakhán, av Zosef venanqei-qa manqa mokho gezø-ein ndægo.

23 Geté waen etoam ezoqam, Zosef matavapøteáv. Teqa yaq-te, manqa mate mø̀ndømbøe-navøém.

Genesis 40 (gen040), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 40 (23 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen040.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen040.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. Our Genesis 37 and 39 (Felo, gaman ezoqam, vos keoge ezoqam-ge kawa, ndimbula; the dream formulas of 37:5–10), the team's 4:5–6 (the fallen face), 1:4 (saw that it was good), 21:8 (the feast), 29:32 (taqa manqa mokho), and the NT: LUK 23:42 (remember me), MRK 6:21 (Herod's birthday banquet), MRK 6:27 (beheading), ACT 10:39 (hanged on a tree), ACT 25:10 (I have done nothing wrong), JHN 6:13 (baskets), LUK 22:18 / REV 14:18 (vine, grapes). Heading after the BSB's "The Cupbearer and the Baker", without tone marks.

Certainty: high = the construction is attested as used; medium = attested parts put together; low = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked (built).

New name forms

None. Reused: Zosef, Felo, Izip, Iblu ezoqam (14:13), Mbumbukiam. No name differs between the Hebrew and the BSB in this chapter. No YHWH in this chapter.

Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

  • cupbearer (waen etoam ezoqam) and baker (flawa løvusam ezoqam): agent nouns, built (term:cupbearer.g, term:baker.g). "Chief" as kandambaqape (ACT 5:17), given once (v2).
  • interpret / interpretation: gap:interpret_dream.g applied ("taqa manqa mokho", nømand); still open.
  • squeeze (vø̄pesegim): built from the winepress noun (term:squeeze.g).
  • budded, blossoms, ripened: put together from HEB 9:4, JAS 1:10, MAT 9:37.
  • white bread (flawa papaqa), the top basket (ma-clause), kidnapped (gi-aqonømem), "put me in the pit" (ginø-nqeitan).
  • Built verb forms: gezømbe-bevap, ndozote-keoge, qati-tamat, zonømbé-einīm, ndø̀qa-vé, soqo-vé, toqoyage, ndø̀nø-itú, ndø̀qanø-tøkéw, genø-tøkéw, gigonem with manqa-zapa.

Style check

style.py --brief first flagged kandambaqape (10 for about 1), kawa (12 for about 3), noqa, mokho, taqa, ezoqam, khoev -te, zenda -te (more) and manqat, matev (fewer). Revised: the chief title only in v2 (later just "the cupbearer", "the baker"); the repeated "the king of Egypt" in v1 folded into "their master" and v5's into Felo-ge; "in prison" as ndimbula-te (ACT 4:3) in v3, v14 and v5 without khoev; "my dream" without noqa in v9, v16; v17 without the extra mokho-te; qo kawa / qo monqo as 3:15 in v13, v19; v22 with the team's "Matev āv tønefakhán" (adds matev). Second run: left as flags zenda -te, kawa, mokho, taqa, tae, noqa, manqa, ezoqam: the chapter's key words (hand ×5, head ×6, interpretation ×6, the vine, the agent nouns). Forms found nowhere: tam and ndøtam (Genesis only, which the check leaves out) and the built forms above.

Open questions

  1. Cupbearer and baker: is there a Kuni word for a king's wine servant or a baker, or are the descriptive "wine-giving man" and "bread-baking man" right?
  2. Interpret a dream (v5, 8, 12, 16, 18, 22): "taqa manqa mokho" (its meaning) with nømand (explain): right, or is there a word for reading a dream? (gap:interpret_dream.g)
  3. v13, v20: the Hebrew play on "lift up the head" (restore) and "lift off the head" (behead, v19) is kept with kawa … eqá (LUK 21:28). Does "Felo qo kawa ndø̀qa-eqá" read as "Pharaoh will restore you"?
  4. v15: "I was kidnapped" (gi-aqonømem) and "they put me in the pit" (ginø-nqeitan): please check the object marking.
  5. v11: "squeezed" (vø̄pesegim) is built from the winepress noun.
  6. v8: the rhetorical "Don't interpretations belong to God?" is built ("Gê, …, Mbumbukiam-qâ mbà?").
  7. Least sure: v8, v13, v15, v17, v19.
