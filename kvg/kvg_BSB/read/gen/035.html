Iqandengaz (Genesis), chapter 35

Zekop nango Betel-te genøwav

1 Yaq Mbumbukiam Zekop gembo-ein, “Mòqo-itán. Betel-té qonǿqáv, neka ndǿgo, até vøgó. Ndǿgo lou-qase vø̀khakheinám, Mbumbukiam-te, ndego qo-te gefakhan, qo qaveqase Isao-te qoqo-khokhon ndøgo.”

2 Yaq Zekop, ekeza ezoqam neka ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, te namba gi꞉yagam, āv gezømbe-eín nqǽgo, “Manqei vini-ak-gí mbumbuk, zo mokho-te ndøgo, vø̀-ogiím. Neka Mbumbukiam-qa bøi-te, vø̀-eqeieqei-éz. Neka zoqa ndabua ndakinak zó-uzám. 3 Yaq ndakin, Betel-té qazinǿ-niáv. Yaq ndǿgo, no lou-qase ndæ̀khakheinám, Mbumbukiam-te. Ndego, ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, no soqaqape-te qægoam, noqa akhayam ge-ewag. Neka ndego no namba ndøgoám, okho ate ndægo, no qæmat.”

4 Yaq ndigu, manqei vini-ak-gí mbumbuk ate ndi꞉gu, tiqa zenda-te gi꞉goam, neka agiwa-qasi, tiqa gea-te gi꞉goam, Zekop sasa mbo-etøomem. Yaq Zekop, ndigu gigiap, tae kandambaqape guzuguzu-te, Sekem taon-te ndøgo, gèkhonáb. Manqei-té gemø-otáz.

5 Yaq ndigu Sekem taon ndø-ivøvem, vø̄zav. Yaq taon ezoqam, ti avønin-te gi꞉yagam, Mbumbukiam-qá møe kandambá gigonem. Yaq ndigu Zekop-gi nakhei angana zøpavateáv.

6 Yaq Zekop, neka ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, te namba gi꞉yagam, Luz taon-te gimøfakhaz. Iz nøme nqambógo, Betél. Ndøgo Keinan manqei-té. 7 Yaq ndǿgo, ndego lou-qase tenø-khakheinám ndǿgo, ta manqei ndøgo, iz El-Betel mbo-akhá. (Taqa manqa mokho: Betel-qa Mbumbukiam.) Zapa ndǿgo, ndǿgo Mbumbukiam mbofakhán, ndego eveqase-te gekhokhon ndøgo.

8 Yaq Debola gunaním. Ndugu sævam, ma Lebeka gumbi꞉-kewagam. Yaq Betel taon avønin-te, tae kandambaqape guzuguzu-te, ginø-otøvém. Yaq Zekop nqanek tae, iz Alon-Bakut mbo-akhá. (Taqa manqa mokho: Eiv matev-qa tae.)

9 Zekop Padan-Alam manqei-te gendoqavan ndøgo, Mbumbukiam nangó mbofakhán, neka zenda vømbø̄eve. 10 Yaq Mbumbukiam gembo-ein, “Qoqa iz, Zekóp. Geté qoqa iz, Zekop sekemba gégoák. Geté Izlael qágoát.” Yaq ndego Zekop, iz Izlael mbo-akhá.

11 Mbumbukiam nangó āv gembøe-eín nqǽgo, “No Mbumbukiam ndégo, Bazaføgakh-Qape. Vø̀mønøzumít, neka bawan bèzø-khouwevtét. Qogi qazi, ezoqa bawan kandambaqape ndǿgoát, neka ezoqa bawabawan kopoav ndǿgoát. Neka ti mokho-te ndigu, ezoqa nøme kawakawa-za vǿgoāt. 12 Manqei ndøgo, no Eibleem neka Aezek qazø-etoam, no ndakin, qo neka qogi qazi, ndigu qoqa zita-te tiqanet, manqei ndøgo ndæ̀zø-etoám.”

13 Taqa zita-te, Mbumbukiam, manqei-te ndøgo, ma ndego te namba manqat ge꞉manqatam, Zekop sège-iváv, genøqavíg.

14 Yaq Zekop, manqei-te ndøgo, ma Mbumbukiam te namba manqat ge꞉manqatam, nandi sasa ndøwuab. Andé uk me me꞉wuáb. Neka Mbumbukiam-te etoam matev, waen nandi-te vō꞉qouz. Neka oil vō꞉qouz, nandi-te. 15 Yaq Zekop ta manqei ndøgo, ma Mbumbukiam te namba ndǿgo manqat ge꞉manqatam, iz Betel mbo-akhá. (Taqa manqa mokho: Mbumbukiam-qá khoév.)

Benzamin geqan, Laisol gunanim

16 Yaq ndigu Betel ndø-ivøvem, vø̄zav. Ndigu Eflat vemiav-te fakhaneav ndøgoám. Zua qambaqapé. Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, Laisol gui-ovaɨn. Tuqa nqosøgeap kandambá tantáv. 17 Ate av-té, tuqa nqosøgeap kandambá ndøgoám, sævam, ndugu Laisol gumbotøke, gumbo-ein, “Møe ndø̀go᷄. Ngenek nakhasam anganeam nøme.” 18 Ate av-té, Laisol gunanimtát, ndugu nakhasam, iz Ben-Oni mbo-akhá. (Taqa manqa mokho: Noqa nqosøgeap-ge yó.) Geté tege eve, iz Benzamin mbo-akhá. (Taqa manqa mokho: Noqa nakeamo zenda-ge yó.)

19 Yaq Laisol gunaním. Yaq Eflat vemiav-qa nakhoa-te, ginø-otøvém. Iz nøme nqambógo, Betliém. 20 Yaq Zekop, tuqa manqei pakha-te, nandi sasa ndøwuab. Andé uk me me꞉wuáb. Yaq nakémbá, até ndakin khøuwa-té-a, nqanek nandi, Laisol-qa manqei pakha-te me꞉yám.

Zekop-gi nakhei angana

21 Yaq Izlael ge-umian. Yaq Eda-qa khoev eqawat-qape yaq-keoqa, palai khoev vø̄puan. 22 Izlael ndǿgo geyagam ndøgo, Luben genøwáv, tege eve-gu sasae sævam, Bila, namba vø̄esømbi. Yaq Izlael nqanek matev ge-ewag.

Zekop nakhei angana tuelv mø̀ndømbo-goám. 23 Lia-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Luben, ndego Zekop-ge yo-khøuwa, neka Simeon neka Livai neka Zuda neka Isaka neka Zebulon. 24 Laisol-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Zosef neka Benzamin. 25 Laisol-gu sasae mbasønakam Bila, tugi nakhei angana ngínik: Den neka Naftali. 26 Lia-gu sasae mbasønakam Zilpa, tugi nakhei angana ngínik: Gad neka Asa. Nginik Zekop-gi nakhei angana, ndigu Padan-Alam manqei-te giqanat.

Aezek genanim

27 Yaq Zekop, tege eve Aezek-te, gendoqavan, Mamle manqei-te, Kiliat-Aba taon avønin-te. Iz nøme nqambógo, Hiblón. Ta manqei-te ndøgo, Eibleem neka Aezek gi꞉yagam. 28 Aezek viav wan andled eiti geyagam. 29 Yaq Aezek ùnime꞉-lawaqape-év, genanim. Yage outap-ús. Yaq ndego ekeza ezoqam zøpaév, ndigu bugukhokhof go꞉pakhaez. Yaq tege nakhei angana, Isao neka Zekop, ginø-otøvém.

Genesis 35 (gen035), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 35 (29 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen035.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen035.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. Our Genesis 34 and 31–33 drafts (names, gembo-ein frames, headings without tone marks); the team's Genesis 28 (Bethel, the pillar), 17 (God Almighty, name change, kings), 23 and 25 (death, burial, "that is Hebron"), 29–30 (births and name meanings in brackets), 10:4 and 25:13 (lists of sons). Headings follow the BSB's four (v1, v16, v21, v27). Short sentences opened by Yaq; the long frame "āv … nqǽgo" for Jacob's order (v2) and God's solemn speech (v11).

New name forms

Debola (Deborah), Alon-Bakut (Allon-bacuth, meaning given), El-Betel (El-bethel), Ben-Oni (Ben-oni), Eda (Eder: "Eda-qa khoev eqawat-qape", the Tower of Eder). From the corpus: Kiliat-Aba (GEN 23:2), Eflat, Betliem, Mamle, Hiblon, Bila, Zilpa, Den, Gad, Asa, Isaka, Zebulon, Benzamin.

Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

  • midwife (gap): "sævam, ndugu Laisol gumbotøke" (the woman who helped Rachel).
  • below a town (gap): rendered "Betel taon avønin-te" (near Bethel).
  • drink offering: wine (waen) after the NT; the Hebrew names no liquid.
  • earrings: ring (agiwa-qasi) + "on their ears" (gea-te, built phrase).
  • purify yourselves, change your garments: built on 1PE 1:22 and COL 3:12.
  • Built verb forms: vø̀khakheinám, ndæ̀khakheinám, vø̀-eqeieqei-éz, qoqo-khokhon, qo-te gefakhan, gemø-otáz, zøpavateáv, gunanimtát, qazø-etoam (for "I gave them").

Open questions

  1. YHWH: no YHWH in this chapter (only Elohim / El Shaddai = Mbumbukiam, Bazaføgakh-Qape).
  2. Drink offering as waen: acceptable, or a neutral "ibøkha" / no liquid?
  3. A Kuni word for a midwife, and for "below / downhill from" a town?
  4. "Be fruitful and multiply" (v11) is singular in Hebrew; the team's form vø̀mønøzumít is plural (to Noah and his sons). Keep, or a singular form?
  5. Least sure: v2 (purify, change clothes), v12 (qazø-etoam), v17 (midwife and "another son"), v18.

(Taqa manqa mokho: Betel-qa Mbumbukiam.) Zapa ndǿgo, ndǿgo Mbumbukiam mbofakhán,
ndego eveqase-te gekhokhon ndøgo.
8 Yaq Debola gunaním. Ndugu sævam, ma Lebeka gumbi꞉-kewagam.
Yaq Betel taon avønin-te, tae kandambaqape guzuguzu-te, ginø-otøvém.
Yaq Zekop nqanek tae, iz Alon-Bakut mbo-akhá. (Taqa manqa mokho: Eiv matev-qa tae.)
9 Zekop Padan-Alam manqei-te gendoqavan ndøgo, Mbumbukiam nangó mbofakhán,
neka zenda vømbø̄eve.
10 Yaq Mbumbukiam gembo-ein,
“Qoqa iz, Zekóp.
Geté qoqa iz, Zekop sekemba gégoák.
Geté Izlael qágoát.”
Yaq ndego Zekop, iz Izlael mbo-akhá.
11 Mbumbukiam nangó āv gembøe-eín nqǽgo,
“No Mbumbukiam ndégo, Bazaføgakh-Qape.
Vø̀mønøzumít,
neka bawan bèzø-khouwevtét.
Qogi qazi, ezoqa bawan kandambaqape ndǿgoát, neka ezoqa bawabawan kopoav ndǿgoát.
Neka ti mokho-te ndigu, ezoqa nøme kawakawa-za vǿgoāt.
12 Manqei ndøgo,
no Eibleem neka Aezek qazø-etoam,
no ndakin,
qo neka qogi qazi, ndigu qoqa zita-te tiqanet, manqei ndøgo ndæ̀zø-etoám.”
13 Taqa zita-te, Mbumbukiam, manqei-te ndøgo,
ma ndego te namba manqat ge꞉manqatam, Zekop sège-iváv, genøqavíg.
14 Yaq Zekop, manqei-te ndøgo,
ma Mbumbukiam te namba manqat ge꞉manqatam,
nandi sasa ndøwuab. Andé uk me me꞉wuáb.
Neka Mbumbukiam-te etoam matev, waen nandi-te vō꞉qouz.
Neka oil vō꞉qouz, nandi-te.
15 Yaq Zekop ta manqei ndøgo,
ma Mbumbukiam te namba ndǿgo manqat ge꞉manqatam, iz Betel mbo-akhá.
(Taqa manqa mokho: Mbumbukiam-qá khoév.)

Benzamin geqan, Laisol gunanim

16 Yaq ndigu Betel ndø-ivøvem, vø̄zav.
Ndigu Eflat vemiav-te fakhaneav ndøgoám. Zua qambaqapé.
Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo,
Laisol gui-ovaɨn.
Tuqa nqosøgeap kandambá tantáv.
17 Ate av-té,
tuqa nqosøgeap kandambá ndøgoám,
sævam,
ndugu Laisol gumbotøke, gumbo-ein,
“Møe ndø̀go᷄.
Ngenek nakhasam anganeam nøme.”
18 Ate
av-té,
Laisol gunanimtát,
ndugu nakhasam, iz Ben-Oni mbo-akhá.
(Taqa manqa mokho: Noqa nqosøgeap-ge yó.) Geté tege eve, iz Benzamin mbo-akhá. (Taqa manqa mokho: Noqa nakeamo zenda-ge yó.)
19 Yaq Laisol gunaním.
Yaq Eflat vemiav-qa nakhoa-te, ginø-otøvém.
Iz nøme nqambógo, Betliém.
20 Yaq Zekop, tuqa manqei pakha-te, nandi sasa ndøwuab. Andé uk me me꞉wuáb.
Yaq nakémbá, até ndakin khøuwa-té-a, nqanek nandi, Laisol-qa manqei pakha-te me꞉yám.

Zekop-gi nakhei angana

21 Yaq Izlael ge-umian.
Yaq Eda-qa khoev eqawat-qape yaq-keoqa, palai khoev vø̄puan.
22 Izlael ndǿgo geyagam ndøgo,
Luben genøwáv,
tege eve-gu sasae sævam, Bila, namba vø̄esømbi.
Yaq Izlael nqanek matev ge-ewag.
Zekop nakhei angana tuelv mø̀ndømbo-goám.
23 Lia-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Luben, ndego Zekop-ge yo-khøuwa, neka Simeon neka Livai neka Zuda neka Isaka neka Zebulon.
24 Laisol-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Zosef neka Benzamin.
25 Laisol-gu sasae mbasønakam Bila, tugi nakhei angana ngínik: Den neka Naftali.
26 Lia-gu sasae mbasønakam Zilpa, tugi nakhei angana ngínik: Gad neka Asa.
Nginik Zekop-gi nakhei angana,
ndigu Padan-Alam manqei-te giqanat.

Aezek genanim

27 Yaq Zekop, tege eve Aezek-te, gendoqavan, Mamle manqei-te, Kiliat-Aba taon avønin-te.
Iz nøme nqambógo, Hiblón.
Ta manqei-te ndøgo, Eibleem neka Aezek gi꞉yagam.
28 Aezek viav wan andled eiti geyagam.
29 Yaq Aezek ùnime꞉-lawaqape-év,
genanim. Yage outap-ús.
Yaq ndego ekeza ezoqam zøpaév,
ndigu bugukhokhof go꞉pakhaez. Yaq tege nakhei angana, Isao neka Zekop, ginø-otøvém.
