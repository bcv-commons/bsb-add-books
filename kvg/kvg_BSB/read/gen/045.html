Iqandengaz (Genesis), chapter 45

Zosef ekeza zapikia-za tenezømas

1 Yaq Zosef sekemba kopømba mbaín, ekeza eiv tenøzøgim, ezoqa ate ndigu, avønin-te giwaniapam. Yaq ndego āv gezømbe-akhá nqǽgo, “Zo ate, bavokhó qazonǿzáv!” Yaq ezoqa nøme mbaín, te namba. Zosef ekeza zapikia-za tènezømás.

2 Yaq ndego gèvín. Eiv gèmbøe-eqavát. Yaq Izip manqei-ak teqa eiv giyogem. Ezoqa ndigu, Felo-qa khoev-te giyagam, ndigu giyogem.

3 Yaq Zosef zapikia-za gezø-ein, “No Zoséf. Gê, nogé tat zua ngekhandî꞉vát?” Geté tegi zapikia-za kopømba mbaín, manqat vømbøé-qavøinām. Ndigu Zosef-qá møe kandambá gigonem.

4 Yaq Zosef zapikia-za gezø-ein, “Noqa avønin-te vø̀ndozáv.” Ndigu avønin-te ginduzáv. Yaq ndego gezø-ein, “No Zoséf, zøveqase. Ezoqam nó, zo Izip manqei-té qazonø-wiím.

5 Yaq ndakin, zo no nqanek qazonø-wiím. Geté taqa yaq-te, zo mbøni bezø̀va᷄. Neka zøkeza-te, qaqa zø̀go᷄. Zapa ndǿgo, Mbumbukiam no zoqa megemege-te ndøkhofotáv, ezoqa khandi꞉zat-qa.

6 Nqanek viav menás, vikia seke qamat. Neka viav faev nøme zua ndǿgoát. Ta viav-te ndøgo, khae-te sasae mbaín, neka avøe vingisam mbaín.

7 Mbumbukiam no bugukhokhof ndøkhofotáv. Yaq ndǿgo, zogi qazi, manqei-qape-te, zua bezø̄-yagé. Mbumbukiam zo kandambá khandi꞉zat-qa ndøgó.

8 Yaq nakémbá, matev ambá av nqǣgo, zo no nqanek qazonø-khofotáv. Geté Mbumbukiam ndégo. Ndego no ndøvé, yaq no andé Felo-ge eve me nogó. Felo-qa khoev ate qægoam neka Izip manqei ate qægoam, no kawa ezoqám.

Zosef ekeza eve-te manqat ndøkhofotav

9 Nakhamas-te, nogé tat-te qazonǿzáv. Yaq āv qazømbøé-einím nqǽgo, ‘Qoge yo Zosef āv gene-eín: Mbumbukiam no Izip manqei ate qægoam, kawa ezoqam ndøvé. No-te qotøndéqāv. Nakhamas-té.

10 Yaq qo Gosen manqei-te qóyagét. Qo noqa avønin-te qógoát: qo, neka qogi nakheis neka zi, neka qogi sip neka kao, neka gigiap ate nandaqambe꞉go.

11 No ndǿgo, qo lou søtaqá-etoám. Zapa ndǿgo, viav faev nøme, vikia seke ndǿgoát. Soqaín ndøgo, qo neka qogi ezoqam, khoev-te ndiqayage, até gigiap ate nandaqambe꞉go-a, gigiap-av ezoqam tozogo.’

12 Nqáe! Zo neka noge ata Benzamin, zøkeza bøi-te zøtéqeivīm, av nqægo, ezoqam nó, ndakin manqat nqazø-manqate.

13 Nogé tat, noqa iz eqawat-qa yaq-te, Izip mokho-te, neka matev ate ndægo, zo nqazøte-qeivi, manqat vømømbó-einīm. Yaq nakhamas-te, nogé tat nqanek vøndō-ituim.”

14 Yaq Zosef eveqase Benzamin sège-møvøiú, vø̄vin. Neka até Benzamín-a, ndego namba gèvín.

15 Yaq Zosef zapikia-za ate gi꞉goam, kopokopo vø̄ngenøtam, neka ti-te vø̄vin. Taqa zita-te, tegi zapikia-za, ndego namba manqat gimanqatam.

Felo Zekop Izip manqei-te gembo-akha

16 Yaq Felo-qa khoev-te ndøgo, ezoqa manqat giyogem, av nqægo, Zosef-gi zapikia-za ndøfakház. Yaq Felo neka tegi gaman sasae ezoqam, khanakhanakh gindapem.

17 Yaq Felo Zosef gembo-ein, “Qoge zapikia-za āv qozømbé-eín nqǽgo, ‘Matev nqánek vozomǿ-gonēm: Zogi donki-qa zita-te, lou vø̀-veém. Yaq Keinan manqei-té qazonǿzáv.

18 Zoge zøve neka zogi ezoqam ate, vøndapēm. Neka no-te vø̄ndozáv. Yaq no Izip manqei-qa eqeieqei-qape nøzǿ-etoám. Zo manqei-qa avøe eqeieqei zólogāt.’

19 Neka qo manqat nøme, āv qozømbé-eín nqǽgo, ‘Matev nqánek vozomǿ-gonēm: Izip manqei mokho-te ndøgo, manqei khagua wil-us vøndapēm, zogi nakheis neka zogi sakheis nonqo. Yaq zoge zøve vøndō-ituim, neka nqawa vø̀ndozáv.

20 Zogi gigiap-qa yaq-te, mbøni bezø̀va᷄. Zapa ndǿgo, Izip manqei ate qægoam, taqa eqeieqei-qape zoqá ndǿgoát.’”

21 Yaq Izlael-gi nakhei angana, manqat ndøgo vømbō-paevem. Yaq Zosef manqei khagua wil-us vøzø̄-etoam, av Felo gembøe-ein ndægo. Até okho nonqo lóu-a, vøzø̄-etoam.

22 Ndego ezoqa kopokopo, ndabua ndakinak vøzø̄-etoam. Geté Benzamin, tri andled silva moni mokho vømbō-etoam, neka ndabua ndakinak faev.

23 Neka eve-te, nqanek ndøkhofotáv: donki ten, Izip manqei-qa gigiap eqeieqei ndi-upøgim, neka donki sakheis ten, wit neka flawa neka lou ndi-upøgim, tege eve-qa okho nonqo.

24 Yaq Zosef zapikia-za gekhofotaz, giqavaz. Yaq ndego gezø-ein, “Zo nakhoa-te beponqòmba᷄p.”

Zekop kha vøndæv ndøndap

25 Yaq ndigu Izip manqei sasa ndø-ivøvem. Keinan manqei-te, tige eve Zekop-te, vømø̄fakhaz.

26 Yaq ndigu āv gimbøe-einím nqǽgo, “Zosef zua ngekhandî꞉vát! Neka ndego Izip manqei ate qægoam, kawa ezoqám.” Geté Zekop nqova mbondáf. Ndego tiqa manqat unimanqatinteáv.

27 Geté ndigu Zosef-qa manqat ate ndægo gimbo-einim, av ndego gezø-ein ndægo. Yaq ndego manqei khagua wil-us geqeiv, ndigu Zosef ndøkhofotáv, ndego itu-qa. Yaq tige eve Zekop, kha vøndæv ndøndáp.

28 Yaq Izlael āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ság! Noge yo Zosef zua ngekhandî꞉vát. No qatǿnáv, ndego tæ-omet. Okhokho-té qabatø-naním.”

Genesis 45 (gen045), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 45 (28 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen045.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen045.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. Our Genesis 44 and 43 (Joseph's brothers, nogé tat, zøveqase, Khanánqa, the speech frames, "is he still alive"), the team's Genesis 1 and Genesis 12, 13, 26, 27, 29 (Esau weeping, Laban embracing Jacob, Isaac sending Abimelech away, "before I die"). NT models: ACT 7:13 (Joseph made himself known), ACT 10:21, JHN 9:37, JHN 14:1, 1TI 2:6, 1TI 5:4, LUK 18:22, ACT 8:28, TIT 3:2, LUK 1:12, ACT 12:3, MRK 16:13. Headings after the BSB's four, without tone marks.

Certainty: high = the construction is attested as used; medium = attested parts put together; low = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked (built).

New name forms

  • Gosen (גֹּשֶׁן, Goshen), as Gosen manqei: not in the corpus or the glossary; sh → s by the letter rules (name:goshen.g45). The BSB and the Hebrew agree on the name.
  • Reused: Zosef (Zoséf as a predicate), Benzamin, Felo, Izip, Izip manqei-ak, Keinan, Zekop, Izlael; Mbumbukiam. No YHWH in this chapter.

Fixed renderings for the following chapters

  • wagons manqei khagua wil-us (46:5, 46:29 chariot); Goshen Gosen manqei (Gen 46–47, 50); "before I die" Okhokho-té qabatø-naním.; "embraced" sège-møvøiú (46:29 fell on his neck); "I will provide for you" lou søtaqá-etoám (47:12, 50:21); "still alive" zua ngekhandî꞉vát (46:30).

Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

  • wagon (v19, 21, 27), Goshen (v10), plowing (v6), remnant (v7), destitution (v11), "father to Pharaoh" (v8), "spirit revived" (v27; one model, 1CO 16:18).
  • Built forms: qazonø-wiím, qazonø-khofotáv (2pl past with the 1sg object), bezø̄-yagé, tozogo, nøzǿ-etoám, vø̀-veém, itu-qa; andé Felo-ge eve me nogó.

Style check

style.py --brief first flagged manqei -te (12 for about 2), megemege -te (4 for 1), te (5 for 1), and fewer mokho -te; watch items neka, ate, ekeza, gigiap. Revised: "here" as nqanek without manqei-te (v5, 8, 13), the famine without manqei-te (v6), "sent me ahead" as bugukhokhof (v7), afraid "of Joseph" instead of "before him" (v3), mokho-te for "in Egypt" (v13, 18, 19), fewer te and ekeza (v1, 3, 4, 14, 15, 23, 24), fewer sentence-initial Yaq and Neka. Left: manqei (18 for about 6), because Genesis writes "Izip manqei", "Keinan manqei" and the wagon term has manqei; the human verses it compares with are from the NT, which names places without manqei. Forms found nowhere: gosen (new name), qoyaget (the team's qóyagét, GEN 12:12; Genesis is left out of the style corpus), itu (the built itu-qa).

Open questions

  1. "A father to Pharaoh" (v8): kept as a likeness (andé Felo-ge eve me nogó, I am like Pharaoh's father). Should it be explained ("his adviser")?
  2. Wagons (v19, 21, 27): the chariot word manqei khagua wil-us; does Kuni have a word for a cart?
  3. The built 2pl past with "me" (qazonø-wiím, qazonø-khofotáv, v4, 5, 8): is qazonø- right?
  4. "His spirit revived" (v27): kha vøndæv ndøndáp (he was refreshed) or khanakhanakh ndøndáp (he became glad)?
  5. Remnant and deliverance (v7) and destitution (v11): rendered by meaning, low certainty.
  6. Least sure: v7, v8, v11, v19, v27.
