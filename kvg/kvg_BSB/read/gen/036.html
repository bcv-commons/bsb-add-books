Genesis 36 (gen036), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 36 (43 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen036.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen036.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. The team's own genealogies: Genesis 10 (lists of sons, "X-gi nakhei angana ngínik: A neka B …", "Nginik ezoqam ate ngi꞉gu, X-gí zí"), 25:1–4 and 25:12–16 (Ishmael's line, "Ndøgo āv tøzømbe-peawáp", the princes megetapak), 22:20–24 (Nahor's sons, the concubine), 26:34 (Esau's wives one by one), 14:1–8 (lists of kings), and our Genesis 35 (the list of Jacob's sons). Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

  • hot springs (gap): "ibøkha tatas … ndøgo manqei mokho-té qando-fakhatám" (hot water that came up from the ground), built on GEN 2:6 and REV 3:15.
  • chief (ʾallûp): no exact word; megetapak (leaders, 25:16 princes) used, said once per list.
  • reign as a verb: none; "was king" (HEB 7:1) and "took his place, became king" (ACT 24:27 + vø̄go).
  • before any king reigned: rephrased as "at that time the Israelites had no king" (mbain qagoam).
  • Word forms not seen with this subject: gimbo-fakhaz with a mother as subject (v5, 14; attested with a father, 10:2, and for "she bore them" the team also uses mø̀ndømbo-fakház, 22:20); gezø-kewagám with donkeys (30:36 has flocks).

Open questions

  1. YHWH: not in this chapter (no divine name at all).
  2. Olibama or Oolibama for Oholibamah (the strict letter rule keeps both vowels)?
  3. Shaul (36:37–38) as Sol, the same as King Saul, or a separate form (Saul)?
  4. 36:39 Hebrew Hadar: the draft follows the BSB's Hadad (1 Chr 1:50); 36:26 Hebrew Dishan, the draft Dison with the BSB.
  5. megetapak for the clan chiefs of Edom and the Horites, or a title before each name as the Hebrew has it ("megeat ezoqam Teman, …")?
  6. "hot springs" (36:24): is there a Kuni word for a hot spring?
  7. Least sure: v6 (long list, gekhatob), v12 (Elifaz-te), v24, v31, v40–43.
