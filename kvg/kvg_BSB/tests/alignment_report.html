Kuni alignments with Ruth and Jonah: eflomal, IBM Model 2 and lexeme-aligner (2026-10-14)

CC0; quotations from the Kuni texts credited in ../CREDITS.md. Made with local experiment scripts (eflomal built against Homebrew libomp), not in the repository; they became scripts/align.py, which rebuilds the lexicons (_learn/).

Corpora

  • eng: BSB ↔ Kuni, NT + Ruth + Jonah: 8,065 verse pairs (133 from Ruth and Jonah). Kuni Jonah has the Hebrew verse numbering now and is paired with the BSB through orig/vrs (Hebrew 2:1 = BSB 1:17).
  • hbo: MACULA Hebrew lexemes (hbo:NNNN, the scaffold API, the same ids as lexeme-aligner) ↔ Kuni, Ruth + Jonah only: 133 verse pairs. Small: these figures are rough.

Both models are symmetrised the same way (intersection + grow-diag). Kuni is tokenised with hyphen parts split off and tone marks removed.

The name gold has two wrong gold forms (Yahweh, Rachel), so 28 names really count.

  • eflomal is the better aligner on both gold checks, and it is now the agents' lexicon: _learn/lexicon_eng.tsv (eflomal, NT + Ruth + Jonah) and _learn/lexicon_hbo.tsv (Hebrew lexeme → Kuni, Ruth + Jonah, with lemma and gloss; concord.py hlex). The NT-only lexicons are kept in _learn/nt-only/.
  • Agreement with lexeme-aligner is low on the Hebrew side (about a third). The disagreements are mostly function words, and both sides have errors. lexeme-aligner aligns the whole corpus (Greek and Hebrew lexemes) jointly, and some of its Ruth rows look off: lut → "he said", zo → "person", qo → "covenant loyalty". Ours gives manqei → "earth" where theirs gives "Moab" (from Moab manqei). These rows are worth sending to lexeme-aligner as examples.
  • Names, where the models miss:
    • Amittai: all three give yo (son), from Amitai-ge yo.
    • Joppa (Ziopa): all three miss.
    • Tamar (Tama): all three give zi.
    • Leah: only IBM2 is right (Lia).
    • Perez: lexeme-aligner gives atanakha.
    • Ruth: lexeme-aligner gives moab.

For the general toolkit

align2.py becomes scripts/align.py <project> (docs/proposals/language-toolkit/): eflomal when installed, IBM2 otherwise, a Hebrew-lexeme corpus wherever the edition has OT books, and the glossary and name checks as its built-in report.
