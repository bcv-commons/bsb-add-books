Ibugukhokhof 1 (test draft, genesis-test)

Mbumbukiam yan neka manqei-qape gekhakheinam

1 Ibugukhokhof, Mbumbukiam yan neka manqei-qape gekhakheinam. 2 Manqei-qape unimé꞉-khakhambá. Gigiap nøme mbaín. Yaq anana-mba-qape bøivun ndønqeivøém. Yaq Mbumbukiam-ge Nqova, ibøkha ová, me꞉løvuvavún.

3 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Waev begó.” Yaq waev mø̀ndøgoám. 4 Yaq Mbumbukiam geqeiv av nqægo, waev ndøgo eqeieqeí. Yaq ndego waev neka bøivun geqatøzo. 5 Yaq Mbumbukiam waev, iz Khøuwa mbo-akhá, neka bøivun, iz Lova mbo-akhá. Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Ndøgo bugukhokhof khøuwá.

6 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ibøkha livin-te, khaiya begó. Yaq ndøgo ibøkha beqatøzo, nøme ova, neka nøme mokho-te.” 7 Yaq Mbumbukiam khaiya gekhakheinam. Yaq ndego ibøkha geqatøzo. Nøme khaiya mokho-te ndøgo, neka nøme khaiya ová ndøgo. Yaq ate av-té qafakhan. 8 Yaq Mbumbukiam khaiya, iz Yan mbo-akhá. Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-menas-éz.

9 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ibøkha, yan mokho-te ndøgo, manqei kopo-té bepindám. Yaq manqei-qape befakhan.” Yaq ate av-té qafakhan. 10 Yaq Mbumbukiam manqei-qape, iz Manqei mbo-akhá, neka ibøkha gipindam ndøgo, iz Ndaola mbo-akhá. Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí.

11 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Manqei-te gagayag bete-umianét: avøe gigiap, nanga-us ndigu, neka tae bugug vinivinimbá, nanga ndǿviam. Ta nanga mokho-te ndøugu, tae nanga.” Yaq ate av-té qafakhan. 12 Yaq manqei-te, gagayag gi-umianat: avøe gigiap, nanga-us ndigu, bugug vinivinimbá, neka tae, nanga ndǿviam, ta nanga mokho-te tae nanga ndøugu, bugug vinivinimbá. Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí. 13 Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-misika-éz.

Mbumbukiam khøuwa neka løvøyak neka nduku gekhakheinam

14 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Waev kopoav khaiya-te begó. Ndigu khøuwa neka lova beqatøzo. Neka ndigu umingiap begó, pindap khøuwa-qa neka khøuwa-qa neka viav-qa. 15 Yaq ndigu waev begó, khaiya-te, neka manqei-qape-te waev betekháev.” Yaq ate av-té qafakhan. 16 Yaq Mbumbukiam waev kandakanda menas gekhakheinam. Waev kandambaqape, ndøgo khøuwá, khøuwa-qá kawá. Neka waev khasøkhasis, ndøgo løvøyák, lova-qá kawá. Neka até nduku-a gekhakheinam. 17 Yaq Mbumbukiam ndigu khaiya-te genøvé. Yaq manqei-qape-te waev betekháev, 18 neka khøuwa neka lova-qá kawá begó, neka waev neka bøivun beqatøzo. Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí. 19 Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Khøuwa mø̀ndømbo-foa-éz.

20 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ibøkha-te, khandi gigiap kopoav-qape begó. Neka pipisi manqei ová bete-løvuvat, khaiya-te.” 21 Yaq Mbumbukiam søvakha gigiap kandakanda gekhakheinam. Ndigu ibøkha-te ndi-okho. Neka ndego khandi gigiap ate ndægo gekhakheinam, bugug vinivinimbá. Ndigu ibøkha-te kopoav-qape ndi-okho. Neka pipisi ate ndægo, ndiløvuvam, bugug vinivinimbá, gekhakheinam. Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí. 22 Yaq Mbumbukiam zenda gezømbove, neka āv gezømbe-eín nqǽgo, “Zo kopoav zógoát. Ndaola-te, ibøkha ate qægoam, zo zóyagát. Neka pipisi, manqei-qape-te, kopoav begó.” 23 Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-faev-éz.

Mbumbukiam søvakha gigiap neka ezoqam gekhakheinam

24 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Manqei-te, khandi gigiap bugug vinivinimbá befakhan: søvakha gigiap nanák, neka gigiap ndivisigiap, neka søvakha gigiap lozokhák, bugug vinivinimbá.” Yaq ate av-té qafakhan. 25 Yaq Mbumbukiam søvakha gigiap lozokhak gekhakheinam, bugug vinivinimbá. Neka ndego søvakha gigiap nanak, bugug vinivinimbá, gekhakheinam. Neka gigiap ate ndægo, manqei-qape-te ndivisigiap, bugug vinivinimbá, gekhakheinam. Yaq ndego geqeiv av nqægo, ndøgo eqeieqeí.

26 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ezoqam zíkhakheinám, andé niqa bugug me. Yaq ndigu kawa zømbógoát: zonga, ndaola-te, neka pipisi, khaiya-te, neka søvakha gigiap nanak, neka manqei-qape-te ate nqægo, neka gigiap ate ndægo, manqei-qape-te ndivisigiap.”

//1 27 Yaq Mbumbukiam ezoqam gekhakheinam, andé ekeza bugug me.

//2 Andé Mbumbukiam-qa bugug me, ezoqam gekhakheinam.

//2 Anganeam neká sævam, tèkhakheinøvém.

28 Yaq Mbumbukiam zenda gezømbove, neka āv gezømbe-eín nqǽgo, “Zo kopoav zógoát. Manqei-qape-te ate nqægo, zo zóyagát. Ndøgo, zoqá zenda-té qatǿvé. Zo zonga, ndaola-te, neka pipisi, khaiya-te, neka khandi gigiap ate ndægo, manqei-qape-te ndivisigiap, kawa zømbógoát.” 29 Yaq Mbumbukiam gezø-ein, “Nqáe! No zo avøe gigiap ate ndægo, nanga-us ndigu, manqei-qape-te ate nqægo, neka tae ate ndægo, ndigu nanga ndǿviam, nanga mokho-te tae nanga-us, mø̀ezø-etoám. Ndigu zogí lou ndǿgoát. 30 Neka søvakha gigiap lozokhak ate ndægo, neka pipisi ate ndægo, khaiya-te, neka gigiap ate ndægo, manqei-qape-te ndivisigiap, khandi-us ndigu, no gagayag ate ndægo, lou mø̀ezø-etoám.” Yaq ate av-té qafakhan. 31 Yaq gigiap ate ndægo, Mbumbukiam gekhakheinam, ndego geqeiv. Áe, ndøgo eqeieqei tantáv. Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-siks-éz.

1 Ibugukhokhof, Mbumbukiam yan neka manqei-qape gekhakheinam.
בְּרֵאשִׁ֖ית בָּרָ֣א אֱלֹהִ֑ים אֵ֥ת הַשָּׁמַ֖יִם וְאֵ֥ת הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 1:1 In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.
Ibugukhokhof as JHN 1:1; 'heavens and earth' as ACT 4:24. Models JHN 1:1, ACT 4:24, JON 1:9. *High.*
2 Manqei-qape unimé꞉-khakhambá. Gigiap nøme mbaín. Yaq anana-mba-qape bøivun ndønqeivøém. Yaq Mbumbukiam-ge Nqova, ibøkha ová, me꞉løvuvavún.
וְהָאָ֗רֶץ הָיְתָ֥ה תֹ֨הוּ֙ וָבֹ֔הוּ וְחֹ֖שֶׁךְ עַל־פְּנֵ֣י תְה֑וֹם וְר֣וּחַ אֱלֹהִ֔ים מְרַחֶ֖פֶת עַל־פְּנֵ֥י הַמָּֽיִם׃
BSB 1:2 Now the earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the surface of the waters.
'empty' from ACT 1:20 (deserted house); 'darkness covered' LUK 23:44; the deep = anana-mba-qape (JON 2:4); hovering = me꞉løvuvavún (MRK 1:10, the Spirit coming down like a dove; whether it can mean 'hover': unsure). Spirit of God = Mbumbukiam-ge Nqova (ROM 8:14). *Medium / low (hovering).*
3 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Waev begó.” Yaq waev mø̀ndøgoám.
וַיֹּ֥אמֶר אֱלֹהִ֖ים יְהִ֣י א֑וֹר וַֽיְהִי־אֽוֹר׃
BSB 1:3 And God said, “Let there be light,” and there was light.
2CO 4:6 quotes this as "Waev bekhā"; begó HEB 9:23; existence mø̀ndøgoám (store). *High.*
4 Yaq Mbumbukiam geqeiv av nqægo, waev ndøgo eqeieqeí. Yaq ndego waev neka bøivun geqatøzo.
וַיַּ֧רְא אֱלֹהִ֛ים אֶת־הָא֖וֹר כִּי־ט֑וֹב וַיַּבְדֵּ֣ל אֱלֹהִ֔ים בֵּ֥ין הָא֖וֹר וּבֵ֥ין הַחֹֽשֶׁךְ׃
BSB 1:4 And God saw that the light was good, and He separated the light from the darkness.
qatøzo separate (MAT 25:32, MRK 10:9). JON 3:10 for 'saw that'. *High.*
5 Yaq Mbumbukiam waev, iz Khøuwa mbo-akhá, neka bøivun, iz Lova mbo-akhá. Yaq qakhagus ndøgo, qanaqanus qandopave. Ndøgo bugukhokhof khøuwá.
וַיִּקְרָ֨א אֱלֹהִ֤ים׀לָאוֹר֙ י֔וֹם וְלַחֹ֖שֶׁךְ קָ֣רָא לָ֑יְלָה וַֽיְהִי־עֶ֥רֶב וַֽיְהִי־בֹ֖קֶר י֥וֹם אֶחָֽד׃פ
BSB 1:5 God called the light “day,” and the darkness He called “night.” And there was evening, and there was morning — the first day.
naming MAT 1:25; evening/morning MAT 14:15, ACT 27:39; 'first' REV 8:7; predicate noun LUK 23:54. *Medium.*
6 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ibøkha livin-te, khaiya begó. Yaq ndøgo ibøkha beqatøzo, nøme ova, neka nøme mokho-te.”
וַיֹּ֣אמֶר אֱלֹהִ֔ים יְהִ֥י רָקִ֖יעַ בְּת֣וֹךְ הַמָּ֑יִם וִיהִ֣י מַבְדִּ֔יל בֵּ֥ין מַ֖יִם לָמָֽיִם׃
BSB 1:6 And God said, “Let there be an expanse between the waters, to separate the waters from the waters.”
expanse = khaiya (the visible sky, HEB 11:12, MAT 16:2); midst = livin-te (ACT 12:6); 'some ... some' (nøme ... nøme) ACT 10:12. *Medium.*

BSB 1:10 God called the dry land “earth,” and the gathering of waters He called “seas.” And God saw that it was good.
earth = manqei (store term:H776), sea = ndaola (JON 1:9). *Medium.*

וַיֹּ֣אמֶר אֱלֹהִ֗ים תַּֽדְשֵׁ֤א הָאָ֨רֶץ֙ דֶּ֔שֶׁא עֵ֚שֶׂב מַזְרִ֣יעַ זֶ֔רַע עֵ֣ץ פְּרִ֞י עֹ֤שֶׂה פְּרִי֙ לְמִינ֔וֹ אֲשֶׁ֥ר זַרְעוֹ־ב֖וֹ עַל־הָאָ֑רֶץ וַֽיְהִי־כֵֽן׃
BSB 1:11 Then God said, “Let the earth bring forth vegetation: seed-bearing plants and fruit trees, each bearing fruit with seed according to its kind.” And it was so.

BSB 1:13 And there was evening, and there was morning — the third day.
LUK 24:21 exactly. *High.*
14 Yaq Mbumbukiam āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Waev kopoav khaiya-te begó. Ndigu khøuwa neka lova beqatøzo. Neka ndigu umingiap begó, pindap khøuwa-qa neka khøuwa-qa neka viav-qa.

umingiap (sign); seasons (מוֹעֲדִים) as pindap khøuwa (feast days, JHN 5:1); 'times and seasons' in ACT 1:7 is khøuwa neka viav. *Low.*
15 Yaq ndigu waev begó, khaiya-te, neka manqei-qape-te waev betekháev.” Yaq ate av-té qafakhan.
וְהָי֤וּ לִמְאוֹרֹת֙ בִּרְקִ֣יעַ הַשָּׁמַ֔יִם לְהָאִ֖יר עַל־הָאָ֑רֶץ וַֽיְהִי־כֵֽן׃
BSB 1:15 And let them serve as lights in the expanse of the sky to shine upon the earth.” And it was so.
betekháev MAT 5:16. *High.*
16 Yaq Mbumbukiam waev kandakanda menas gekhakheinam. Waev kandambaqape, ndøgo khøuwá, khøuwa-qá kawá. Neka waev khasøkhasis, ndøgo løvøyák, lova-qá kawá. Neka até nduku-a gekhakheinam.
וַיַּ֣עַשׂ אֱלֹהִ֔ים אֶת־שְׁנֵ֥י הַמְּאֹרֹ֖ת הַגְּדֹלִ֑ים אֶת־הַמָּא֤וֹר הַגָּדֹל֙ לְמֶמְשֶׁ֣לֶת הַיּ֔וֹם וְאֶת־הַמָּא֤וֹר הַקָּטֹן֙ לְמֶמְשֶׁ֣לֶת הַלַּ֔יְלָה וְאֵ֖ת הַכּוֹכָבִֽים׃
BSB 1:16 God made two great lights: the greater light to rule the day and the lesser light to rule the night. And He made the stars as well.
*Explicitation added:* the sun and moon are named (the Hebrew avoids the names); sun, moon, stars as LUK 21:25. 'rule' as predicate noun kawa (chief). khasøkhasis (small) is usually plural (JON 3:5): check. 'as well' até ...-a (store). *Low / medium.*
17 Yaq Mbumbukiam ndigu khaiya-te genøvé. Yaq manqei-qape-te waev betekháev,
וַיִּתֵּ֥ן אֹתָ֛ם אֱלֹהִ֖ים בִּרְקִ֣יעַ הַשָּׁמָ֑יִם לְהָאִ֖יר עַל־הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 1:17 God set these lights in the expanse of the sky to shine upon the earth,

Genesis 1 (gen001), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 1 in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen001.txt, BHSA clause lines) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni NT, Ruth and Jonah (eBible kvg, 2015, and the newer NT working copy), the store and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Tagged source: gen001.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model: the human Ruth 1, Ruth 2 and Jonah 1 (no earlier Genesis chapter exists). Short sentences opened by Yaq, verb last, the long frame āv gene-eín nqǽgo for God's solemn speech, Áe for narrative 'behold'. Headings carry no tone marks.

Words and forms not found in the corpus

  • zíkhakheinám (let us make, v26): hortative zí- + khakhein, by the pattern of JON 1:7.
  • bepindám (let them gather, v9): pind- is only used of people; the waters are treated as gathering like a crowd.
  • bete-løvuvat (let them fly, v20): optative bete- (MAT 13:30, MAT 5:16) + løvuv- (fly).
  • mø̀ndømø-menas-éz, mø̀ndømø-siks-éz (second, sixth day): by the pattern of mø̀ndømø-misika-éz (LUK 24:21) and mø̀ndømø-faev-éz (LUK 19:18).
  • nanga-us, khandi-us (with seeds, with life): -us 'with, having' as in anganeam-ús (RUT 1:21).
  • Constructions not found: a verb for 'swarm' (v20), 'multiply' (v22, v28), 'subdue' (v28), 'rule' said of the sun and moon (v16, v18). Each is recast; see the store gaps gap:fruitful_multiply.h, gap:rule_over.h, gap:waters_gather.h, gap:birds_fly.h, gap:let_us_make.h.

Additions for the team (feature explicitation)

  • v16: the sun and the moon are named ("ndøgo khøuwá", "ndøgo løvøyák"). The Hebrew deliberately says only 'the greater light' and 'the lesser light'.
  • v28: 'subdue it' is rendered as a grant ("I put it into your hands").
  • v22, v28: 'fill the earth / the waters' as 'live all over the earth / in all the water'.

New name forms

No personal names in this chapter. The book title is new (below). God = Mbumbukiam throughout; the chapter has no YHWH.

Open questions

  1. Book title: proposed Ibugukhokhof (In the beginning, after JHN 1:1, the book's first word), in the style of the NT's descriptive titles. Store name:genesis_title [open].
  2. Expanse / sky: khaiya for the expanse, which God names Yan; is the distinction between khaiya (visible sky) and yan (heaven) right for the Kuni reader?
  3. Hovering (v2): me꞉løvuvavún is the Spirit coming down like a dove (MRK 1:10). Does it allow 'hovering over the water'?
  4. Sun and moon named in v16, and 'rule' as kawa (chief): acceptable?
  5. Be fruitful and multiply: 'Zo kopoav zógoát' (become many) is built; is there a Kuni idiom?
  6. Seasons (מוֹעֲדִים, v14) as pindap khøuwa (feast days), or khøuwa neka viav as in ACT 1:7?
  7. Plural of majesty in v26 (niqa, our): the team may prefer a reading.
