Lut 1 (test draft, ruth-test)

Neomi tuni-ngæmam-ev

1 Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, manqa ovøyam megetapak Izlael ezoqam gizømbo-megetam, vikia seke ndøfakhán, ta manqei-te ndøgo. Yaq ezoqa kopo, Betliem vemiav-ák, Zuda manqei-te ndøgo, ndego tegu zas neka tegi nakhei angana menas namba, Moab manqei-té genøwáv, ndǿgo yage-qa. 2 Ngenek ezoqam-qa iz, Elimelék. Tegu zas, tuqa iz Neomí. Neka tegi nakhei angana menas, tiqa iziz Malon neka Kilión. Ndigu Eflata bawan-ák, Betliem vemiav-ák, Zuda manqei-te ndøgo. Yaq ndigu Moab manqei-té ginøzáv, ndǿgo vø̄qom. Ta manqei-te ndøgo, ndigu mo-manqei-aním mbá. 3 Yaq Elimelek, Neomi-ge zivis, genanim. Yaq Neomi tùni-ngæmam-év. Ndugu tugi nakhei angana menas namba mba gugoám. 4 Yaq nginik nakhei angana menas, nøme Moab manqei-ak sævam ndø-okɨ́, tuqa iz Opá. Neka nøme Moab manqei-ak sævam ndø-okɨ́, tuqa iz Lút. Yaq ndigu ndǿgo tiyagám, viav ande āv ten ndi꞉gú. 5 Yaq até Malon neka Kilión-a, ndigu ezoqa menas, vø̄pakhaez. Yaq Neomi ndugu, ekeza ezoqam mbaín. Zivis mø̀ndønaním, neka tugi nakhei angana menas mø̀ndø-pakhaéz.

Lut tugu zakhapuka Neomi gumbo-paev

6 Yaq Neomi Moab manqei-te gu꞉goam, manqat gu-ewag, av nqægo, Evezøza tegi ezoqam-te tøke-qa ndowāv, lou vøzø̄-etoam. Yaq ndugu tugi ziwap menas namba, até gundigu, Moab manqei ivav-qa gugoám, Zuda manqei-te nqawa veseze-qa. 7 Yaq ndugu ta manqei ndøgo, ma guyagam, gè-iváv, tugi ziwap menas namba. Yaq ndigu nakhoa-té ginøzáv, Zuda manqei-te nqawa veseze-qa. 8 Yaq Neomi tugi ziwap menas, āv guzømbe-eín nqǽgo, “Zo nango tozovesez. Zo ezoqa kopokopo, ekeza evo-qá khoev-té sozó-okhoát. Evezøza tanakh matev bezøgó, zo-te, av zo tanakh matev qazo-matønumam, ti-te ndigu gipakhaez, neka no-te. 9 Evezøza zo pusup bezø-etoám, zo ezoqa kopokopo, ekeza zivis-qá khoev-te.” Yaq ndugu guzø-ngenøtam. Yaq ndigu kandambaqapé gè-eivám. 10 Geté ndigu gimbo-einim, “Āv taoká. Ni qo namba, qogi bawan ezoqam-té qazinǿ-qaván.” 11 Geté Neomi guzø-qavøiu, “Nogi nakhei mbasi! Nango tozovesez. Gekha zapâ, zo no namba tozo-apat? Gê, noqa emu mokho-te, nakhei angana zua ndøgô, zogi zivis tigoat? 12 Nogi nakhei mbasi! Nango tozovesez. Sozó-okhoát. Zapa ndǿgo, no ngæpuká. Zivis nango tændap, kopømba mbaín. Oskiá ndøgo, no āv tæ-ein nqægo, ‘No vintønat zua nogó,’ neka nqægo lova zivis tændap, neka nakhei angana tæ-ova, 13 gê, zo ndigu zozø-kewagāt, atema vømǿ-khouwēv? Gê, zo ti zapaya, zivis nøme ndapeav zógoāt? Āv taoká, nogi nakhei mbasi. Noqa mbøni nqosøgeap, zoqa mbøni nqosøgeap mø̀ndøløvú. Zapa ndǿgo, Evezøza yaq-fia ndøtá, no-te.” 14 Yaq ndigu nqawa kandambaqapé gè-eivám. Yaq Opa tugu zakhapuka sasa ndøngenøtam. Geté Lut ndugu, tu-te gùmu-tøkenøvém. 15 Yaq Neomi Lut gumbo-ein, “Nqáe! Opa, qogu ezoqam, tugi bawan ezoqam-te neka tugi mbumbuk-te, nango gunøvesez. Até qó-a, qogu ezoqam-qa zita-te, nango qanøqáv.” 16 Geté Lut gundu-qavøiu,

18 Neomi mø̀ndæ-otév, av nqægo, Lut matavap āv gunigō nqǽgo: Tu namba gunǿwáv. Yaq kopømba mbaín, tuqa matavap toqo-enend. Yaq ndugu Lut sekemba pouweáv.

Neomi neka Lut Betliem vemiav-te giqavaz

19 Yaq ndigu ezoqa menas ginøzáv, atema Betliem vemiav-te vømø̄-fakhaz. Yaq Betliem vemiav-te gimøfakhaz, vemiav-ak ezoqa ewaqape, tiqa yaq-te nqova zøndáf. Yaq vemiav-ak sakheis gi-einim, “Gê, ngunuk Neomî?” 20 Yaq ndugu guzø-qavøiu, “Zo no Neomi nø-akhàya᷄m. Noqa iz Mala ndǿgoát. Zapa ndǿgo, Mbumbukiam Bazaføgakh-qape, matev khaqain-qape gego, no-te. 21 No qæmat, nogi ezoqam namba. Geté Evezøza no khakha-mbá nqawa gendo-itú. Gekha zapâ, no Neomi ndozonø-akhayam? Evezøza noqa yaq-te manqa ge-ein, neka Mbumbukiam Bazaføgakh-qape, yage føgakh-qape gego, no-te.” 22 Yaq Neomi Moab manqei-te gunduqavan, tugu ziwap Lut namba, ndugu Moab manqei-ak sævam. Yaq ndigu Betliem vemiav-te gimøfakhaz, ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, ezoqam bali avøe gigiap pea ndøngazém.

1 Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, manqa ovøyam megetapak Izlael ezoqam gizømbo-megetam, vikia seke ndøfakhán, ta manqei-te ndøgo. Yaq ezoqa kopo, Betliem vemiav-ák, Zuda manqei-te ndøgo, ndego tegu zas neka tegi nakhei angana menas namba, Moab manqei-té genøwáv, ndǿgo yage-qa.

BSB 1:1 In the days when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. And a certain man from Bethlehem in Judah, with his wife and two sons, went to reside in the land of Moab.
בִּימֵי שְׁפֹט הַשֹּׁפְטִים → Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, manqa ovøyam megetapak Izlael ezoqam gizømbo-megetam (In the days when the judges ruled)
Frame "Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, Elod ... kawa gegoam" Luk 1:5; "manqa ovøyam megetapak" Act 7:51 (judges of the council), "manqa ovøyam megeat ezoqam" Luk 18:2; "gezømbo-megetam" glossary (kings ruled them), plural gi- by analogy. Medium.
וַיְהִי רָעָב בָּאָרֶץ → vikia seke ndøfakhán, ta manqei-te ndøgo (famine in the land)
Verbatim Luk 15:14. High.
אִישׁ מִבֵּית לֶחֶם יְהוּדָה … → ezoqa kopo, Betliem vemiav-ák, Zuda manqei-te ndøgo ... Moab manqei-té genøwáv, ndǿgo yage-qa (a certain man from Bethlehem in Judah ... went to reside in Moab)
"Ezoqa kopo mø̀ndøgoám" Luk 15:11; "Betliem vemiav, Zuda manqei" Mat 2:6; "-ák" Act 8:27; "Elan taon-té genøwáv, vømø̄qom" Act 7:4; purpose "-qa" (tøke-qa Luk 15:15). "Fields of Moab" rendered "Moab manqei" (land, as BSB). High.
הוּא וְאִשְׁתֹּו וּשְׁנֵי בָנָיו → ndego tegu zas neka tegi nakhei angana menas namba (with his wife and two sons)
"tegu zas" Luk 1:5; "nakhei angana menás" Act 7:29; "namba" with. High.

וְשֵׁ֣ם הָאִ֣ישׁ אֱֽלִימֶ֡לֶךְ וְשֵׁם֩ אִשְׁתּ֨וֹ נָעֳמִ֜י וְשֵׁ֥ם שְׁנֵֽי־בָנָ֣יו׀מַחְל֤וֹן וְכִלְיוֹן֙ אֶפְרָתִ֔ים מִבֵּ֥ית לֶ֖חֶם יְהוּדָ֑ה וַיָּבֹ֥אוּ שְׂדֵי־מוֹאָ֖ב וַיִּֽהְיוּ־שָֽׁם׃
BSB 1:2 The man’s name was Elimelech, his wife’s name was Naomi, and the names of his two sons were Mahlon and Chilion. They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem in Judah, and they entered the land of Moab and settled there.
וְשֵׁם הָאִישׁ אֱלִימֶלֶךְ ... → Ngenek ezoqam-qa iz, Elimelék. Tegu zas, tuqa iz Neomí. ... tiqa iziz Malon neka Kilión (The man's name was Elimelech ...)

Act 7:4 "genøwáv, vømø̄qom"; the added sentence "they were foreigners there" is verbatim Act 7:29 and carries the Hebrew "sojourn" (לָגוּר) of v1. High.

6 Yaq Neomi Moab manqei-te gu꞉goam, manqat gu-ewag, av nqægo, Evezøza tegi ezoqam-te tøke-qa ndowāv, lou vøzø̄-etoam. Yaq ndugu tugi ziwap menas namba, até gundigu, Moab manqei ivav-qa gugoám, Zuda manqei-te nqawa veseze-qa.

BSB 1:6 When Naomi heard in Moab that the LORD had attended to His people by providing them with food, she and her daughters-in-law prepared to leave the land of Moab.

"ndugu até gundigu" (she then rose and) Luk 7:38, Mrk 1:31; "vesezam-qa ndøgoám" (wanted to return) Act 7:39; "veseze-qa" 1Co 16:11; "ziwap" Luk 12:53. Medium.

BSB 1:7 Accompanied by her two daughters-in-law, she left the place where she had been living and set out on the road leading back to the land of Judah.
וַתֵּצֵא מִן־הַמָּקֹום אֲשֶׁר הָיְתָה־שָׁמָּה → ta manqei ndøgo, ma guyagam, gè-iváv (left the place where she had been living)
"ta manqei ndøgo gè-iváv" Luk 15:20; "ma Deivid ge꞉yagam" Jhn 7:42; "guyagam" Luk 4:26; "nakhoa-té ginøzáv" after "Yesu-té genøwáv". High.
8 Yaq Neomi tugi ziwap menas, āv guzømbe-eín nqǽgo, “Zo nango tozovesez. Zo ezoqa kopokopo, ekeza evo-qá khoev-té sozó-okhoát. Evezøza tanakh matev bezøgó, zo-te, av zo tanakh matev qazo-matønumam, ti-te ndigu gipakhaez, neka no-te.
וַתֹּ֤אמֶר נָעֳמִי֙ לִשְׁתֵּ֣י כַלֹּתֶ֔יהָ לֵ֣כְנָה שֹּׁ֔בְנָה אִשָּׁ֖ה לְבֵ֣ית אִמָּ֑הּ יַ֣עַשׂ יְהוָ֤ה עִמָּכֶם֙ חֶ֔סֶד כַּאֲשֶׁ֧ר עֲשִׂיתֶ֛ם עִם־הַמֵּתִ֖ים וְעִמָּדִֽי׃
BSB 1:8 Then Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, “Go back, each of you to your mother’s home. May the LORD show you loving devotion, as you have shown to your dead and to me.
לֵכְנָה שֹּׁבְנָה אִשָּׁה לְבֵית אִמָּהּ → Zo nango tozovesez. Zo ezoqa kopokopo, ekeza evo-qá khoev-té sozó-okhoát (Go back, each of you to your mother's home)
"Zion-te nqawa tozovesez" Luk 7:22; "Zo ezoqa kopokopo" 1Pe 4:10; "Manqei-qape ate nqægo, sozó-okhoát" Mrk 16:15; "Pita-qá khoev-té" Mat 8:14. High.
יעשׂה יְהוָה עִמָּכֶם חֶסֶד … → Evezøza tanakh matev bezøgó, zo-te, av zo tanakh matev qazo-matønumam, ti-te ndigu gipakhaez, neka no-te (May the LORD show you loving devotion, as you have shown to your dead and to me)

Ruth 1: translator's notes

What this draft is

A machine draft of Ruth 1 in Kuni (kvg), made 14.10.2026 from the Hebrew (orig/heb/rut001.txt, 22 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The drafter knows no Kuni: every word, suffix and construction was taken from the Kuni NT through _learn/concord.py, and the grammar sketch written first is export/kvg/kvg_BSB/kvg_learning_notes.md. Confidence per verse: high = every piece is an attested NT form or a direct substitution in an attested frame; medium = one or two forms built by analogy (prefix swap for gender/number, optative or question tone added to an attested stem); low = a construction I could not find a close model for. Status: experiment, not reviewed; no footnotes.

Exegetical observations

  • v1 "fields of Moab" (שְׂדֵי מֹואָב, vv. 1, 2, 6, 22): rendered "Moab manqei" (land of Moab) with the BSB; the NT glossary already has "Moab manqei" (Balam).
  • v1 "to sojourn" (לָגוּר) is spread over two sentences: "went there to live" and, in v2, the NT's own phrase for a resident foreigner, "mo-manqei-aním mbá" (Act 7:29).
  • v6 "visited his people" (פָקַד): the NT has an exact idiom for this (Luk 1:68, 7:16), taken over.
  • v8 חֶסֶד: "tanakh matev" (mercy, compassion) rather than "kuku matev" (love, grace). "tanakh" is what the NT uses for the Lord's kindness to a woman (Luk 1:58) and for human compassion (Luk 15:20). "matev eqeieqei" (good deeds) would stress the deed; kept uniform as BSB does.
  • v12 "tonight" (הַלַּיְלָה): "nqægo lova" (Luk 12:20).
  • v13 מַר־לִי מְאֹד מִכֶּם: the BSB's comparative ("more bitter for me than for you") is followed; the alternative "bitter for me because of you" would be "zoqa zapaya".
  • v13 "the hand of the LORD has gone out against me": the NT renders "hand of the Lord against you" (Act 13:11) with "yaq-fia ... ta" (hold judgment / punish), so the idiom, not the image, is translated.
  • v16 "do not urge me": פגע-ב is pressing/entreating; "pouwe" is the NT's "urge, beg, press" (Luk 15:28, 24:29; Act 21:14).
  • v16 "wherever you go I will go": the NT formula "Qo ande āv khoqoté-okhoāt, no até nøtaqá-pavát" says "I will follow you", which is what the clause means in context.
  • v17 the oath formula "thus may the LORD do to me and more" has no NT counterpart; it is unpacked into "only death shall part us; if anything else does, may the LORD punish me severely". The Hebrew כִּי after the oath is read as "if (anything but) death" with the BSB.
  • v17 "I will be buried" is made active-nominal: "my grave will be there" ("manqei ozu", Act 2:29), because no passive of "bury" with a 1sg patient is attested.
  • v19 "the whole town was stirred": the NT's own rendering of the same idiom (Mat 21:10 "ezoqa ewaqape nqova zøndáf") is used; "they said" (fem.) is made explicit as "the women of the village", as in the BSB.
  • v20 "Mara": transliterated "Mala"; the pun (bitter) is not reproducible; no footnotes in this experiment.
  • v21 "full/empty": "with my family" vs "empty-handed" ("khakha-mbá"), the NT's idiom for being sent away with nothing.
  • v21 עָנָה בִי: BSB "testified against me"; the Kuni says "spoke about/against me". Other readings (afflicted me, humbled me) are possible.
  • v22 "Ruth the Moabitess": "Moab manqei-ak sævam" (Moab-land-person woman), after "Itiopia manqei-ák" (Act 8:27) and "Samalia sævam" (Jhn 4:7).

Dialect and style (corpus counts, Kuni NT)

  • Divine names: Mbumbukiam 1207 alignments for "God", Evezøza 555 for "Lord" (concord lex). YHWH = Evezøza, as in the NT's OT quotations (Act 7:49). Shaddai = "Mbumbukiam Bazaføgakh-qape" (Jas 5:4 has "Evezøza Bazaføgakh-qape" for "Lord of Hosts"; Rev 1:8 "ndego bazaføgakh ate ndægo ndømbogo" for "Almighty"). "gods" = mbumbuk (9).
  • Kin terms: zas 31 (wife), sævam 192 (woman), sakheis 74 (women), zivis 18 / anganeam 11 (husband), yo 350 (son), yu 16 (daughter), nakhei mbasi 2 (daughters), nakheis 87, ngæmam 13 (widow), zakhapuka 2 (husband's mother) vs zimpuka 3 (wife's mother), ziwap 2 (daughter-in-law), evo 62 (mother).
  • Narrative connectors: "Yaq" sentence-initial (thousands), "neka", "Geté", "Zapa ndǿgo". Speech frame "āv ...-eín nqǽgo" and "gembo-ein/gumbo-ein/gezø-ein"; "gimbo-einim" 62.
  • Headings: no tone marks, verb-final clauses with the actors named (Luk 1-2 headings).
  • Poetry: "//1" / "//2" lines as in Luk 1:46-55, only where the BSB has poetry (vv. 16-17).
  • Numbers: "ten" (English loan, 21 alignments), "menas" two (122), "viav" years (49).
  • "about ten years": "viav ande āv ten ndi꞉gú" after Luk 1:56.

Open questions

  1. Ruth's name: the glossary spells the person "Lut" (Deivid entry) but the book "Rut 4:17"; the project's book title is "Rut". The text uses Lut; the chapter title "Rut" as given.
  2. חֶסֶד: "tanakh matev" (mercy) or "kuku matev" (love/grace)? Both are frequent; tanakh was chosen for its use with persons (Luk 1:58, 15:20).
  3. v13 comparison "more bitter than for you" vs causal "because of you".
  4. v16 the prohibitive "nøpòuwe᷄" (don't urge me) and the whole oath in v17 are built, not found; a Kuni speaker should check the tone pattern and the oath idiom.
  5. v17 "I will be buried": nominal "my grave will be there" acceptable?
  6. v20-21 "call me Mara" as "my name shall be Mala" (Luk 1:60 pattern) and "testified against me" as "spoke about/against me": both need a reviewer's judgement.
  7. "sister-in-law" (v15): "qogu ezoqam" (your female relative) is the closest attested term; a more exact kin term may exist outside the NT.
  8. "Ephrathites": "Eflata bawan-ak" uses the NT's tribe/clan noun "bawan"; "khagua bawan" (Luk 2:36) is the full form for a tribe.
  9. v22 "beginning of the barley harvest": "ezoqam bali avøe gigiap pea ndøngazém" (people began reaping the barley) is a paraphrase; check whether "avøe" alone means harvest.
  10. The gender prefix gu- when a man speaks to a woman (gumbo-ein) and when a woman acts (guzø-, gunø-): the sketch assumes gu- marks a feminine participant; worth confirming.
