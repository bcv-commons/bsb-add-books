Boaz Lut genqovotaz

1 Yaq Boaz vemiav mboqog-té genøwáv, ndǿgo vømø̄qom. Yaq nqáe! Ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo, Boaz teqa yaq-te manqat ge-ein, ndego nakhoa-te gewav. Yaq Boaz gembo-ein, “Nǿfu. Qotøndéqāv. Nqanek qanøqóm.” Yaq ndego ndowáv, vømø̄qom. 2 Yaq Boaz vemiav-ak megetapak ten mø̀ndøzømbøkhæ-akhá, gezø-ein, “Até vo꞉qonøvé nqanek.” Yaq ndigu tinøqonáv ndǿgo. 3 Yaq Boaz ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo gembo-ein, “Neomi, ndugu Moab manqei-te gunduqavan, nigi nøfu Elimelek-qa manqei nøme, wi-qa gugoám. 4 Yaq nakémbá, no qo nqanek manqat nqataqa-manqate: Nginik ezoqam-qa megemege-te, ndigu nqanek tinøqonáv, neka nogi bawan ezoqam-gi megetapak-qa megemege-te, qo kopømbaqapé, ta manqei toqowi. Qo ta manqei nqovotaz-qa ndoqombogo, yaq gè-nqovotáz. Geté qo nqovotaz-qa poeveav tøgoat, yaq qonømbé-eīn. Zapa ndǿgo, ezoqa nøme mbaín, ta manqei tenqovotaz. Qo mba. Neka no qoqa zita-te.” Yaq ndego gendo-qavøiu, “No ta manqei nó-nqovotáz.” 5 Yaq Boaz āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, qo Neomi-te manqei toqowi, até Lut-té-a, ndugu Moab manqei-ak sævam, ndugu ngæmam, yaq qo até ndugú-a toqo-okɨ. Yaq ta ezoqam-qa iz, ndego genanim, teqa manqei-te nango begó.” 6 Yaq ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo gendo-qavøiu, “No kopømba mbaín, tænqovotaz. Av tægoat ndægo, yaq nøkeza manqei soqaqape-té qanømá. Noqa nqovotaz matev, qakezan qotøndó-ndáp, yaq gè-nqovotáz. Zapa ndǿgo, no kopømba mbaín, tænqovotaz.” 7 Ibugukhokhof, Izlael manqei-te, matev nqánek ndøgoám: Ezoqa manqei nqovotaz-qa o manqei wi-qa tegoat, yaq ndego ekeza tamak ndøkhofoé, neka zifu vømbō-etoam. Yaq ta matev-te ndøgo, ndigu ekeza manqat gè-unimanqatinimín, Izlael manqei-te. 8 Yaq ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo, Boaz gembo-ein, “Qo toqowi. Qakeza manqei ndǿgoát.” Yaq ndego ekeza tamak ndøkhofoé. 9 Yaq Boaz megetapak neka ezoqa ewaqape, āv gezømbe-eín nqǽgo, “Zo nqægo khaiya, nqanek matev, zøkeza bøí qazøte-qeivám. No Neomi-te, Elimelek-qa gigiap ate ndægo, neka Kilion neka Malon-qa gigiap ate ndægo, nowí. 10 Neka até Lút-a, ndugu Moab manqei-ak sævam, Malon-gu zas ndøgoám, no ndugu no-okɨ́. Ndugu nogu zas ndǿgoát. Yaq ta ezoqam-qa iz, ndego genanim, teqa manqei-te nango begó. Neka teqa iz, tegi nøfuap mokho-te neka teqa vemiav mboqog-te, gemǿ-navøemák. Zo nqægo khaiya, nqanek matev, zøkeza bøí qazøte-qeivám.” 11 Yaq ezoqa ewaqape, ndigu vemiav mboqog-te gi꞉goam, neka megetapak, gimbo-qavøinam, “Ni nikeza bǿi qatiqeivím. Ngunuk sævam, ndugu qoqa khoev-te ndowáv, Evezøza ndugu andé Laisol neka Lia bè-matanám. Ndigu sakheis menas, Izlael ezoqam-gi atanakha-zapazapa-za ndøgoám. Qo Eflata manqei-te, gigiap kandakanda betaqagó. Neka Betliem vemiav-te, qoqa emanqat bè-panqavøém. 12 Neka qoqa khoev, andé Pelez-qa khoev begó. Pelez, ndego Zuda-gé yó. Tegu evo, Tamá. Zapa ndǿgo, ngunuk mbasønakam mokho-te, Evezøza qogi qazi qá-etoám.”

Boaz Lut ndø-okɨ

13 Yaq Boaz Lut ndø-okɨ́. Yaq ndugu tegu zas ndøgoám. Yaq ndigu venanqei nonqo gi꞉tøkuanem. Yaq Evezøza ndugu gumbo-tøke, yaq ndugu emu é-møndæ-itán. Yaq ndugu gui-ova, nakhasam anganeám gemboqan. 14 Yaq sakheis Neomi gimbo-einim, “Evezøza-qa iz, yà beto-eqawát. Nqægo khaiya, ndego qo ekeza ezoqam kopo geqa-etoam, qo nqovotaz-qa. Teqa emanqat, Izlael manqei-te, bè-panqavøém. 15 Ndego qo khandi ndakinak qá-etoám. Neka qo ngæpuka toqogoat, ndego qo qá-kewagát. Zapa ndǿgo, qogu ziwap, ndugu qo-te kuku kandambá, ndugu ngenek nakhasam gemboqan. Ndugu qo-te, nakhei angana seven, mø̀ndøløvú.” 16 Yaq Neomi nakhasam gèndāf, ekeza ava-te vø̄ve. Yaq ndugu ngenek nakhasam gumbo-kewagam. 17 Yaq sakheis, ndigu Neomi avønin gimbo-puatat, āv gini-einím nqǽgo, “Nakhasam anganeam ndøqán, Neomi-te.” Yaq ndigu iz Obed mbo-akhaém. Obed, ndego Zesi-gé evé. Neka Zesi, ndego Deivid-gé evé.

Deivid-gi atanakha-zapazapa-za

18 Nqanek Pelez-gi qazí. Pelez gendomanaz, Ezlon vø̄qan. 19 Ezlon gendomanaz, Lam vø̄qan. Lam gendomanaz, Aminadab vø̄qan. 20 Aminadab gendomanaz, Nason vø̄qan. Nason gendomanaz, Salmon vø̄qan. 21 Salmon gendomanaz, Boaz vø̄qan. Boaz gendomanaz, Obed vø̄qan. 22 Obed gendomanaz, Zesi vø̄qan. Zesi gendomanaz, Deivid vø̄qan.

1 Yaq Boaz vemiav mboqog-té genøwáv, ndǿgo vømø̄qom. Yaq nqáe! Ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo, Boaz teqa yaq-te manqat ge-ein, ndego nakhoa-te gewav. Yaq Boaz gembo-ein, “Nǿfu. Qotøndéqāv. Nqanek qanøqóm.” Yaq ndego ndowáv, vømø̄qom.
וּבֹ֨עַז עָלָ֣ה הַשַּׁעַר֮ וַיֵּ֣שֶׁב שָׁם֒ וְהִנֵּ֨ה הַגֹּאֵ֤ל עֹבֵר֙ אֲשֶׁ֣ר דִּבֶּר־בֹּ֔עַז וַיֹּ֛אמֶר ס֥וּרָה שְׁבָה־פֹּ֖ה פְּלֹנִ֣י אַלְמֹנִ֑י וַיָּ֖סַר וַיֵּשֵֽׁב׃
BSB 4:1 Meanwhile, Boaz went to the gate and sat down there. Soon the kinsman-redeemer of whom he had spoken came along, and Boaz said, “Come over here, my friend, and sit down.” So he went over and sat down.
וּבֹעַז עָלָה הַשַּׁעַר וַיֵּשֶׁב שָׁם → Yaq Boaz vemiav mboqog-té genøwáv, ndǿgo vømø̄qom (Boaz went to the gate and sat down there)
formula went_and_settled (Act 7:4); "taon mboqog-te" city gate (Act 9:24, 14:13) → vemiav mboqog (Bethlehem is a vemiav in ch1-3). Medium (vemiav mboqog built).
וְהִנֵּה הַגֹּאֵל עֹבֵר אֲשֶׁר דִּבֶּר־בֹּעַז → Yaq nqáe! Ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo, Boaz teqa yaq-te manqat ge-ein, ndego nakhoa-te gewav (Soon the kinsman-redeemer of whom he had spoken came along)
"Nqáe!" (Luk 1:20); the redeemer = "ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo" (that relative, ch3 v12); appositive relative "X, ndego bugukhokhof ge-ein" (Jhn 18:9); "Yesu gewav" (as Jesus was passing by, Jhn 9:1). Medium.
סוּרָה שְׁבָה־פֹּה פְּלֹנִי אַלְמֹנִי → “Nǿfu. Qotøndéqāv. Nqanek qanøqóm.” (Come over here, my friend, and sit down)
"Nǿfu." vocative to an unnamed man (Luk 5:20, 11:5, Mat 22:12); "Qotøndéqāv" (ch2 v14); "nqanek qanøqóm" (ch3 v13). High.
וַיָּסַר וַיֵּשֵׁב → Yaq ndego ndowáv, vømø̄qom (So he went over and sat down)
"ndowáv" came (Luk 7:16), "vømø̄qom" (Mat 15:29). High.
2 Yaq Boaz vemiav-ak megetapak ten mø̀ndøzømbøkhæ-akhá, gezø-ein, “Até vo꞉qonøvé nqanek.” Yaq ndigu tinøqonáv ndǿgo.
וַיִּקַּ֞ח עֲשָׂרָ֧ה אֲנָשִׁ֛ים מִזִּקְנֵ֥י הָעִ֖יר וַיֹּ֣אמֶר שְׁבוּ־פֹ֑ה וַיֵּשֵֽׁבוּ׃
BSB 4:2 Then Boaz took ten of the elders of the city and said, “Sit here,” and they did so.
וַיִּקַּח עֲשָׂרָה אֲנָשִׁים מִזִּקְנֵי הָעִיר → Boaz vemiav-ak megetapak ten mø̀ndøzømbøkhæ-akhá (Boaz took ten of the elders of the city)
"tegi sasae ezoqam ten mø̀ndøzømbøkhæ-akhá" (called ten of his servants, Luk 19:13); megetapak elders (lexicon 56/0.76). "Took" read as "called". High.
שְׁבוּ־פֹה וַיֵּשֵׁבוּ → “Até vo꞉qonøvé nqanek.” Yaq ndigu tinøqonáv ndǿgo (“Sit here,” and they did so)
"Até vo꞉qonøvé nqanek" (Sit here, Mrk 14:32); "Yaq ezoqa tinøqonáv ndǿgo" (the men sat down, Jhn 6:10). High.
3 Yaq Boaz ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo gembo-ein, “Neomi, ndugu Moab manqei-te gunduqavan, nigi nøfu Elimelek-qa manqei nøme, wi-qa gugoám.
וַיֹּ֨אמֶר֙ לַגֹּאֵ֔ל חֶלְקַת֙ הַשָּׂדֶ֔ה אֲשֶׁ֥ר לְאָחִ֖ינוּ לֶאֱלִימֶ֑לֶךְ מָכְרָ֣ה נָעֳמִ֔י הַשָּׁ֖בָה מִשְּׂדֵ֥ה מוֹאָֽב׃
BSB 4:3 And he said to the kinsman-redeemer, “Naomi, who has returned from the land of Moab, is selling the piece of land that belonged to our brother Elimelech.
חֶלְקַת הַשָּׂדֶה אֲשֶׁר לְאָחִינוּ … → Neomi, ndugu Moab manqei-te gunduqavan, nigi nøfu Elimelek-qa manqei nøme, wi-qa gugoám (Naomi ... is selling the piece of land that belonged to our brother Elimelech)
"Moab manqei-te gunduqavan" (ch1 v22); "manqei nøme" piece of land (Act 5:1); wi sell (Act 5:1 gèwí, 4:37); "X-qa gugoám" (was about to, ch1 v6 ivav-qa gugoám); nøfu brother (lexicon 24). Medium (wi-qa gugoám built).
4 Yaq nakémbá, no qo nqanek manqat nqataqa-manqate: Nginik ezoqam-qa megemege-te, ndigu nqanek tinøqonáv, neka nogi bawan ezoqam-gi megetapak-qa megemege-te, qo kopømbaqapé, ta manqei toqowi. Qo ta manqei nqovotaz-qa ndoqombogo, yaq gè-nqovotáz. Geté qo nqovotaz-qa poeveav tøgoat, yaq qonømbé-eīn. Zapa ndǿgo, ezoqa nøme mbaín, ta manqei tenqovotaz. Qo mba. Neka no qoqa zita-te.” Yaq ndego gendo-qavøiu, “No ta manqei nó-nqovotáz.”
וַאֲנִ֨י אָמַ֜רְתִּי אֶגְלֶ֧ה אָזְנְךָ֣ לֵאמֹ֗ר קְ֠נֵה נֶ֥גֶד הַֽיֹּשְׁבִים֮ וְנֶ֣גֶד זִקְנֵ֣י עַמִּי֒ אִם־תִּגְאַל֙ גְּאָ֔ל וְאִם־לֹ֨א יִגְאַ֜ל הַגִּ֣ידָה לִּ֗י וְאֵֽדְעָה֙ כִּ֣י אֵ֤ין זוּלָֽתְךָ֙ לִגְא֔וֹל וְאָנֹכִ֖י אַחֲרֶ֑יךָ וַיֹּ֖אמֶר אָנֹכִ֥י אֶגְאָֽל׃
BSB 4:4 I thought I should inform you that you may buy it back in the presence of those seated here and in the presence of the elders of my people. If you want to redeem it, do so. But if you will not redeem it, tell me so I may know, because there is no one but you to redeem it, and I am next after you.” “I will redeem it,” he replied.
וַאֲנִי אָמַרְתִּי אֶגְלֶה אָזְנְךָ לֵאמֹר → Yaq nakémbá, no qo nqanek manqat nqataqa-manqate: (I thought I should inform you)
"Manqat nqanek ... no nqataqa-manqate" (what I am telling you, 2Ti 2:7); "I thought" dropped. Medium.
קְנֵה נֶגֶד הַיֹּשְׁבִים וְנֶגֶד … → Nginik ezoqam-qa megemege-te, ndigu nqanek tinøqonáv, neka nogi bawan ezoqam-gi megetapak-qa megemege-te, qo kopømbaqapé, ta manqei toqowi (you may buy it back in the presence of those seated here and the elders of my people)
"tiqá megemege-té" before them (Luk 22:66); "nogi bawan ezoqam" (Mat 2:6); "qo kopømbaqapé, toqo-X" you may (Act 26:1). Medium.
אִם־תִּגְאַל גְּאָל → Qo ta manqei nqovotaz-qa ndoqombogo, yaq gè-nqovotáz (If you want to redeem it, do so)
"qo ... ndape-qa ndoqombogo" (if you want to, Mat 19:17); imperative gè- (gè-etoám, Luk 6:38). Low (gè-nqovotáz built).
וְאִם־לֹא יִגְאַל הַגִּידָה לִּי וְאֵדְעָה → Geté qo nqovotaz-qa poeveav tøgoat, yaq qonømbé-eīn (But if you will not redeem it, tell me so I may know)
"poeveav tøgoat" (ch3 v13); "Qonømbé-eīn" tell me (Act 22:27). With the BSB, "you" (many MSS) not "he". "so I may know" dropped. Medium.
כִּי אֵין זוּלָתְךָ לִגְאוֹל … → Zapa ndǿgo, ezoqa nøme mbaín, ta manqei tenqovotaz. Qo mba. Neka no qoqa zita-te (there is no one but you to redeem it, and I am next after you)
"ezoqa nøme mbaín, tiqa gigiap tiwiat" (no one left to buy, Rev 18:11); "mba" only; "X-qa zita-te" after. Medium.
וַיֹּאמֶר אָנֹכִי אֶגְאָל → Yaq ndego gendo-qavøiu, “No ta manqei nó-nqovotáz.” (“I will redeem it,” he replied)
"gendo-qavøiu" (Jhn 9:27); 1sg future nó- as "nó-unimanqatín" (Heb 8:8). Low (nó-nqovotáz built).
5 Yaq Boaz āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, qo Neomi-te manqei toqowi, até Lut-té-a, ndugu Moab manqei-ak sævam, ndugu ngæmam, yaq qo até ndugú-a toqo-okɨ. Yaq ta ezoqam-qa iz, ndego genanim, teqa manqei-te nango begó.”
וַיֹּ֣אמֶר בֹּ֔עַז בְּיוֹם־קְנוֹתְךָ֥ הַשָּׂדֶ֖ה מִיַּ֣ד נָעֳמִ֑י וּ֠מֵאֵת ר֣וּת הַמּוֹאֲבִיָּ֤ה אֵֽשֶׁת־הַמֵּת֙ קָנִ֔יתָה לְהָקִ֥ים שֵׁם־הַמֵּ֖ת עַל־נַחֲלָתֽוֹ׃
BSB 4:5 Then Boaz said, “On the day you buy the land from Naomi and also from Ruth the Moabitess, you must also acquire the widow of the deceased in order to raise up the name of the deceased on his inheritance.”
בְּיוֹם־קְנוֹתְךָ הַשָּׂדֶה מִיַּד נָעֳמִי … → Ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, qo Neomi-te manqei toqowi, até Lut-té-a, ndugu Moab manqei-ak sævam (On the day you buy the land from Naomi and also from Ruth the Moabitess)
"Ta khøuwa-te ndǿgo, ... tozo-einim" (on the day you say, Luk 13:35); "até X-té-a" (Col 1:6 "Até zo mokho-té-a"); Moabitess as ch1 v22. Medium.
אֵשֶׁת־הַמֵּת קָנִיתָ → ndugu ngæmam, yaq qo até ndugú-a toqo-okɨ (you must also acquire the widow of the deceased)
qere "you acquire" with the BSB; "ngæmam ... be-okɨát" (marry the widow, Luk 20:28), toqo-okɨ (learning notes). Medium.
לְהָקִים שֵׁם־הַמֵּת עַל־נַחֲלָתוֹ → Yaq ta ezoqam-qa iz, ndego genanim, teqa manqei-te nango begó (in order to raise up the name of the deceased on his inheritance)
wish "begó" (ch2 v9); inheritance = manqei (Act 7:5, 13:19); "ndego genanim" the one who died (Luk 20:28). Low (see gap:raise_up_name).
6 Yaq ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo gendo-qavøiu, “No kopømba mbaín, tænqovotaz. Av tægoat ndægo, yaq nøkeza manqei soqaqape-té qanømá. Noqa nqovotaz matev, qakezan qotøndó-ndáp, yaq gè-nqovotáz. Zapa ndǿgo, no kopømba mbaín, tænqovotaz.”
וַיֹּ֣אמֶר הַגֹּאֵ֗ל לֹ֤א אוּכַל֙ לִגְאָל־לִ֔י פֶּן־אַשְׁחִ֖ית אֶת־נַחֲלָתִ֑י גְּאַל־לְךָ֤ אַתָּה֙ אֶת־גְּאֻלָּתִ֔י כִּ֥י לֹא־אוּכַ֖ל לִגְאֹֽל׃
BSB 4:6 The kinsman-redeemer replied, “I cannot redeem it myself, or I would jeopardize my own inheritance. Take my right of redemption, because I cannot redeem it.”
לֹא אוּכַל לִגְאוֹל־לִי → No kopømba mbaín, tænqovotaz (I cannot redeem it myself)
"kopømba mbaín tæmatanam" (I cannot do it, Rom 7:18). High.
פֶּן־אַשְׁחִית אֶת־נַחֲלָתִי → Av tægoat ndægo, yaq nøkeza manqei soqaqape-té qanømá (or I would jeopardize my own inheritance)
"Av tægoat ndægo" (if I do so, 1Co 9:18); "soqaqape-té qanømá" (leads to destruction, Mat 7:13). Low.
גְּאַל־לְךָ אַתָּה אֶת־גְּאֻלָּתִי → Noqa nqovotaz matev, qakezan qotøndó-ndáp, yaq gè-nqovotáz (Take my right of redemption)
"qotøndó-ndáp" (bring, 2Ti 4:13); "qakezan" yourself (Act 26:1). Low (nqovotaz matev built).
כִּי לֹא־אוּכַל לִגְאֹל → Zapa ndǿgo, no kopømba mbaín, tænqovotaz (because I cannot redeem it)

Ruth 4: translator's notes

What this draft is

A machine draft of Ruth 4 in Kuni (kvg), made 14.10.2026 from the Hebrew (orig/heb/rut004.txt, 22 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The drafter knows no Kuni: every word, suffix and construction was taken from the Kuni NT, first through the verse pack (_learn/packs/rut004.md) and the learning notes, then through _learn/concord.py (33 lookups) for what the pack lacked, and from chapters 1-3, whose renderings are reused verbatim wherever the Hebrew repeats (Moab manqei-ak sævam, Moab manqei-te gunduqavan, ekeza ezoqam, nqovotaz-qa, poeveav tøgoat, nqægo khaiya, tegu zas, Qotøndéqāv, qanøqóm, mbasønakam, ziwap, the comparison "A, B mø̀ndøløvú"). The genealogy (vv. 18-22) is copied from Mat 1:3-6. Confidence per verse: high = every piece is an attested NT form or a direct substitution in an attested frame; medium = one or two forms built by analogy; low = a construction without a close model. Status: experiment, not reviewed; no footnotes.

Exegetical observations

  • v1 הִנֵּה "behold" is "Nqáe!" as in ch3 v8. פְּלֹנִי אַלְמֹנִי ("so-and-so", the narrator keeps the man nameless) is the NT vocative "Nǿfu." (friend), as the BSB has it.
  • The redeemer (גֹּאֵל) still has no noun. In narrative he is "ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo" (that relative), introduced in v1 as the one "of whom Boaz had spoken" (ch3 v12); the verb is nqovotaz throughout, as in chapters 2-3. In v4-6 the object is the land ("ta manqei").
  • v3 מָכְרָה (perfect) is read with the BSB as "is selling" ("wi-qa gugoám", was about to sell). The NT stem wi both buys (Luk 14:18, Act 1:18) and sells (Act 5:1, 4:37).
  • v4 "uncover your ear" = "I am telling you this" (2Ti 2:7); the BSB's "you" in "if you will not redeem" (many MSS) is followed against the MT "he".
  • v5 קניתי (ketiv "I acquire") is read with the qere and the BSB: "you acquire". The levirate purpose "raise up the name of the dead on his inheritance" is a wish that his name stay on his land (begó); Luk 20:28's "patu bembō-qatayat" (raise up offspring) is the alternative.
  • v7 The narrator's custom note is rendered as a case ("if a man wanted to redeem or buy land, he took off his sandal and gave it to his friend"); "confirmation" uses Heb 6:16's oath sentence verbatim. "Exchange" (תְּמוּרָה) is folded into wi (trade).
  • v8 The Hebrew order (he said "buy it", then drew off his sandal) is kept; the BSB reverses it.
  • v11 "build the house of Israel" is "they were the ancestors of the Israelites"; "do valiantly / be prosperous" is "may you have many possessions" (the BSB's reading of חַיִל, as in 2:1 and 3:11 for wealth/worth); "call a name" is "may your fame spread".
  • v14 "who has not left you without a redeemer" is turned positive ("who has given you a relative to redeem you"), since "has not left (you)" is attested only with 1sg objects.
  • v15 "restorer of life" = "he will give you new life" (renewal, 2Co 4:16); "sustain your old age" = "when you are old, he will look after you".
  • v16 אֹמֶנֶת "nurse" (foster mother) = "she looked after the child" (kewag).
  • v18-22 copied from Mat 1:3-6 (same persons, same form); the heading is Mat 1's "...-gi atanakha-zapazapa-za" with David.

New name forms

  • Laisol (Rachel): attested, Mat 2:18 and the glossary (Benzamin).
  • Lia (Leah): not in the NT; English "Lee-uh", i as in Zesi, final -a kept (Ena). The learning notes list "Leia (Leah)" as an example, but no such form is in the NT (Leiab is Rahab).
  • Pelez (Perez), Tama (Tamar), Ezlon, Lam, Aminadab, Nason, Salmon, Obed, Zesi, Deivid: attested, Mat 1:3-6 / Luk 3:32-33.
  • Eflata (Ephrathah, from ch1) here as a place: "Eflata manqei".

Open questions

  1. The kinsman-redeemer: "ta ekeza ezoqam ndøgo" in narrative, nqovotaz for the verb; the built forms gè-nqovotáz (imperative), nó-nqovotáz (1sg future) and "nqovotaz matev" (right of redemption) need a speaker. Is there a Kuni term for the relative who takes over a dead man's land and widow?
  2. v5/v10 "raise up the name of the dead on his inheritance" as the wish "teqa iz, teqa manqei-te nango begó": does it carry the levirate sense, or should Luk 20:28's "patu bembō-qatayat" (raise up offspring for him) be used?
  3. v7 the custom note: is a conditional case ("tegoat ..., yaq ...") with a plain past natural for "used to", and does "wi" cover "exchange"?
  4. v11 "May the LORD make the woman like Rachel and Leah" (bè-matanám with "andé"): built; "Lia" for Leah is a guess.
  5. v11/v14 "be famous / may his name be known" as "may the news of you spread" (bè-panqavøém): the positive optative of panqavøem is not attested.
  6. v15 "renew your life" ("give you new life") and "sustain your old age" (qá-kewagát): both built.
  7. v3 "Naomi is selling" as "wi-qa gugoám" (was about to sell): is the tense right for a sale in progress?
  8. "City gate" as "vemiav mboqog" (the NT has only "taon mboqog"): should Bethlehem here be a taon?
