Genesis 1: our draft against the human Kuni Genesis

Our draft (genesis-test/gen001.txt) was made on 2026-10-15 by one fresh agent with the tagged clause packages, before the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30 arrived (2026-10-15, source/ of the edition). It is a clean test: no agent had seen the human text. Since then Genesis is part of the corpus, and the draft is kept here as a test.

The best Kuni test so far (Ruth 1–4: 60.1–64.3) and the widest margin over the retrieval floor (+9.0; Ruth +5.9 to +8.5). Per verse 38.6–81.3; 1:1 is 81.3.

Where it differs, in three systematic places:

  1. The day formula ("and there was evening, and there was morning: the Nth day", 1:5, 8, 13, 19, 23, 31) is the lowest group (1:13 42.4, 1:19 38.7, 1:23 39.8). Our draft built the ordinal on Luke 24:21's "the third day" (Khøuwa mø̀ndømø-misika-éz), rated "high" because the NT phrase matched exactly; the human text uses another construction (gè- with the number) and another wording for evening and morning. A store correction; it recurs in the genealogies.
  2. The name given to the light ("he called the light Day"): the human text uses another word than Khøuwa.
  3. 1:2 (38.6): the human text explains and is about twice as long, as the Ruth translators do.
