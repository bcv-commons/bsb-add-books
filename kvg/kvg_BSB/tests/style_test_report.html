Test drafts

Style check and revision test, 2026-10-14

CC0; quotations from the Kuni texts credited in ../CREDITS.md. Tool: scripts/style.py (docs/proposals/language-toolkit/). Drafts: the NT-only Ruth test drafts (ruth-test/). Human Ruth: eBible kvg 2015, quarantined during the test and released afterwards.

1. Does the check find real deviations?

Each flag says "the draft has more (fewer) X than the human translators would". Checked against the human Ruth, chapter by chapter: the human text lies in the flagged direction for 20 of 26 flags (77%) and for 49 of 60 flags + watch items (82%). Pooled over all four chapters the check is weaker (7 of 12): it is meant per chapter.

Wrong flags come from the gap between the NT and OT narrative, which the NT-based expectations can't see: land phrases (manqei -te, "in the land of Moab"), taon, ezoqam -te; and from content the Hebrew requires (ndabua clothes, zenda feet in Ruth 3). The human Ruth itself gets 6 flags, our four drafts 8.

2. Does revising with the report improve the drafts?

Four fresh agents revised Ruth 1 and Ruth 3, without access to the human Ruth, the store, the wiki or the lexicons (only the NT and Jonah through concord.py). Two had the style report, two (control) only the instruction to make the text read like the corpus.

In Ruth 1 the report agent moved -qape (Shaddai) 4 → 2 (human 1) and ndego 1 → 4 (human 3), but followed a wrong flag on manqei -te (10 → 8, human 11).

  • The style moves to the human translators' figures with the report, not without it: the control agents fixed word forms and verb choices and left the style as it was.
  • chrF does not rise. Removing a connector or a pronoun changes only a few characters, and the agent's rewording of the rest of the sentence seldom matches the human words. chrF measures closeness to one human text; it can't show a gain in style.
  • Cost: 100–120k tokens, 4–5 minutes per chapter for a separate revision agent.

3. Use

  • Per chapter, as the drafting agent's last step (style_check: true in project.yaml), not as a separate agent.
  • The agent should treat a flag as a question: keep the draft where the Hebrew needs the word (the Ruth 3 agent rightly kept ndabua and zenda).
  • Whether the moved style reads better is for the Kuni team to judge: give them the original and the revised Ruth 3 (style-test/report/rut003.txt, with rut003_revision.md) side by side.
