Lut Boaz-qa khae-te bali nanga gu-vingisam

1 Yaq Neomi-ge zivis Elimelek, tege ekeza ezoqam mø̀ndøgoám. Ndego gigiap eve ezoqám neka iz akhae ezoqám. Ndego Elimelek-qa bawan-ák. Teqa iz Boáz. 2 Yaq Lut, ndugu Moab manqei-ak sævam, Neomi āv gumbøe-eín nqǽgo, “Ambá no khae-té qatǿnáv, ezoqa nøme-qa zita-te, bali nanga vømé-vingisām, ndego kuku tømbogo, no-te.” Yaq Neomi gumbo-ein, “Ngólam. Mòqoqáv.” 3 Yaq Lut gè-iváv, khae-té gunøwáv. Yaq sasae ezoqam-qa zita-te, bali nanga gu-vingisam. Yaq matev āv qanefakhán nqǽgo: Ta khae ndøgo, ma ndugu gumøfakhan, Boaz-qá khaé, ndego Elimelek-qa bawan-ák. 4 Yaq nqáe! Boaz Betliem vemiav-te gendowáv. Yaq ndego sasae ezoqam gezø-ein, “Evezøza ndø̀goát, zo namba.” Yaq ndigu gimbo-einim, “Evezøza qo bèkhakheinøqavém.” 5 Yaq Boaz tege sasae ezoqam gembobevap, ndego sasae ezoqa nøme gezø-kewagam, “Gê, ngunuk mbasønakam, gekha ezoqam-gû?” 6 Yaq ndego sasae ezoqam, ndego sasae ezoqa nøme gezø-kewagam, gendo-qavøiu, “Ngunuk mbasønakam, Moab manqei-ak sævám. Ndugu Neomi namba, Moab manqei-te gunduqavan. 7 Ndugu āv guni-eín nqǽgo, ‘Ambá no sasae ezoqam-qa zita-te, bali møvøyam mokho-te, bali nanga vømé-vingisām.’ Yaq ndugu qanaqanus ndowáv. Yaq atema ndakin, sasae gu꞉goam. Khoev-te mba, khøuwa elavøqase, pusa ndøtá.”

Boaz Lut-te tanakh matev gego

8 Yaq Boaz Lut āv gumbøe-eín nqǽgo, “Ngólam. Nqáe! Qo khae nøme-te, bali nanga vingisam-qa, ndø-òkho᷄. Neka nqanek khae ndø-ìva᷄v. Geté nogi sasae mbasønakheis namba mba qanøqóm. 9 Qoqa bøi, ta khae-te mbá begó, ma ndigu avøe gigiap ndipeanem ndøgo. Yaq ndigu sasae mbasønakheis-qa zita-te qanøqáv. No ezoqa ndakinak, manqa āv qazømbe-løvuá nqǽgo, ‘Zo ngunuk sævam kha mbokhanø̀ze᷄m.’ Yaq ibøkha naq tøqa-papat, qo kopømbaqapé, ibøkha ziøvem nonqo-te toqo-iz, ndøgo ezoqa ndakinak ibøkha gindi-takhaemem.” 10 Yaq ndugu ekeza kha, manqei me꞉laváo, bugug até manqei-té, vømbō-ein, “Gekha zapâ, qo kuku kandambá no-te, neka no qoqonø-omet? No manqei vini-ák.” 11 Geté Boaz gumbo-qavøiu, “No qoqa yaq-te, manqat kandambá qæ-ewagam. Qeivis gènaním, taqa zita-te, qo qogu zakhapuka-te, matev eqeieqei ate ndægo qoqo-matønumam. Neka qo qave neka qavo neka qakeza manqei, qoqo-ivøvem. Yaq bawan ezoqam-te qoqotøndoqav, ndigu qo bugukhokhof zøtezateáv. 12 Evezøza qoqa sasae-qa yaq-fia, qo betaqá-etoám. Yaq-fia ate ndægo, Evezøza-te betaqagó, ndego Izlael ezoqam-ge Mbumbukiam. Qo te-te qoqotøndoqav, qakeza kha teqá taf mokho-té qoqozav.” 13 Yaq ndugu gundu-qavøiu, “Khanánqa. Kuku até betaqagó, no-te. Zapa ndǿgo, qo no kha føgakh qoqonø-etoam, neka zita tøpøzu manqat qoqonø-ein, no qogu sasae sævam. Geté no qogu sasae mbasønakam kopo mbá.” 14 Yaq lou loge khøuwa-te, Boaz gumbo-ein, “Qotøndéqāv. Flawa mòqológ, neka qoqa lou mbewat, waen khaqain-qape-te vø̀qoqóm.” Yaq ndugu sasae ezoqam-qa avønin-te, sègeqóm. Yaq Boaz bali nanga vesu, sasa mbo-etoam. Yaq ndugu lou gulog, até vømbō-ewez. Lou nøme sège-iváv. 15 Yaq Lut até gundigu, bali nanga vingisam-qa. Yaq Boaz tegi ezoqa ndakinak, manqa āv gezømbe-løvuá nqǽgo, “Oskiá ndøgo, ndugu bali møvøyam mokho-te bali nanga tu-vingisam, zo ngunuk sævam mivi mbo-etøòme᷄m. 16 Neka até zó-a, bali møvøyam-te, bali nøme vø̀khæ-khofoém, tu-te vø̄-ivøvem. Yaq ndugu bè-vingisam. Neka ngunuk sævam mbogùgu᷄.”

Lut tugu zakhapuka-te nango gunduqavan

17 Yaq Lut khae-te, bali nanga gu-vingisam, atema khagús. Yaq bali nanga ndøgo, gu-vingisam, sasa ndøtitisam, tag vø̄khofovøem. Yaq bali ndøgo, ande āv yakhav kopo ndægó. 18 Yaq ndugu bali ndø-upøgím, Betliem vemiav-té gunøwáv. Yaq tugu zakhapuka gu-omet, bali nanga ndøgo, Lut gu-vingisam. Yaq lou nøme ndøgo, Lut gè-iváv, ndugu bavokhó gundo-itú, tugu zakhapuka sasa mbo-etoam. 19 Yaq tugu zakhapuka gumbo-ein, “Qo nqægo khaiya, gekham-tē bali nanga qoqo-vingisam? Neka gekham-tē sasae qoqogoam? Ngenek ezoqam, qo geqa-omet, Mbumbukiam ndego bèkhakheinøvém.” Yaq Lut tugu zakhapuka manqat gumbøe-ein, ta ezoqam-qa yaq-te, ma te namba sasae gugoam ndøgo. Āv guni-eín nqǽgo, “Ngenek ezoqam, no te namba nqægo khaiya sasae qægoam, teqa iz Boáz.” 20 Yaq Neomi tugu ziwap gumbo-ein, “Evezøza ndego bèkhakheinøvém. Evezøza tanakh matev zua gegó, khandi ezoqam-te neka løvøte ezoqam-te.” Yaq Neomi manqat nøme gumbo-ein, “Ngenek ezoqam, nigi ekeza ezoqám. Ni-te avønín. Ndego nigi ekeza ezoqa nøme, ndigu ni nqovotaz-qa ndi꞉gú.” 21 Yaq Lut, ndugu Moab manqei-ak sævam, āv guni-eín nqǽgo, “Até ndegó-a, āv genømbe-eín nqǽgo, ‘Qo nogi ezoqa ndakinak namba mba qanøqóm, atema noqa avøe gigiap ate ndægo, vømǿ-navøēm.’ ” 22 Yaq Neomi tugu ziwap Lut gumbo-ein, “Ngólam. Qo tegi sasae mbasønakheis namba toqo-apat, ndøgo qanimáv. Soqaín ndøgo, khae nøme-te, matev soqa tømøqa-fakhan.” 23 Yaq Lut Boaz-gi sasae mbasønakheis-te gùmu-tøkenøvém, bali nanga vingisam-qa, atema bali vingisam khøuwa neka wit vingisam khøuwa, vømǿ-navøēm. Yaq ndugu tugu zakhapuka namba gu꞉yagam.

BSB 2:1 Now Naomi had a relative on her husband’s side, a prominent man of noble character from the clan of Elimelech, whose name was Boaz.

"tege ekeza ezoqam" (his relative, Jhn 18:26), "qogu ezoqam" (Luk 1:36); "X mø̀ndøgoám. Teqa iz Y." Luk 1:5; "Neomi-ge zivis" ch1 v3. High.
אִישׁ גִּבּוֹר חַיִל → Ndego gigiap eve ezoqám neka iz akhae ezoqám (a prominent man of noble character)
"Ndego gigiap eve ezoqám" (he was very wealthy, Luk 19:2; Luk 14:12 rich neighbours); "Iz akhae ezoqám" (a man of noble birth, Luk 19:12; "Gaman iz akhae ezoqám" Act 8:27). Wealth + standing, as the Hebrew idiom. Medium.
מִמִּשְׁפַּחַת אֱלִימֶלֶךְ וּשְׁמוֹ בֹּעַז → Ndego Elimelek-qa bawan-ák. Teqa iz Boáz (from the clan of Elimelech, whose name was Boaz)
"Asa khagua bawan-ák" Luk 2:36, ch1 "Eflata bawan-ák"; "Teqa iz Zekalayá" Luk 1:5 (high tone on the predicate name; Boáz in the glossary). High.

BSB 2:2 And Ruth the Moabitess said to Naomi, “Please let me go into the fields and glean heads of grain after someone in whose sight I may find favor.” “Go ahead, my daughter,” Naomi replied.
אֵלְכָה־נָּא הַשָּׂדֶה וַאֲלַקֳטָה בַשִּׁבֳּלִים → Ambá no khae-té qatǿnáv, ... bali nanga vømé-vingisām (Please let me go into the fields and glean heads of grain)
"Ambá" opens a request (Jhn 4:7 "Ambá ibøkha qonø-etoamɨ́n", Act 7:46); "qatǿnáv, ... vømé-zømesīm" (I will go and preach, Act 18:6) with "Khae-té genøwáv" (Mrk 4:3); heads of grain = "wit nanga ndøvingisømém" (picked the heads of grain, Mat 12:1, Mrk 2:23, Luk 6:1), barley "bali" (store). Gleaning = picking heads of grain (bali nanga vingisam) throughout. Medium.
אַחַר אֲשֶׁר אֶמְצָא־חֵן בְּעֵינָיו → ezoqa nøme-qa zita-te, ..., ndego kuku tømbogo, no-te (after someone in whose sight I may find favor)
"X-qa zita-te" behind (ch1 v15); favour = kuku: "Mbumbukiam kukú qo-te" (you have found favour with God, Luk 1:30), "kuku ndømbogo" (1Co 8:3) with irrealis tø-. Medium.
לְכִי בִתִּי → Ngólam. Mòqoqáv. (Go ahead, my daughter)
Verbatim Mrk 5:34 / Luk 8:48 ("Daughter ... Go"). High.

וַתֵּלֶךְ וַתָּבוֹא וַתְּלַקֵּט בַּשָּׂדֶה … → gè-iváv, khae-té gunøwáv. Yaq sasae ezoqam-qa zita-te, bali nanga gu-vingisam (departed and went out into the field and gleaned after the harvesters)
"gè-iváv" Luk 2:4; harvesters = "sasae ezoqam" (Mat 13:30 "I will tell the harvesters", Luk 10:2 workers of the harvest); "gu-vingisam" built (gu- + vingisam; "toqo-vingisam" attested). Medium.

BSB 2:5 And Boaz asked the foreman of his harvesters, “Whose young woman is this?”

"gembobevap" asked him (Luk 1:18); "tege sasae ezoqam" (Mat 8:13); foreman = the servant who looked after the other workers: "guzø-kewagam" (looked after them, 1Ti 5:10), "khae gimbøe-kewagam" (Luk 20:10). Medium.

ch1 v22 verbatim for both phrases; "gendo-qavøiu" he replied (Jhn 1:46). High.
7 Ndugu āv guni-eín nqǽgo, ‘Ambá no sasae ezoqam-qa zita-te, bali møvøyam mokho-te, bali nanga vømé-vingisām.’ Yaq ndugu qanaqanus ndowáv. Yaq atema ndakin, sasae gu꞉goam. Khoev-te mba, khøuwa elavøqase, pusa ndøtá.”
וַתֹּ֗אמֶר אֲלַקֳטָה־נָּא֙ וְאָסַפְתִּ֣י בָֽעֳמָרִ֔ים אַחֲרֵ֖י הַקּוֹצְרִ֑ים וַתָּב֣וֹא וַֽתַּעֲמ֗וֹד מֵאָ֤ז הַבֹּ֨קֶר֙ וְעַד־עַ֔תָּה זֶ֛ה שִׁבְתָּ֥הּ הַבַּ֖יִת מְעָֽט׃
BSB 2:7 She has said, ‘Please let me glean and gather among the sheaves after the harvesters.’ So she came out and has continued from morning until now, except that she rested a short time in the shelter.”
אֲלַקֳטָה־נָּא וְאָסַפְתִּי בָעֳמָרִים → Ambá no ... bali møvøyam mokho-te, bali nanga vømé-vingisām (Please let me glean and gather among the sheaves)
As v2; sheaves = "bali møvøyam": "vø̄-møvøemem" (tie them in bundles, Mat 13:30), "lou møvøyam khoev" (barn, Mat 13:30), "ndemøvøyam" (stores, Mat 13:52). No noun "sheaf" in the NT. Low for møvøyam.
וַתָּבוֹא וַתַּעֲמוֹד מֵאָז הַבֹּקֶר וְעַד־עַתָּה → ndugu qanaqanus ndowáv. Yaq atema ndakin, sasae gu꞉goam (she came out and has continued from morning until now)
"qanaqanus" morning (Act 5:21, 28:23 "qanaqanus atéma khagus"); "atema ndakin" (1Jn 2:13); "khokho sasae gugoam" (she did servant's work, 1Ti 5:10), durative gu꞉- (Act 16:15). High.
זֶה שִׁבְתָּהּ הַבַּיִת מְעָט → Khoev-te mba, khøuwa elavøqase, pusa ndøtá (except that she rested a short time in the shelter)

Exegetical observations

  • Gleaning (לקט) has no NT word. The NT's "pick the heads of grain" (wit nanga vingisam, Mat 12:1, Mrk 2:23, Luk 6:1) is the closest act, so gleaning is "bali nanga vingisam" (picking barley heads) throughout; "glean" and "gather" (v7 אסף, v16 לקט) are not distinguished.
  • Harvesters (קֹצְרִים) are "sasae ezoqam", the NT's word in Mat 13:30 and Luk 10:2 (workers of the harvest); reaping is "pea" (Jhn 4:35, Luk 10:2).
  • v1 גִּבּוֹר חַיִל: the BSB's "prominent man of noble character" is rendered with the NT's two social labels, "gigiap eve ezoqám" (a man of property, Luk 19:2) and "iz akhae ezoqám" (a man of name/standing, Luk 19:12, Act 8:27). "Relative" is "ekeza ezoqam" (36x), the NT's only kin word of this kind; "clan" is "bawan" as in chapter 1.
  • v2, 10, 13 "find favour in the eyes of": the NT says "kuku ... X-te" (favour/love is toward someone: Luk 1:30, 1Co 4:14). v13 uses the blessing form "Kuku até betaqagó, no-te" (may you keep having favour toward me), i.e. the wish is phrased from Boaz's side.
  • v3 "she happened to come": no word for chance in the NT; "matev āv qanefakhán nqǽgo:" (it turned out thus) carries the sense.
  • v4 greetings: "The LORD be with you" is 2Ti 4:22's closing blessing; "The LORD bless you" uses the NT's "blessed is he" verb (khakheinøv, Jhn 12:13 and parallels, Luk 1:42) with the 2sg object and the optative be-. The same verb stands in v19 and v20 for בָּרוּךְ.
  • v5 "foreman" is described: the servant who looked after the other workers (kewag, 1Ti 5:10).
  • v7 "sheaves" (עֳמָרִים): "bali møvøyam" (bundled/stored barley) from the NT's "tie in bundles" and "barn" (Mat 13:30); the noun is not attested. Same in v15 and v16 (צְבָתִים).
  • v7 "she rested a short time in the shelter": the Hebrew is obscure; the BSB's reading is followed with the NT's "rested" (pusa ndøtá, Heb 4:4) and "a short time" (khøuwa elavøqase).
  • v8 "Listen": "Nqáe!" is what the NT puts for "Listen!" (Mrk 4:3, 1Co 15:51) as well as "Look!".
  • v9 "not to touch you": the order is given as a direct quote, as the NT frames orders (Act 16:23), with the attested plural prohibitive "kha mbokhanø̀ze᷄m" (2Co 6:17).
  • v9 "go and drink from the jars": "go" is dropped; "you may drink from the jars" uses the NT's permission pattern "qo kopømbaqapé, toqo-X" (Luk 5:12).
  • v12 "under whose wings you have taken refuge": no word for refuge; made concrete as "you came to him and put yourself under his wings" (taf mokho-te, the hen's wings of Luk 13:34; "put into my hand" Luk 19:20). "Repay your work" and "full reward" both use yaq-fia (reward, repayment, 211x), with "Evezøza-te" (from the LORD, Col 3:24).
  • v13 "comforted and spoken to the heart": the NT's two idioms, "gave me strength of body" (kha føgakh etoam, 2Co 7:6) and "spoke words that push the back" (zita tøpøzu manqat, Act 11:23 "encouraged").
  • v14 vinegar = "waen khaqain-qape" (sour wine, Jhn 19:29); "morsel" = "lou mbewat" (Jhn 13:26). "Roasted grain": "vesu" is attested once ("zonga-kha vesu", broiled fish, Luk 24:42); low.
  • v14 "was satisfied": "ewez" with a personal object; in the NT "mbo-ewez" of a person twice means "grew weary/aggravated" (Jhn 4:6, Act 16:18) while "zø-ewez" means "were filled" (Luk 9:17, Rev 19:21). The form here (vømbō-ewez) follows the "filled" sense; a speaker should check.
  • v15 "insult/shame her" = "give her shame" (mivi etoam, 1Co 11:22), with the attested prohibitive "mbo-etøòme᷄m" (Eph 4:27). v16 "rebuke" = gugu (Luk 23:40, 9:55), prohibitive built.
  • v17 "beat out": the NT's grain-processing phrase "rubbed them, took the husks off" (Luk 6:1) stands for threshing by hand; "ephah" = "about one yakhav" (basket, measure: Jhn 6:13, Luk 16:6).
  • v20 "not withdrawn his kindness from the living and the dead": turned positive ("still shows mercy to the living and the dead"), with chapter 1's "tanakh matev" for חֶסֶד and the NT's "khandi ezoqam neka løvøte ezoqam" (2Ti 4:1). "Kinsman-redeemer" (גֹּאֵל) has no term; "one of our relatives who are there to redeem us" uses the NT's redeem verb (nqovotaz, Act 7:35, 1Co 1:30, Luk 1:68). Low; a Kuni legal/kin term may exist.
  • v22 "so that nothing will happen to you": near-verbatim Jhn 5:14 ("Soqaín ndøgo, ... tømøqa-fakhan").
  • v23 "clung to" (דבק) reuses chapter 1's built "gùmu-tøkenøvém" (v14 there).

Open questions

  1. Gleaning as "bali nanga vingisam" (picking barley heads) and harvesters as "sasae ezoqam": is there a Kuni word for gathering what the reapers leave, and for reapers?
  2. "Sheaves" = "bali møvøyam" (vv. 7, 15, 16): the noun is built from "tie in bundles" / "barn" (Mat 13:30); a reviewer should supply the real word for a bundle of cut grain.
  3. v1 "gigiap eve ezoqám neka iz akhae ezoqám" for גִּבּוֹר חַיִל: wealth and standing, or should one of them stand alone?
  4. v4 "Evezøza qo bèkhakheinøqavém" (the LORD bless you) and v19/20 "... ndego bèkhakheinøvém" (blessed be he): the optative on khakheinøv with a 2sg object is built; check the tones.
  5. v12 "refuge under his wings" made concrete as "put yourself under his wings"; v12 "repay" and "reward" both with yaq-fia: acceptable?
  6. v14 "vømbō-ewez" (was satisfied) may read as "grew weary" (Jhn 4:6); and "vesu" (roasted) rests on one verse.
  7. v17 "ephah" as "about one yakhav" (basket/measure): is yakhav a usable dry measure?
  8. v20 "kinsman-redeemer": "nigi ekeza ezoqa nøme, ndigu ni nqovotaz-qa ndi꞉gú" is a description; Kuni kin law may have a term (the one who takes over a dead brother's wife and land).
  9. v8/v21 "stay close to" as "namba mba qanøqóm" (stay/sit only with): is qom right for keeping company, or should "tøkenøv" (cling, v23) be used in the imperative?
  10. The store's reading of gu- as feminine (chapter 1, open question 10): the ꞉-forms gu꞉-/gunu꞉- are durative/locative with any subject (Peter "gu꞉qotam" Mat 26:69, Jesus "gunu꞉-apát" Mat 12:1, God "gunu꞉gú" 1Jn 4:12); only the plain gu- forms pattern with women. Recorded in the store.
