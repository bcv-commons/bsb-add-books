Read Genesis 45 with someone who knows Kuni (Kuni-Boazi, Papua New Guinea)

Open the chapter and switch to Verse by verse: each verse with the Hebrew, the BSB and the drafting agent's comments. The open questions from its notes:

  • "A father to Pharaoh" (v8): kept as a likeness (andé Felo-ge eve me nogó, I am like Pharaoh's father). Should it be explained ("his adviser")?
  • Wagons (v19, 21, 27): the chariot word manqei khagua wil-us; does Kuni have a word for a cart?
  • The built 2pl past with "me" (qazonø-wiím, qazonø-khofotáv, v4, 5, 8): is qazonø- right?
  • "His spirit revived" (v27): kha vøndæv ndøndáp (he was refreshed) or khanakhanakh ndøndáp (he became glad)?
  • Remnant and deliverance (v7) and destitution (v11): rendered by meaning, low certainty.
  • Least sure: v7, v8, v11, v19, v27.

Show it to others: the website

The public website shows every project's drafts, marked as drafts, with the language memory and the credits; the text of a non-free translation never appears there, only short quotes within the limits. To put this project there, the files go into git and the site is deployed:

python3 scripts/deploy_site.py
