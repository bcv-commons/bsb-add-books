# Rule bundles by familiarity

`scripts/make_prompt.py` adds the bundle for the project's `familiarity` (`scripts/modes.py`,
`docs/onboarding.md`) to every chapter prompt, before the project's own `rules`. A rule tagged `[store]`,
`[wiki]`, `[pack]` or `[lexicon]` is added only when the project has that tool. Placeholders: `{project}`, `{project_dir}`,
`{label}` (the language), `{lang}` (its code, the concord command), `{corpus}` (the style editions), `{similar_verses}` (the pack's corpus verses),
`{close_to}` (project `close_to`, for a variety of a standard language), `{school_language}` (project
`school_language`: the team's working language). Language-specific rules (divine
names, test drafts to avoid) stay in the project's `rules`.

## known

- [pack] **Verse pack, first:** `{project_dir}/_learn/packs/<code>NNN.md` (built by `scripts/verse_pack.py`) lists, for every verse, the project's decisions that touch it (terms, formulas, names) and the store's index. Read it before drafting and use the decisions as they stand; where your verse needs a different choice, keep it and say why in the notes.
- [store] **The project's memory:** `{project_dir}/profile/` holds the decisions made so far, each with the verses that show it (`python3 scripts/concord.py {project} store <regex>` prints entries). They are binding like the style guide: the same Hebrew or Greek term, formula or name gets the same rendering in every book, unless the context needs another.
- [lexicon] **Corpus lookups:** `python3 scripts/concord.py {project} eng "<regex>"` shows how {corpus} renders an English (BSB) expression, `lex <word>` its usual renderings. Use them for recurring terms and formulas, to match the corpus.
- [store] **Extend the memory at the end:** add to `profile/decisions.json` the decisions your chapter made that a later chapter needs (a rendering chosen for a recurring term, a formula, a name form; `method: project-decision`), each with evidence (verses of {corpus} or of this project's drafts, `"BOOK C:V"`), `by: agent:<code><NNN>` and today's date; a decision that changes an earlier one `supersedes` it. Then run `python3 scripts/language_profile.py check {project_dir}/profile --project {project}` and fix every problem.
- [store] **Glosses in Greek or Hebrew, never English in a value:** every `term`, `formula` and `name` you write gets a `gloss`: the source word it renders, as its verse writes it, with the lemma and the reference: Greek from an NT verse of its evidence, Hebrew (the WLC, as `python3 scripts/gloss.py words {project} "<BOOK C:V>"` prints it; first in a decision about our Tanakh drafts) from a verse of the Tanakh, e.g. `"gloss": [{"lang": "hbo", "form": "נֵלְכָ֖ה", "lemma": "הָלַךְ", "ref": "GEN 37:17"}, {"lang": "grc", "form": "Ἄγωμεν", "lemma": "ἄγω", "ref": "MRK 1:38"}]`. A form built by rule with no source word gets an `interlinear` line (`1PL.HORT-go`). The `value` holds only {label}; English goes to the `note`. A gloss marked `"proposed": true` (by `scripts/gloss.py propose`) that you come across: check it against its verse, drop `proposed` when it is right, correct it when it is not.
- [wiki] **The wiki** (`{project_dir}/wiki/`, in {school_language}: all text and analysis; only quotations in {label}; shared with the language team, who edit it too) explains the decisions: why a rendering, what was rejected, open questions. Add to the pages what your chapter decided (`python3 scripts/wiki.py sync {project}` first and after, then `lint`), one line in `wiki/log.md`. The block between `store:begin` and `store:end` is generated: change the store instead. A `> **Correction (…)**` by a person is binding: follow it and turn it into a store entry. Store entries marked `[team]` / `TEAM` (the pack's "Team decisions") are the language team's own: binding, and never superseded by you; where one seems wrong, follow it and write the question in your notes.

## unknown

- **No knowledge of {label} is assumed.** You don't know this language. Everything you write must be modelled on the corpus ({corpus}): a word, a suffix, a construction or a name form you use must be attested there (or follow a pattern you can show there). Never invent a {label} word.
- [pack] **Verse pack, first:** `{project_dir}/_learn/packs/<code>NNN.md` (built by `scripts/verse_pack.py`) is your main source. Its header gives the store's grammar features, an index of every decided term, formula and name, the name letters and the glossary entries of the chapter. For every verse it gives the store entries that match, the lexicon's renderings (count/share), the verse's phrases that occur in the corpus with their shortest corpus verses (BSB and {label} side by side: these show the constructions), {similar_verses}, and a line **Still needs a search** with the words nothing in the pack covers. Read the pack in full and the wiki's `index.md` before drafting, and open the wiki pages your verses need; do not read the store's JSON files for drafting (`concord.py {project} store <id>` prints any entry in full). Use the decided terms, formulas and name forms as they stand; an entry marked [open] is a question you may settle.
- **Searches:** search only for the words on a verse's *Still needs a search* line, a name, or a construction none of the pack's phrases shows; aim for at most two searches per verse and batch them. Tool: `python3 scripts/concord.py {project}` (read its docstring): `eng`, `{lang}`, `forms`, `gloss` and `like` search the corpus; `lex <word>` gives the alignment's renderings, `hlex <Hebrew lexeme, lemma or gloss>` the Hebrew side (Tanakh books in the corpus); `store <regex>` the store's entries.
- [store] **Every search ends in the store:** each word or construction you searched for gets an entry, so that no later chapter searches it again: found in the corpus → a `term` or `formula` with the corpus evidence; not found → a `gap` saying what you searched (the pattern) and what you used instead. Give such entries `words`: the BSB words they answer, lower case (`"words": ["sandal"]`); the verse pack matches entries by them and takes the words off later *Still needs a search* lines.
- [wiki] **The wiki** (`{project_dir}/wiki/`, Markdown, in {school_language}: all text and analysis; only quotations in {label}; shared with the language team, who edit it too) explains what the store records: how a construction works, the best corpus models, what was tried and rejected, open questions. Each page's prose is yours to improve; the block between `store:begin` and `store:end` is generated from the store and must not be edited (change the store instead). A `> **Correction (…)**` written by a person is binding: follow it, turn it into a store entry (with `supersedes` where it changes one) and mark it `(done: agent:<code><NNN>)`.
- [store] **Extend the store at the end:** append to `profile/observations.json` and `profile/decisions.json` what your chapter taught you (new terms, formulas, name forms, gaps; a feature you confirmed, refined or found wrong, with `supersedes`), every entry with corpus evidence, `by: agent:<code><NNN>`, today's date. Then run `python3 scripts/language_profile.py check {project_dir}/profile --project {project}` and fix every problem. Then run `python3 scripts/wiki.py sync {project}` and edit the wiki: add to the pages of what you learned (explanation and the corpus models, not a copy of the entries), place the inbox's new entries on a page (its `entries:`), start a page if none fits, run `python3 scripts/wiki.py sync {project}` and `lint {project}` again, and add one line to `wiki/log.md`.
- [store] **Glosses in Greek or Hebrew, never English in a value:** every `term`, `formula` and `name` you write gets a `gloss`: the source word it renders, as its verse writes it, with the lemma and the reference: Greek from an NT verse of its evidence, Hebrew (the WLC, as `python3 scripts/gloss.py words {project} "<BOOK C:V>"` prints it; first in a decision about our Tanakh drafts) from a verse of the Tanakh, e.g. `"gloss": [{"lang": "hbo", "form": "נֵלְכָ֖ה", "lemma": "הָלַךְ", "ref": "GEN 37:17"}, {"lang": "grc", "form": "Ἄγωμεν", "lemma": "ἄγω", "ref": "MRK 1:38"}]`. A form built by rule with no source word gets an `interlinear` line (`1PL.HORT-go`). The `value` holds only {label}; English goes to the `note`. A gloss marked `"proposed": true` (by `scripts/gloss.py propose`) that you come across: check it against its verse, drop `proposed` when it is right, correct it when it is not.
- **Names:** use the corpus form where the name is in the corpus or the glossary (`gloss`); otherwise transliterate by the letter rules on the wiki's names page and list the name under new name forms.
