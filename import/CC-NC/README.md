# import/CC-NC/: editions under a Creative Commons licence with NC and without ND

For editions under CC BY-NC or CC BY-NC-SA: adaptations are allowed, non-commercially, with credit (and under the same
licence where it is share-alike). An NC-ND edition goes to `CC-ND/` instead.

- **The texts are not in git**, only each edition's `edition.yaml`, README, build scripts, `.gitignore` and
  `fetch.lock.json` (as for `non-free/`; `import/README.md`).
- **Style use is as for a non-free edition:** books it has are drafted in quarantine, and quotations follow the limits
  in `docs/licensing.md`. Our independent drafts, the store, the wiki and statistics are ours and CC0.
- **Derived work** (a revision, a draft made with the edition's book in view, a file in its own structure) may go to a
  project under `export/CC-NC/`, published non-commercially under the edition's licence with its credit, or to one under
  `local_output/` (`docs/licensing.md`, *Three output paths*). Never to `export/` itself, which is CC0.
