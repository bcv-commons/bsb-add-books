Read Genesis 1 with someone who knows Jamaican Creole English

Open the chapter and switch to Verse by verse: each verse with the Hebrew, the BSB and the drafting agent's comments. The open questions from its notes:

  • Expanse: wan big ruuf (proposal) or simply skai? The decision affects 1:6–8, 14–20 and later Psalms.
  • אָדָם: piipl in Genesis 1, or man / uman biin (not in the corpus)? From Genesis 2 the word becomes the name Adam.
  • Be fruitful and multiply: Unu av nof nof pikni fits people; for fish and birds (1:22) the team may prefer a word for breeding.
  • "according to its kind": difrant difrant kaina (from 1CO 15:38) or a fixed phrase like kaina bai kaina (not attested)?
  • Divine names remain open (questions page): Genesis 1 has only אֱלֹהִים = Gad.

Corrections go into the chapter or the language memory; Claude Code makes them when asked:

Correct Genesis 1 in jam/jam_BSB: …

Draft the next chapters

One chapter after another, never several at once: each chapter's agent reads what the earlier chapters decided (names, word forms, spelling) and adds its own decisions to the memory, so the chapters stay consistent. Ask for the next chapter when one is done. In Claude Code:

Show it to others: the website

The public website shows every project's drafts, marked as drafts, with the language memory and the credits; the text of a non-free translation never appears there, only short quotes within the limits. To put this project there, the files go into git and the site is deployed:

python3 scripts/deploy_site.py
