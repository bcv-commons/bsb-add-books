Genesis, Chapter 1

Ou Gad Mek Evriting

1 Wen taim did staat, Gad mek di evn an di ort. 2 Nou di ort neva av no shiep, an it did emti. Daak did kova di diip waata, an Gad Spirit did a flai uova di waata.

Di Fos Die

3 Den Gad se, “Mek lait de1!” An lait kom. 4 Gad si se di lait gud, an im plit di lait fram di daak. 5 Gad kaal di lait “Die,” an di daak im kaal “Nait.” Iivnin kom, an maanin kom: dat a did di fos die2.

Di Sekan Die

6 Gad se, “Mek wan big ruuf3 de iina di migl a di waata, fi plit di waata fram di waata.” 7 So Gad mek di ruuf, an im plit di waata we de anda di ruuf fram di waata we de uova it. An a so it apm. 8 Gad kaal di ruuf “Skai.” Iivnin kom, an maanin kom: dat a did di sekan die.

Di Tord Die

9 Gad se, “Mek di waata we de anda di skai gyada tugeda iina wan plies, an mek di jrai lan kom out.” An a so it apm. 10 Gad kaal di jrai lan “Ort,” an di waata we gyada tugeda im kaal “Sii.” An Gad si se it gud. 11 Den Gad se, “Mek di ort bier griin tingz: plaant we bier siid, an evri fruut chrii we bier fruut wid siid iina it, difrant difrant kaina, pan di ort.” An a so it apm. 12 So di ort bier griin tingz: plaant we bier siid, difrant difrant kaina, an chrii we bier fruut wid siid iina it, difrant difrant kaina. An Gad si se it gud. 13 Iivnin kom, an maanin kom: dat a did di tord die.

Di Fuot Die

14 Gad se, “Mek lait dem de iina di skai ruuf, fi plit di die fram di nait. Dem ago bi sain fi shuo di siizn dem, di die dem an di ier dem. 15 Mek dem shain iina di skai ruuf fi gi lait pan di ort.” An a so it apm. 16 Gad mek tuu big lait: di biga lait fi ruul di die, an di likla lait fi ruul di nait. Im mek di staar dem tu. 17 Gad put dem iina di skai ruuf fi gi lait pan di ort, 18 fi ruul uova di die an di nait, an fi plit di lait fram di daak. An Gad si se it gud. 19 Iivnin kom, an maanin kom: dat a did di fuot die.

Di Fif Die

20 Gad se, “Mek di waata ful op wid nof nof sitn we liv, an mek bod flai uova di ort, kraas di skai ruuf.” 21 So Gad mek di big big animal dem iina di sii. Im mek aal kaina sitn we liv an muuv bout, we di waata ful op wid, difrant difrant kaina. An im mek evri bod we av wing, difrant difrant kaina. An Gad si se it gud. 22 Gad bles dem an se, “Unu av nof nof pikni, an ful op di waata iina di sii dem, an mek di bod dem ton nof nof pan di ort.” 23 Iivnin kom, an maanin kom: dat a did di fif die.

Di Siks Die

24 Gad se, “Mek aal kaina sitn we liv kom out a di ort: animal we piipl kip, animal we kraal pan di grong, an wail animal pan ort, difrant difrant kaina.” An a so it apm. 25 Gad mek di wail animal dem pan ort, difrant difrant kaina, di animal dem we piipl kip, difrant difrant kaina, an evriting we kraal pan di grong, difrant difrant kaina. An Gad si se it gud. 26 Den Gad se, “Mek wi mek piipl fi luk laik wi, fi bi laik wi. Mek dem ruul uova di fish iina di sii, di bod iina di skai, di animal dem we piipl kip, di uol ort4, an evri sitn we kraal pan di ort.”

//1 v275 So Gad mek piipl fi luk laik imself;

//2 im mek dem fi luk laik Gad;

//2 im mek dem man an uman.

28 Gad bles dem an se tu dem se, “Unu av nof nof pikni, an ful op di ort an tek kanchuol a it. Ruul uova di fish iina di sii, di bod iina di skai, an evri sitn we liv an muuv pan di ort.” 29 Den Gad se, “Luk ya, mi gi unu evri plaant we bier siid pan di uol ort, an evri chrii we av fruut wid siid iina it. Dem ago bi fi unu fuud. 30 An tu evri wail animal pan di ort, tu evri bod iina di skai, an tu evriting we kraal pan di ort — evriting we av di bret a laif iina dem — mi gi evri griin plaant fi nyam.” An a so it apm. 31 Gad luk pan evriting we im did mek, an si se it wel gud. Iivnin kom, an maanin kom: dat a did di siks die.

1 Wen taim did staat, Gad mek di evn an di ort.
בְּרֵאשִׁ֖ית בָּרָ֣א אֱלֹהִ֑ים אֵ֥ת הַשָּׁמַ֖יִם וְאֵ֥ת הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 1:1 In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.
formula:in_the_beginning (JHN 1:1 Wen taim did staat); בָּרָא = mek (term:make_create; the corpus has no separate 'create'); di evn an di ort (ACT 4:24, REV 14:7). High.
2 Nou di ort neva av no shiep, an it did emti. Daak did kova di diip waata, an Gad Spirit did a flai uova di waata.
וְהָאָ֗רֶץ הָיְתָ֥ה תֹ֨הוּ֙ וָבֹ֔הוּ וְחֹ֖שֶׁךְ עַל־פְּנֵ֣י תְה֑וֹם וְר֣וּחַ אֱלֹהִ֔ים מְרַחֶ֖פֶת עַל־פְּנֵ֥י הַמָּֽיִם׃
BSB 1:2 Now the earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the surface of the waters.
תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ described with negative concord (neva av no shiep) + emti (MAT 12:44); תְהוֹם = di diip waata; Gad Spirit (term:spirit_of_god); the Piel participle as past progressive did a flai uova (by rule; the image of a bird over its young). Medium.
3 Den Gad se, “Mek lait de!” An lait kom.
וַיֹּ֥אמֶר אֱלֹהִ֖ים יְהִ֣י א֑וֹר וַֽיְהִי־אֽוֹר׃
BSB 1:3 And God said, “Let there be light,” and there was light.
formula:let_there_be (2CO 4:6 Mek di lait shain); וַיְהִי־אוֹר as an event, An lait kom, not stative de (which would need did). High/medium.
4 Gad si se di lait gud, an im plit di lait fram di daak.
וַיַּ֧רְא אֱלֹהִ֛ים אֶת־הָא֖וֹר כִּי־ט֑וֹב וַיַּבְדֵּ֣ל אֱלֹהִ֔ים בֵּ֥ין הָא֖וֹר וּבֵ֥ין הַחֹֽשֶׁךְ׃
BSB 1:4 And God saw that the light was good, and He separated the light from the darkness.
formula:god_saw_good; separate = plit … fram (HEB 4:12). Medium.
5 Gad kaal di lait “Die,” an di daak im kaal “Nait.” Iivnin kom, an maanin kom: dat a did di fos die.
וַיִּקְרָ֨א אֱלֹהִ֤ים׀לָאוֹר֙ י֔וֹם וְלַחֹ֖שֶׁךְ קָ֣רָא לָ֑יְלָה וַֽיְהִי־עֶ֥רֶב וַֽיְהִי־בֹ֖קֶר י֥וֹם אֶחָֽד׃פ
BSB 1:5 God called the light “day,” and the darkness He called “night.” And there was evening, and there was morning — the first day.
Naming with kaal X “Y”; the fronted object of the Hebrew kept (an di daak im kaal “Nait”); refrain formula:evening_morning (Wen iivnin kom, MAT 8:16; a did past copula, LUK 3:34). Footnote: Hebrew 'day one'. Medium.
6 Gad se, “Mek wan big ruuf de iina di migl a di waata, fi plit di waata fram di waata.”
וַיֹּ֣אמֶר אֱלֹהִ֔ים יְהִ֥י רָקִ֖יעַ בְּת֣וֹךְ הַמָּ֑יִם וִיהִ֣י מַבְדִּ֔יל בֵּ֥ין מַ֖יִם לָמָֽיִם׃
BSB 1:6 And God said, “Let there be an expanse between the waters, to separate the waters from the waters.”
רָקִיעַ = wan big ruuf (term:expanse, a proposal: ruuf 7× in the corpus; footnote wan big kova); iina di migl a (REV 4:6 iina di migl an roun). Low/medium.
7 So Gad mek di ruuf, an im plit di waata we de anda di ruuf fram di waata we de uova it. An a so it apm.
וַיַּ֣עַשׂ אֱלֹהִים֮ אֶת־הָרָקִיעַ֒ וַיַּבְדֵּ֗ל בֵּ֤ין הַמַּ֨יִם֙ אֲשֶׁר֙ מִתַּ֣חַת לָרָקִ֔יעַ וּבֵ֣ין הַמַּ֔יִם אֲשֶׁ֖ר מֵעַ֣ל לָרָקִ֑יעַ וַֽיְהִי־כֵֽן׃
BSB 1:7 So God made the expanse and separated the waters beneath it from the waters above. And it was so.
we de anda / we de uova it as relative clauses; וַיְהִי כֵן = An a so it apm (formula:it_was_so). Medium.
8 Gad kaal di ruuf “Skai.” Iivnin kom, an maanin kom: dat a did di sekan die.
וַיִּקְרָ֧א אֱלֹהִ֛ים לָֽרָקִ֖יעַ שָׁמָ֑יִם וַֽיְהִי־עֶ֥רֶב וַֽיְהִי־בֹ֖קֶר י֥וֹם שֵׁנִֽי׃פ
BSB 1:8 God called the expanse “sky.” And there was evening, and there was morning — the second day.
Gad kaal di ruuf “Skai”: the expanse named with the store's skai (term:sky). Medium.
9 Gad se, “Mek di waata we de anda di skai gyada tugeda iina wan plies, an mek di jrai lan kom out.” An a so it apm.
וַיֹּ֣אמֶר אֱלֹהִ֗ים יִקָּו֨וּ הַמַּ֜יִם מִתַּ֤חַת הַשָּׁמַ֨יִם֙ אֶל־מָק֣וֹם אֶחָ֔ד וְתֵרָאֶ֖ה הַיַּבָּשָׁ֑ה וַֽיְהִי־כֵֽן׃
BSB 1:9 And God said, “Let the waters under the sky be gathered into one place, so that the dry land may appear.” And it was so.
Niphal jussive as Mek X gyada tugeda (1CO 5:4); jrai lan (HEB 11:29); 'appear' = kom out (JAS 4:14, of the sun). Medium.
10 Gad kaal di jrai lan “Ort,” an di waata we gyada tugeda im kaal “Sii.” An Gad si se it gud.
וַיִּקְרָ֨א אֱלֹהִ֤ים׀לַיַּבָּשָׁה֙ אֶ֔רֶץ וּלְמִקְוֵ֥ה הַמַּ֖יִם קָרָ֣א יַמִּ֑ים וַיַּ֥רְא אֱלֹהִ֖ים כִּי־טֽוֹב׃
BSB 1:10 God called the dry land “earth,” and the gathering of waters He called “seas.” And God saw that it was good.
Ort as the name of the dry land, Sii (the corpus has no plural form; Sii stands for the collective). Medium.
11 Den Gad se, “Mek di ort bier griin tingz: plaant we bier siid, an evri fruut chrii we bier fruut wid siid iina it, difrant difrant kaina, pan di ort.” An a so it apm.
וַיֹּ֣אמֶר אֱלֹהִ֗ים תַּֽדְשֵׁ֤א הָאָ֨רֶץ֙ דֶּ֔שֶׁא עֵ֚שֶׂב מַזְרִ֣יעַ זֶ֔רַע עֵ֣ץ פְּרִ֞י עֹ֤שֶׂה פְּרִי֙ לְמִינ֔וֹ אֲשֶׁ֥ר זַרְעוֹ־ב֖וֹ עַל־הָאָ֑רֶץ וַֽיְהִי־כֵֽן׃
BSB 1:11 Then God said, “Let the earth bring forth vegetation: seed-bearing plants and fruit trees, each bearing fruit with seed according to its kind.” And it was so.
Mek di ort bier griin tingz (bier: JHN 15:2; no model for 'bring forth', gap:bring_forth); plaant we bier siid, fruut chrii; לְמִינוֹ = difrant difrant kaina (1CO 15:38). Medium/low.
12 So di ort bier griin tingz: plaant we bier siid, difrant difrant kaina, an chrii we bier fruut wid siid iina it, difrant difrant kaina. An Gad si se it gud.
וַתּוֹצֵ֨א הָאָ֜רֶץ דֶּ֠שֶׁא עֵ֣שֶׂב מַזְרִ֤יעַ זֶ֨רַע֙ לְמִינֵ֔הוּ וְעֵ֧ץ עֹֽשֶׂה־פְּרִ֛יאֲשֶׁ֥ר זַרְעוֹ־ב֖וֹ לְמִינֵ֑הוּ וַיַּ֥רְא אֱלֹהִ֖ים כִּי־טֽוֹב׃
BSB 1:12 The earth produced vegetation: seed-bearing plants according to their kinds and trees bearing fruit with seed according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.
Fulfilment echoes v11 word for word. Medium.
13 Iivnin kom, an maanin kom: dat a did di tord die.
וַֽיְהִי־עֶ֥רֶב וַֽיְהִי־בֹ֖קֶר י֥וֹם שְׁלִישִֽׁי׃פ
BSB 1:13 And there was evening, and there was morning — the third day.
Refrain; tord (REV 4:7). High.
14 Gad se, “Mek lait dem de iina di skai ruuf, fi plit di die fram di nait. Dem ago bi sain fi shuo di siizn dem, di die dem an di ier dem.
וַיֹּ֣אמֶר אֱלֹהִ֗ים יְהִ֤י מְאֹרֹת֙ בִּרְקִ֣יעַ הַשָּׁמַ֔יִם לְהַבְדִּ֕יל בֵּ֥ין הַיּ֖וֹם וּבֵ֣ין הַלָּ֑יְלָה וְהָי֤וּ לְאֹתֹת֙ וּלְמ֣וֹעֲדִ֔ים וּלְיָמִ֖ים וְשָׁנִֽים׃
BSB 1:14 And God said, “Let there be lights in the expanse of the sky to distinguish between the day and the night, and let them be signs to mark the seasons and days and years.
מְאֹרֹת = lait dem; the weqatal of v14b as Dem ago bi sain (future, a new sentence; the style check asked for shorter sentences); מוֹעֲדִים = siizn (JHN 10:22). Medium.

וַיַּ֣עַשׂ אֱלֹהִ֔ים אֶת־שְׁנֵ֥י הַמְּאֹרֹ֖ת הַגְּדֹלִ֑ים אֶת־הַמָּא֤וֹר הַגָּדֹל֙ לְמֶמְשֶׁ֣לֶת הַיּ֔וֹם וְאֶת־הַמָּא֤וֹר הַקָּטֹן֙ לְמֶמְשֶׁ֣לֶת הַלַּ֔יְלָה וְאֵ֖ת הַכּוֹכָבִֽים׃

וַיֹּ֣אמֶר אֱלֹהִ֔ים יִשְׁרְצ֣וּ הַמַּ֔יִם שֶׁ֖רֶץ נֶ֣פֶשׁ חַיָּ֑ה וְעוֹף֙ יְעוֹפֵ֣ף עַל־הָאָ֔רֶץ עַל־פְּנֵ֖י רְקִ֥יעַ הַשָּׁמָֽיִם׃

Genesis 1 (gen001), Jamaican Creole English: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 1 (31 verses, Hebrew and BSB numbering agree) in Jamaican Creole English, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen001.txt, with the clause packages) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The spelling, word forms and constructions come from Di Jamiekan Nyuu Testiment (looked up with frequency counts), the verse pack, the wiki and the store. Status: draft, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen001.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. This is the project's first chapter: no earlier draft exists, so the wiki's rules (bare verb for the narrative past, did for states and backgrounds, Mek + subject + verb for jussives, se … se speech frames, phonemic spelling) and the corpus's creation passages (JHN 1:1–3, ACT 4:24, REV 14:7, JAS 3:7, 2PE 3:5) are the model. The chapter fixes the refrains every later creation and genealogy chapter will reuse (see the store entries by agent:gen001).

Exegetical observations

  • 1:1–2. 1:1 is read as a main clause (BSB), with Wen taim did staat from JHN 1:1, the NT's own echo of the verse. 1:2 is background (S-V order, qatal and nominal clauses): did marks it (did kova, did a flai), while the wayyiqtol main line from 1:3 on has bare verbs (se, si, plit, kaal, mek).
  • The expanse. רָקִיעַ is something beaten out and spread; wan big ruuf keeps the picture of a vault without the English loan firmament. 1:8 names it Skai, so 1:14–20 say di skai ruuf (the expanse of the sky).
  • The jussives (יְהִי, תַּדְשֵׁא, יִשְׁרְצוּ, תּוֹצֵא) are all Mek + subject + verb; the weqatal continuations (1:14b, 1:15) are Dem ago bi / Mek dem shain.
  • Day 6. The plural נַעֲשֶׂה "let us" is kept (Mek wi mek) without comment, as the BSB keeps it. אָדָם is collective and 1:26b already says "let them rule", so piipl and dem run through 1:26–28; 1:27's singular אֹתוֹ is folded into dem. man an uman is the corpus's pair for male and female.
  • 1:29–30. The gift of food is a speech; Luk ya for הִנֵּה (attested 22×), mi gi (bare verb for the perfect of a declared act). 1:30 has no verb in the Hebrew; mi gi is repeated from 1:29.

Dialect and style (corpus counts from Di Jamiekan Nyuu Testiment)

  • Spelling checked word by word; the style check found no form missing from the corpus. Key forms: ort (earth), evn (heavens), daak, waata, skai, sii, plaant (81×), siid, fruut, chrii, staar (36×), bod (27×), animal (92×), kraal (4×), wail (wild, in wail animal), jrai (34×), emti (3×), ruuf (7×), plit (24×), gyada (69 verses), siizn (3 verses), sain, nof nof (38 verses), kanchuol (62 verses), bret (2×), likla (3 verses), biga.
  • Ordinals: fos 190, sekan 34, siks 18, tord 14, fuot 4, fif 2.
  • Mek X de (let there be) by rule from Mek di lait shain (2CO 4:6); An a so it apm by rule from A so it ago go (REV 1:7) and So it apm (JAS 2:23).
  • Style check (scripts/style.py): the first draft had too many Den (9; expected about 2) and uova (12; about 2): Den is now kept only where the BSB has "Then" (1:3, 11, 26, 29), and uova stands once before each list in 1:26, 28. 1:14 and 1:21 were split into shorter sentences. Remaining flags are the refrains themselves (An a so it apm ×6, a did ×6, si se ×7), which are fixed formulas here, and the chapter is still a little shorter per BSB word than the human chapters (0.78× vs 0.82×): Genesis 1 is a terse list.
  • The verb bier (bear) is used for the earth too (Mek di ort bier griin tingz): the corpus has no "bring forth" (gap:bring_forth); animals kom out a di ort (1:24).

New name forms

None (no personal names in the chapter). Headings: Ou Gad Mek Evriting, Di Fos Die … Di Siks Die.

Open questions

  1. Expanse: wan big ruuf (proposal) or simply skai? The decision affects 1:6–8, 14–20 and later Psalms.
  2. אָדָם: piipl in Genesis 1, or man / uman biin (not in the corpus)? From Genesis 2 the word becomes the name Adam.
  3. Be fruitful and multiply: Unu av nof nof pikni fits people; for fish and birds (1:22) the team may prefer a word for breeding.
  4. "according to its kind": difrant difrant kaina (from 1CO 15:38) or a fixed phrase like kaina bai kaina (not attested)?
  5. Divine names remain open (questions page): Genesis 1 has only אֱלֹהִים = Gad.

25 Gad mek di wail animal dem pan ort, difrant difrant kaina, di animal dem we piipl kip, difrant difrant kaina, an evriting we kraal pan di grong, difrant difrant kaina.
An Gad si
se it gud.
26 Den Gad se,
“Mek wi mek piipl fi luk laik wi, fi bi laik wi.
Mek dem ruul uova di fish iina di sii, di bod iina di skai, di animal dem we piipl kip, di uol ort4, an evri sitn
we kraal pan di ort.” //1
27 So Gad mek piipl fi luk laik imself; //2
im mek dem fi luk laik Gad; //2

an se tu dem se,

“Luk ya,
mi gi unu evri plaant
we bier siid
pan di uol ort,
an evri chrii
we av fruut

30 An tu evri wail animal pan di ort, tu evri bod iina di skai, an tu evriting
we kraal pan di ort — evriting
we av di bret a laif iina dem —
mi gi evri griin plaant fi nyam.”
