Genesis test drafts against Den Frie Bibel

Two sets of our Danish drafts of chapters that Den Frie Bibel (DFB) has published, kept as tests and scored against DFB's own chapters (chrF, python3 scripts/score.py dan/dan_BSB <draft> --reference import/CC0/dan/den-frie-bibel/text/<code>NNN.txt; the floors' corpus leaves Genesis out). DFB is public domain; credited in CREDITS.md.

genesis-test/: Gen 1–19 and 28

Drafted before DFB published these chapters; kept in quarantine/ (out of the agents' reach) until 2026-10-15. Each chapter by one fresh agent, prose with notes, without the clause packages (the first way of working).

Independence was measured separately: 20% of 5+-word runs shared with DFB, the baseline for two independent translations (docs/workflow.md).

The spread between runs of the same set-up (about ±0.5) is as large as the differences between set-ups: tagging costs no quality, and the lean package matches the full one at fewer tokens (179–188k against 201–246k).

These are tests, not the project's drafts of these chapters: DFB's chapters are the text this project continues.
